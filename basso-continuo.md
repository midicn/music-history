::: zh
一条低音线，加上几个数字，就能让键盘手在瞬间补出和弦。通奏低音把巴洛克音乐的重心从声部堆叠移到低音与和声的骨架。

## 时间与地点

约1600至1750年，意大利与欧洲。它随单声歌曲和早期歌剧出现，在科雷利、珀塞尔、巴赫的室内乐与教堂音乐里成为标准配置。

## 发生了什么

通奏低音由两件乐器组成：一件持续低音的旋律乐器，如大提琴、低音管或低音维奥尔；一件能奏和弦的乐器，如羽管键琴、管风琴或琉特琴。谱上只写低音线和数字，数字告诉演奏者上方要补几度。演奏者不是照谱按键，而是根据低音走向即时决定和弦与声部连接。

## 改变了什么

和声成为独立的结构层，作曲家可以用一条低音规划整段音乐。伴奏者从读谱的人变成共同创作的人，同一首作品在不同键盘手手里会有不同的织体。通奏低音支撑了歌剧、康塔塔、协奏曲与奏鸣曲，直到十八世纪末才逐渐退出。数字低音的教学也催生了和声学。

## 听这个转折

先听科雷利的三重奏鸣曲，两条高音声部下面有一条独立的低音；再听珀塞尔的三重奏鸣曲，听数字低音如何让和声站稳；最后听巴赫的《三重奏鸣曲》，长笛与小提琴在上方交错，低音始终托底。

::: en
One bass line and a few figures let a keyboard player supply chords on the spot. Basso continuo moved the centre of Baroque music from stacked voices to a frame of bass and harmony.

## Time and place

Italy and Europe, roughly 1600–1750. It emerged with monody and early opera and became standard in the chamber and church music of Corelli, Purcell and Bach.

## What happened

A continuo group has two parts: a melodic instrument on the bass, such as cello, bassoon or bass viol, and a chordal instrument such as harpsichord, organ or lute. The score gives only the bass line and figures; the figures tell the player which intervals to add above. The player does not press keys as written but decides chords and voice-leading from the bass as it moves.

## What changed

Harmony became an independent structural layer, and a composer could plan a whole movement from one bass. Accompanists turned from readers into co-creators, so the same work could have a different texture under different keyboard players. Continuo supported opera, cantata, concerto and sonata until it faded late in the eighteenth century. Figured-bass teaching also gave rise to the study of harmony.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Corelli's trio sonata, where an independent bass runs under two upper voices; then hear Purcell's trio sonata and how the figures keep the harmony upright; finally Bach's trio sonata, with flute and violin crossing above a bass that never lets go.
