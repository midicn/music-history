## 读音乐史

本站按时期、地区、人物、体裁与具体作品组织内容。时期总览提供背景；人物与作品条目进一步说明写法、演出条件和传播过程。中国音乐与世界各地的传统也在本站收录，可从分类与相关条目进入。

## 选择读法

- 初次了解一个时期：先读时期总览，再选择一位人物或一部作品。
- 带着曲目来：从作品条目核对年代、版本和创作背景，再沿相关链接补充上下文。
- 比较不同传统：同时看音乐的使用场合、参与者与记谱方式，不把欧洲分期直接套用于所有地区。

页面中的MIDI示例用于辨认旋律、节奏与结构，不能替代历史乐器、唱腔或真实演出的音色。引用与参考资料列在条目末尾。

## Read music history

Entries are organised by period, region, person, genre and individual work. Period overviews give context; entries on people and works examine musical writing, performance conditions and circulation. Chinese music and traditions from other regions are included here through categories and related entries.

## Choose a route

- For a new period, start with its overview, then choose a person or work.
- For a specific piece, check its date, version and background, then follow related entries.
- When comparing traditions, consider purpose, participants and notation rather than applying European period labels everywhere.

MIDI examples help identify melody, rhythm and structure. They do not reproduce historical instruments, vocal practices or the timbre of an actual performance. References appear at the end of each entry.
