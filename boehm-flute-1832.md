::: zh
旧式长笛只有几个键，半音靠交叉指法硬凑，音色在每个调上都不一样。贝姆把音孔放到声学要求的位置，再用一套连杆让手指够得着，长笛的设计从此可以计算。

## 时间与地点

1832年与1847年，慕尼黑。贝姆本是金匠与长笛演奏者，1832年做出圆锥管、环键与按声学排列的音孔，1847年改成圆柱管与抛物线形吹口。1860年巴黎音乐学院采用贝姆长笛，此后它成为世界通行的形制。

## 发生了什么

贝姆的原则很简单，音孔要开在声学上正确的位置，手指够不到的地方用连杆与按键解决。旧式长笛为了手指方便，把音孔开在指距合适的地方，代价是音准与音色随调变化，半音还要靠交叉指法。新体系把音孔放大、位置重排，按键由一套联动的杆件控制，指法在各调之间趋于一致。1847年的圆柱管与抛物线吹口进一步改善了高音区与整体音准。同一套思路后来被克洛泽用到单簧管上，形成贝姆单簧管。

## 改变了什么

乐器制造开始以声学计算为依据，而不只靠师傅的经验。长笛的音量变大，音区更统一，可以在乐队里担任旋律而不被弦乐盖住。作曲家因此敢写更高的音、更快的走句与更大的跳进。标准化也让长笛可以跨国家互换，旧式木长笛退到民间与古乐演奏里。代价是音色变亮变统一，失去旧乐器那种每个调各有性格的差别。

## 听这个转折

先听匡茨的快板，一把键的长笛在远调上要靠交叉指法，音色会发闷。再听莫扎特的长笛协奏曲，写作年代早于贝姆，声音更柔，高音区不如后来明亮。最后听安德森的长笛练习曲，为贝姆长笛而写，音区上下均匀，大跳干净，半音阶像走平路。三首连起来，能听见一件乐器从手感优先变成声学优先。

::: en
An old flute had only a few keys; semitones were produced by cross-fingering, and the tone changed from key to key. Boehm put the holes where acoustics wanted them and added a linkage so the fingers could reach, and flute design became something that could be calculated.

## Time and place

Munich, 1832 and 1847. Boehm was a goldsmith and flautist. In 1832 he produced a conical flute with ring keys and tone holes placed by acoustic principle; in 1847 he moved to a cylindrical body with a parabolic headjoint. The Paris Conservatoire adopted the Boehm flute in 1860, and it became the instrument used around the world.

## What happened

Boehm's principle was simple. Holes should sit where acoustics requires, and where fingers cannot reach, rods and keys should carry the action. The old flute placed holes where fingers fell conveniently, at the cost of tuning and tone that shifted with the key, and it needed cross-fingerings for semitones. The new system enlarged the holes, rearranged their positions and linked them with keywork, so fingering became more uniform across keys. The cylindrical body and parabolic head of 1847 improved the top register and overall intonation. Hyacinthe Klose applied the same logic to the clarinet, producing the Boehm clarinet.

## What changed

Instrument making began to rest on acoustic calculation rather than workshop habit alone. The flute grew louder and more even, able to carry a melody in the orchestra without being buried by strings. Composers therefore wrote higher notes, faster runs and wider leaps. Standardisation also made flutes exchangeable between countries, while the old wooden flute retreated into folk music and historical performance. The price was a brighter, more uniform tone, and the loss of an older instrument whose character changed with every key.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Quantz's Allegro, where a one-key flute in a remote key must rely on cross-fingerings and the tone turns veiled. Then Mozart's Flute Concerto, written long before Boehm, softer in sound and less brilliant at the top. Finally Andersen's flute studies, composed for the Boehm instrument, even across the registers, clean in wide leaps, chromatic passages running like level ground. In sequence, they let you hear an instrument move from finger comfort to acoustic priority.
