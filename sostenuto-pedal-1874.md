::: zh
钢琴右边那只踏板把所有的音一起留住，右边的邻居一响，和声就浑了。中踏板换了一个办法，只留住你踩下时已经按住的音，后来的音保持干净。

## 年代与城市

1874年前后，纽约。斯坦威公司为一种选择性延音装置申请专利，通常记在阿尔伯特·斯坦威名下。十九世纪的其他厂商也提供类似装置，但多数欧洲钢琴的中踏板是弱音或低音延音，不是真正的持续音踏板。

## 当时发生了什么

中踏板的机械结构是一根横杆，踩下时升起，恰好挡住当时已经抬起的制音器，使这些音的制音器无法落回。之后再弹的音，制音器照常工作，声音干爽。演奏者因此可以让一个低音或一个和弦一直响，同时在上方弹断奏或快速音型。专利在十九世纪七十年代出现，但三踏板成为标准配置要晚得多，二十世纪的钢琴才普遍装齐。

## 后来变了什么

钢琴的织体多了一种分层方式，持续音、和声层与旋律层可以各走各的。作曲家开始写只有中踏板才能干净实现的段落，二十世纪的乐谱偶尔直接标出。它也让演奏者对踏板的控制更精细，延音不再是一踩全响的开关。另一方面，中踏板在各国的形制长期不统一，同一段音乐在不同钢琴上效果不同，巡演钢琴家因此要事先确认乐器。

## 听这一刻

先听德彪西的《塔》，低音持续，上方音型不断移动，延音踏板会让整片和声糊在一起，中踏板只留低音。再听拉威尔的《绞刑架》，一个钟声般的音贯穿全曲，其他声部在它周围走动，听那个音怎样不被和声吞掉。最后听李斯特的《叹息》，踏板把琶音连成一片，低音托住旋律。三首用的都是延音踏板，中踏板要解决的是同一个问题，只是更挑音。

::: en
The right pedal on a piano holds every note at once, and a neighbour chord turns the harmony to mud. The middle pedal took another route, holding only the notes already down when it was pressed, leaving later notes clean.

## Year and city

New York, around 1874. Steinway patented a selective sustaining device, usually credited to Albert Steinway. Other makers offered similar mechanisms, but on many European pianos the middle pedal is a moderator or a bass sustain rather than a true sostenuto.

## What happened then

The mechanism is a bar that rises when the pedal is pressed and catches the dampers already lifted, so they cannot fall back. Notes played afterwards keep their normal damping and sound dry. A player can therefore let a bass note or a chord ring while playing staccato or fast figures above it. The patent appeared in the 1870s, but the three-pedal layout became standard much later, and only in the twentieth century did pianos commonly carry all three.

## What changed after

The piano gained a way of layering its texture, with a sustained note, a harmonic layer and a melody each going their own way. Composers began writing passages that only the middle pedal can realise cleanly, and twentieth-century scores occasionally mark it. Control became more refined, since sustaining was no longer an all-or-nothing switch. On the other hand, the middle pedal was never uniform across countries, and the same music behaves differently on different instruments, so touring pianists learned to check the piano in advance.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with Debussy's Pagodes, where the bass holds while figures move above; the damper pedal would smear the harmony together, while a sostenuto would keep only the bass. Then Ravel's Le gibet, a bell-like note sounding through the whole piece while other voices move around it, and hear how that note escapes being swallowed. Finally Liszt's Un sospiro, the pedal joining arpeggios into one sheet of sound with the bass supporting the melody. All three use the damper pedal; the middle pedal solves the same problem, but note by note.
