## 1983年的公开演示

1983年1月，在加利福尼亚州阿纳海姆的冬季NAMM展会上，Sequential Circuits Prophet-600与Roland Jupiter-6通过MIDI连接，展示了不同厂商乐器之间的通信。戴夫·史密斯、梯郁太郎及多家厂商的工程团队参与了此前的协议协商；这不是两个人在展会当天临时发明的一根通用线。

公开演示也不等于所有产品立即兼容、规范立即完善。MIDI协会的历史记录把1983年后的协调与组织建设视为持续过程。

## 消息不等于声音

MIDI可描述音符开关、力度、音色选择与控制变化，也有时钟等消息。它传输的是演奏和控制数据，不直接传输录音波形。实际声音取决于接收端的音源、音色映射和设置；更换音源可以改变听感，但不能保证任意设备播放效果一致。

这让音序器和电脑能够编辑、复制、量化及重放演奏事件，并与音频录制并行工作。MIDI没有在1983年把磁带或音频录音整体取代。

## The public demonstration in 1983

At Winter NAMM in Anaheim, California, in January 1983, a Sequential Circuits Prophet-600 and a Roland Jupiter-6 communicated over MIDI. Dave Smith, Ikutaro Kakehashi and engineering teams from several manufacturers contributed to earlier discussions. The interface was not simply invented by two people at the show.

The demonstration did not make every product compatible overnight. The MIDI Association’s history describes continuing technical coordination and organization after 1983.

## Messages are not sound

MIDI describes note events, velocity, program selection and other controls, with messages for functions such as timing. It does not directly carry an audio waveform. Sound depends on the receiving instrument, patch mapping and settings; another sound source does not guarantee identical playback.

Sequencers and computers could edit, copy, quantize and replay events alongside audio recording. MIDI did not replace tape and recorded audio wholesale in 1983.
