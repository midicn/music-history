::: zh
死亡金属把鞭击金属的速度继续下压，用低吼、爆发鼓点与降调颤音 riff 构成，1980 年代中期在佛罗里达成形。

## 起源与年代
1983 年，Death 的前身 Mantas 在佛罗里达的阿尔塔蒙特斯普林斯组队，1987 年的《Scream Bloody Gore》确立模板。坦帕的 Morbid Angel、Obituary 与 Deicide 把场景集中到佛罗里达。1980 年代末，斯德哥尔摩的 Entombed 在 Sunlight 录音室做出另一种失真音色，哥德堡的 At the Gates 与 In Flames 后来加入旋律。英国的 Bolt Thrower 与荷兰的 Pestilence 让这一类型真正国际化。

## 听辨特征
人声是低吼或喷腔，歌词围绕死亡、暴力与神秘学。鼓以爆发鼓点与双踩为主，吉他用颤音拨弦弹低音 riff，降调后音色浑浊。段落常突然变速，从极快到半速重压，独奏不追求旋律，更多是噪声与半音滑行。录音偏干，低频多，混响少，鼓被推得很前。吉他在低音弦上快速交替，riff 常以半音或减五度移动；鼓的爆发段落里，军鼓、底鼓与镲几乎同时落下。人声与吉他同处低音区，听上去像另一件打击乐器。贝斯常跟着吉他的低音走，几乎不独立。

## 代表人物
Death 的 Chuck Schuldiner 把结构写得比同期更清楚，Morbid Angel 的 Trey Azagthoth 用不协和音程，Obituary 的 John Tardy 把人声推向含混的低吼。Entombed 的 Sunlight 音色成为“瑞典声”的代名词，At the Gates 与 In Flames 把旋律线带进这一类型。

## 谱系
上承鞭击金属、硬核与 1980 年代的磁带小样网络，下接旋律死亡、死亡核与 1990 年代的极端金属。它把地下交换网络变成国际场景，也把低音与鼓的比重推到前所未有的位置。1990 年代的唱片公司开始签下这些地下乐队，录音预算提高，音色也随之变干净。它的低吼与爆发鼓点后来成为极端金属的通用语。

## 常见误解
常见说法是死亡金属没有结构，其实它高度依赖 riff 的排列与变速。另一个误解是它只属于佛罗里达，瑞典、英国与荷兰各有自己的音色。把它等同于死亡核，也会混淆两代人的编制与制作方式。把它当成一种音色同样不准确，瑞典与佛罗里达的录音方式差别明显。

::: en
Death metal pushed thrash further down, building from growls, blast beats and down-tuned tremolo riffs, and formed in Florida in the mid-1980s.

## Origins and era
In 1983 Death's predecessor Mantas formed in Altamonte Springs, Florida, and Scream Bloody Gore in 1987 set the template. Morbid Angel, Obituary and Deicide in Tampa concentrated the scene in Florida. In the late 1980s Entombed in Stockholm produced a different distortion at Sunlight Studio, and At the Gates and In Flames in Gothenburg later added melody. Bolt Thrower in Britain and Pestilence in the Netherlands made the style genuinely international.

## What you hear
Vocals are growls or guttural rasps, and lyrics circle around death, violence and the occult. Drums rely on blast beats and double kick, guitar plays low riffs with tremolo picking, and down-tuning muddies the tone. Sections change speed abruptly, from full pace to half-time weight, and solos avoid melody in favour of noise and chromatic sliding. Recordings are dry, low-heavy and short on reverb, with drums pushed far forward. Guitar alternates rapidly on the low strings and riffs move by semitone or diminished fifth, while blast sections land snare, kick and cymbal almost together. The voice sits in the guitar's register and sounds like another percussion instrument. Bass mostly follows the guitar's low notes rather than striking out on its own.

## Key figures
Chuck Schuldiner of Death wrote clearer structures than his contemporaries, Trey Azagthoth of Morbid Angel used dissonant intervals, and John Tardy of Obituary pushed the voice into an indistinct growl. Entombed's Sunlight tone became shorthand for the Swedish sound, and At the Gates and In Flames brought melodic lines into the genre.

## Lineage
Thrash metal, hardcore and the 1980s tape-demo network feed in; melodic death, deathcore and 1990s extreme metal come out. It turned a tape-trading underground into an international scene and gave bass and drums a weight they had not had before. In the 1990s labels began signing these underground bands, budgets rose, and the sound grew cleaner. Its growls and blast beats later became a common language across extreme metal.

## Common misunderstandings
The common claim is that death metal has no structure, when it depends heavily on riff order and tempo change. Another is that it belongs only to Florida, though Sweden, Britain and the Netherlands each developed their own tone. Equating it with deathcore also blurs two generations of line-up and production. Treating it as one tone is equally wrong, since Swedish and Floridian recordings differ clearly.
