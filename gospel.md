::: zh
福音音乐把信仰唱成可参与的节奏，领唱抛出一句，会众用和声与拍手接住。

## 起源与年代
1930 年代，托马斯·多尔西把黑人灵歌、赞美诗与爵士和声编成福音歌曲，芝加哥成为中心。1940 到 1950 年代，马哈利娅·杰克逊与金门四重唱把它带进音乐厅，1960 年代又进入灵魂乐与民权运动。

## 听辨特征
先听领唱与和声的交替，短句一抛一接，句尾常加装饰音。Hammond 风琴铺长音，钢琴用切分和弦，拍手落在二四拍。速度中慢，副歌常转调上行，把情绪再推一层。歌词重复，方便会众跟上。

## 代表人物
托马斯·多尔西确立福音歌曲的写法，马哈利娅·杰克逊把独唱推到戏剧高度，金门四重唱定义四部和声。詹姆斯·克利夫兰把现代福音与合唱团结合，安德烈·克劳奇把福音带进当代流行。

## 谱系
上承黑人灵歌、赞美诗与五旬节派教会音乐，下接灵魂乐、R&B 与当代福音流行；它的唱腔也进入摇滚与爵士。

## 常见误解
福音不只是教堂音乐，它有完整的演唱技术与和声语言；也不等于慢，快板福音与跳跃节奏同样常见。把它只当宗教背景，会漏掉它对流行唱法的塑造。

::: en
Gospel turns belief into a rhythm everyone can join, as a lead throws a phrase and the congregation answers with harmony and claps.

## Origins and era
In the 1930s Thomas Dorsey fused black spirituals, hymns and jazz harmony into gospel songs, centred on Chicago. In the 1940s and 1950s Mahalia Jackson and the Golden Gate Quartet took gospel to concert halls; in the 1960s it entered soul and the civil rights movement.

## What you hear
Start with lead and choir, short phrases thrown and caught, endings decorated with melisma. Hammond holds long notes, piano plays syncopated chords, and claps fall on two and four. Tempos are medium or slow, and the chorus often modulates upward for another lift. Words repeat so the congregation can join.

## Key figures
Dorsey established the gospel song, Mahalia Jackson raised the solo to drama, and the Golden Gate Quartet defined four-part harmony. James Cleveland joined modern gospel to the choir, and Andraé Crouch brought gospel into contemporary pop.

## Lineage
Black spirituals, hymns and Pentecostal music feed in; soul, R&B and contemporary gospel pop come out. Its singing enters rock and jazz.

## Common misunderstandings
Gospel is not just church music; it has a full vocal and harmonic language. Nor is it only slow; uptempo gospel and shout rhythms are common. Treating it as background misses how it shaped pop singing.
