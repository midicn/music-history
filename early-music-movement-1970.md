::: zh
当演奏者开始为弓法、音高和乐器尺寸找证据，音乐会的标准就不再只是好听。

## 时间与地点

1953到1970年代，维也纳、阿姆斯特丹与伦敦。哈农库特1953年创办维也纳古乐合奏团，莱昂哈特在阿姆斯特丹教大键琴，芒罗1967年在伦敦组建早期音乐合奏团。布鲁日等地的古乐节在1960年代成形。

## 发生了什么

这一代演奏者按史料复制乐器，研究弓法、指法、音高与装饰，重新编辑乐谱，也重新学习如何用没有阀门的铜管、肠弦与竖笛演奏。他们不再把巴洛克作品当成用现代乐队演奏的旧曲子，而是当成需要重建发声条件的音乐。1970年代，古乐从学术圈进入唱片与音乐节，竖笛被搬进小学课堂，大键琴与维奥尔琴回到目录。

## 改变了什么

作品不再是唯一对象，声音本身成为可以争论的问题。演奏者开始说明为什么这样分句、这样定音高，唱片内页变成方法论。音乐厅的曲目随之改变，文艺复兴与巴洛克作品从开场点缀变成整场节目。乐器制造业也随之复苏起来，复制乐器的作坊成为一个小产业。争议同样重要，本真演奏是否可能，乐谱与录音谁更权威。此后几乎所有古典演奏都被迫对自己的选择作出说明。

## 听这个转折

先听《第五勃兰登堡协奏曲》第一乐章，大键琴的华彩长得像一首独立的曲子，古乐运动坚持用拨弦的古乐器而不是现代钢琴。再听泰勒曼的竖笛与长笛协奏曲，两种笛子的音色差别被刻意保留。最后听蒙特威尔第的牧歌，声部之间不加伴奏，装饰由歌者按当时的规则即兴。三首都能听出，用什么样的声音演奏，本身成了作品的一部分。

Once players began looking for evidence about bowing, pitch and instrument size, the concert standard stopped being simply what sounded good.

## Time and place

Vienna, Amsterdam and London, 1953 to the 1970s. Harnoncourt founded the Concentus Musicus Wien in 1953, Leonhardt taught harpsichord in Amsterdam, and David Munrow formed the Early Music Consort in London in 1967. Early music festivals such as Bruges took shape during the 1960s.

## What happened

This generation copied instruments from evidence, studied bowing, fingering, pitch and ornament, re-edited scores, and relearned how to play valveless brass, gut strings and recorders. They treated Baroque works not as old pieces to be played by a modern orchestra but as music whose conditions of sound had to be rebuilt. In the 1970s early music moved from seminar rooms into recordings and festivals; the recorder entered primary schools, and harpsichords and viols returned to the catalogues.

## What changed

The work ceased to be the only object; sound itself became arguable. Players began explaining why they phrased a line or chose a pitch, and liner notes turned into method. Concert programmes changed, with Renaissance and Baroque music moving from opening decoration to whole evenings. Instrument making revived too, and workshops copying old models became a small industry. The argument mattered as much, whether authentic performance is possible and whether the score or the recording carries authority. Since then every classical performer has had to justify choices.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the first movement of the Fifth Brandenburg Concerto, where the harpsichord cadenza grows into a piece of its own and the movement insists on a plucked historical instrument rather than a piano. Then Telemann's concerto for recorder and flute, where the difference between the two pipes is deliberately preserved. Finally a Monteverdi madrigal, unaccompanied, with ornament left to the singers under the rules of the time. All three show that what kind of sound plays the notes has become part of the work.
