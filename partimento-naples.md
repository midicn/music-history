::: zh
一条只有低音与数字的线，学生要在琴上当场补出完整的织体。那不勒斯的音乐学院用这种练习教作曲，和声从此变成可以上手操作的东西。

## 年代与城市

十七世纪末到十八世纪，那不勒斯。四所音乐学院原本是收容孤儿的慈善机构，后来成了职业音乐家的训练场。亚历山德罗·斯卡拉蒂、杜兰特与莱奥等人在这里任教，具体课程安排与年份仍有争议，通常只按世纪叙述。

## 当时发生了什么

教师发给学生一条低音线，有时带数字，有时什么都不标，学生要在键盘上实时补全上方声部，做出正确的和弦、经过音与终止式。这套材料叫 partimento。练习从最简单的八度规则开始，把音阶的每个音配上固定和弦，再进入终止式、模进、延留音与赋格段。学生在琴上把和声走通之后，才动笔写总谱。也就是说，作曲训练的顺序被倒过来，先听见，再写下。

## 后来变了什么

和声从理论条文变成手指上的图式，学生在弹奏中记住成百上千的和声走向，写作时直接调用。职业训练因此高度标准化，一个那不勒斯毕业生在欧洲多数宫廷都能立刻上手。十九世纪巴黎音乐学院建立起以和声学与对位法课堂为核心的体系，这套键盘教学逐渐退出正式课程，只在私人教学里留存。二十世纪末，学者重新整理这些手稿，partimento 又回到即兴与历史演奏的课堂。

## 听这一刻

先听斯卡拉蒂 K.208 奏鸣曲，低音线走得慢，能听见和声怎样一步一步跟着低音换。再听 K.27，模进与终止式密集，几乎每一步都能对上教科书里的图式。最后听佩尔戈莱西的大键琴奏鸣曲，段落短，开头与结尾都是标准终止式，像学生做完的练习。听的时候试着只跟低音，想象上方还能加什么，这就是 partimento 的听法。

::: en
A line with only a bass and some figures, and the student must supply a full texture at the keyboard. The conservatories of Naples taught composition this way, and harmony became something the hands could operate.

## Year and city

Naples, from the late seventeenth century through the eighteenth. The four conservatories began as charitable homes for orphans and became training grounds for professional musicians. Alessandro Scarlatti, Francesco Durante and Leonardo Leo taught there; the exact curriculum and dates are still debated, so the story is usually told by century.

## What happened then

A teacher handed out a bass line, sometimes figured, sometimes bare, and the student realised the upper voices at the keyboard in real time, producing correct chords, passing notes and cadences. The material was called a partimento. Exercises began with the rule of the octave, which assigns a standard chord to each step of the scale, then moved to cadences, sequences, suspensions and fugal passages. Only after the harmony worked under the hands did the student write a score. The order of training was reversed: hear first, write later.

## What changed after

Harmony shifted from a set of rules into patterns held in the fingers. Students memorised hundreds of harmonic moves while playing and called on them while composing. Training became highly standardised, so a Neapolitan graduate could take up a post at most European courts and start at once. In the nineteenth century the Paris Conservatoire built its system around harmony and counterpoint classes, and the keyboard method faded from official curricula, surviving in private teaching. In the late twentieth century scholars catalogued the manuscripts, and partimento returned to classes in improvisation and historical performance.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with Scarlatti's Sonata K.208, where the bass moves slowly and you can hear harmony changing step by step with it. Then K.27, dense with sequences and cadences, almost every move matching a textbook pattern. Finally a harpsichord sonata by Pergolesi, short sections framed by standard cadences, like an exercise a student has finished. Listen by following only the bass and imagining what could be added above it. That is how partimento was heard.
