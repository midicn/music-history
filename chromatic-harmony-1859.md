::: zh
1850年代末，半音不再只是旋律上的装饰音，它开始决定一首曲子往哪里走，以及还能不能停下来。

## 时间与地点

1857到1860年，魏玛、苏黎世与维也纳。瓦格纳在苏黎世写《特里斯坦与伊索尔德》与《韦森东克歌曲》，李斯特在魏玛写《浮士德交响曲》，并把手上的瓦格纳段落改成钢琴曲。

## 发生了什么

瓦格纳把半音写成句法，而不只是色彩。《韦森东克歌曲》先试验了同一套语汇，其中两首后来成为《特里斯坦》的草稿。李斯特在同一时期把增和弦、半音经过与不解决的属和弦推向标题音乐。所谓特里斯坦和弦并不是新发明的和弦，而是一个不按常规解决的和弦，它让听者等待，等待本身成了结构。这个时期的作品常常长时间不出现明确的终止式，调性靠低音与重复维持。

## 改变了什么

和声的重心从和弦属于哪个调，转向和弦往哪里去。作曲家可以在一个调上停留很久而不确认它，听众的期待被延长、被欺骗、被反复利用。演奏者的难点也变了，不再是音准或速度，而是如何在长达数分钟的不解决里保持方向。这套语汇后来被勋伯格推到极限，调性才真正崩塌。把这一时期称作半音化的危机，是因为它同时改变了写作、演奏与聆听三件事。

## 听这个转折

先听《韦森东克歌曲》中的《在温室里》，钢琴前奏不断离调，人声进来时仍没有落点。再听《梅菲斯托圆舞曲》的开头，增和弦与半音阶把舞曲的拍子搅乱，舞步还在，地面已经不稳。最后听李斯特改编的《罗恩格林》，同一段和声换到钢琴上，紧张感没有减少。三首都在1857到1860年之间，可以听出半音化怎样从局部手法变成整首曲子的空气。

::: en
Around 1860 chromaticism stopped being ornament and began to decide where a piece went, and whether it could stop at all.

## Time and place

1857 to 1860, in Weimar, Zurich and Vienna. Wagner was writing Tristan und Isolde and the Wesendonck Lieder in Zurich; Liszt was writing the Faust Symphony in Weimar and turning Wagner's scenes into piano pieces.

## What happened

Wagner made chromaticism grammatical rather than decorative. The Wesendonck Lieder tested the vocabulary first, and two of them became studies for Tristan. Liszt pushed augmented chords, chromatic passing motion and unresolved dominants into programme music at the same time. The so-called Tristan chord was not a new invention but a chord that refused its expected resolution; it makes the listener wait, and the waiting becomes the structure. Works of this period often go minutes without a clear cadence, holding a key by bass and repetition alone.

## What changed

Harmony shifted from asking which key a chord belongs to toward asking where it is going. A composer could stay in a key for a long time without confirming it, and expectation was stretched, cheated and reused. The performer's difficulty changed as well, no longer intonation or speed but keeping direction through minutes of non-resolution. Schoenberg later took the vocabulary to its limit and tonality genuinely collapsed. Calling this period a chromatic crisis is fair because it changed writing, playing and listening at once.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Im Treibhaus from the Wesendonck Lieder, where the piano prelude keeps leaving the key and the voice enters without landing. Then the opening of the Mephisto Waltz, where augmented chords and chromatic scales disturb the dance; the steps remain but the floor moves. Finally Liszt's transcription of Lohengrin, where the same harmony on a piano loses none of its tension. All three date from 1857 to 1860 and show chromaticism becoming the air of a whole piece.
