## 他是谁

**威廉·亨利·蒙克**（1823–1889）是英国的管风琴师、合唱指挥与编订者。他生于伦敦，长期在伦敦的教堂任管风琴师，同时在国王学院任管风琴教授。1861 年他出任《古今圣诗集》的音乐主编，这本集子后来卖出数以百万册，成为英国国教会最通用的赞美诗集。他为《Abide with Me》写的曲调 Eventide 收在其中，是他最广为流传的作品。

## 时代与位置

他处在一个出版与礼拜同时扩张的年代。十九世纪中期，英国教堂的音乐水平参差，赞美诗集各用各的曲调；《古今圣诗集》的做法是把歌词与曲调固定配对，统一和声版本，让全国各地的教堂唱同一套音乐。蒙克的工作是把大量来源不一的曲调整理、移调、配和声，使它们能在会众的音域里唱得下去。

## 听什么

先听**四句的结构**。Eventide 是典型的四句式曲调，第三句往往把音区推高，最后一句落回主音，听它怎样把整首的情绪推上去再放下来。再听**和声的支撑**。会众唱的是最高声部，下面三个声部由管风琴弹，听低音在每句结尾如何走一个短小的终止。最后听**语言的落位**。同一首曲调配不同语言的歌词，重音位置会变，听歌手怎样调整。

## 代表作

- 曲调 Eventide，《Abide with Me》通用配对
- 《古今圣诗集》（1861）音乐主编
- 曲调《St Denys》《St Ethelwald》等
- 管风琴曲与教会合唱音乐

## 影响

《古今圣诗集》把「一书一曲调」的做法固定下来，此后英语世界的赞美诗集大多照此编排。Eventide 成为最常被唱的曲调之一，在婚礼、葬礼与纪念仪式上反复出现。蒙克的名字不像作曲家那样显眼，但英语世界教堂里每星期唱出的音乐，有相当一部分经过他的手。

## Who he was

**William Henry Monk** (1823–1889) was an English organist, choirmaster and editor. He was born in London, worked for years as a church organist there and taught the organ at King's College. In 1861 he became musical editor of Hymns Ancient and Modern, a book that went on to sell in millions and became the standard hymnal of the Church of England. His tune Eventide, written for Abide with Me, was printed in it and is his most widely sung piece.

## His place

He worked in a period when publishing and parish worship were both expanding. In the mid-nineteenth century English church music was uneven and every hymnal used its own tunes; Hymns Ancient and Modern fixed words to tunes, standardised the harmony, and let churches across the country sing the same music. Monk's task was to sort, transpose and harmonise a mass of tunes from different sources so that congregations could actually sing them.

## What to hear

Listen first for **the shape of four lines**. Eventide is a classic four-line tune: the first two lines rise, the last two fall back, and the melodic peak arrives in the third line, so hear how the whole feeling is pushed up and then set down. Then listen for **harmonic support**. The congregation sings the top part while the organ plays the other three, so follow how the bass makes a short cadence at the end of each line. Finally listen for **where the language falls**. The same tune set to words in another language shifts the accents, so hear how the singers adjust.

## Key works

- The tune Eventide, standard pairing for Abide with Me
- Hymns Ancient and Modern (1861), as musical editor
- Tunes including St Denys and St Ethelwald
- Organ music and church choral music

## Influence

Hymns Ancient and Modern fixed the practice of one tune per text, and most English-language hymnals afterwards were laid out the same way. Eventide became one of the most frequently sung tunes in the language, returning at weddings, funerals and memorial services. Monk's name is less visible than a composer's, but a good share of what English churches sang every week passed through his hands.
