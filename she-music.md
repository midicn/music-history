## 年代与地方

畲族分布在福建宁德、福州，浙江丽水、温州，江西上饶、赣州，广东潮州、梅州与安徽宁国。闽东、浙南的山区是主要聚居区。畲语属苗瑶语族，接近客家话的地区多兼用汉语，没有通行的传统文字。三月三是最重要的歌会，婚礼、采茶与秋收也唱。畲歌在村里由长辈教，孩子从旁听会。

## 它是怎么来的

畲歌又称歌言，是畲族口传的主体。高皇歌唱盘瓠与高辛帝的三公主，记畲族的起源与迁徙，篇幅长，在祭祖、婚礼与歌会中唱。嫁女歌唱新娘离家、父母养育与姐妹情分，带哭腔。采茶歌在茶山唱，句幅短，节奏随采茶动作。二十世纪后期，畲歌被整理成文字，歌会进入旅游与节庆舞台，村里的对唱仍在进行。高皇歌的完整唱本各地不同，公开记录以闽东、浙南为主。

## 声音的骨架

畲歌多为单声部清唱，假声与真声交替，起句高，句尾上扬，常用啰、哩一类衬词。歌词多为七言双句，押韵紧，唱时按语音拉长。旋律以五声为主，音域不宽，同一条调子可套不同的词。婚礼歌用哭腔，句尾下滑带颤；采茶歌节奏轻快，句幅短。乐器在畲族音乐中不占主导，笛子、二胡多用于伴奏与舞台。

## 听什么

先听假声起句怎样站住，再听句尾的上扬与衬词。chinafolk-001265 标题写明畲歌，能听到假声与句尾；chinafolk-001564 是高皇歌，听长篇叙事的句读；chinafolk-001569 是歌会上的对唱，听称呼与应答；chinafolk-001575 是嫁女歌，听哭腔与祝愿。四首都是学习区录音，不是舞台改编。

## 常被误会的地方

常见误解是把畲歌当成客家山歌的一支。畲语与客家话有接触，但畲歌的假声、句尾与衬词有自己的规律。高皇歌不是普通民歌，它是族群起源的唱本。也不是所有畲族地区都唱同样的调，闽东、浙南、赣南各有差别。畲歌仍在三月三与婚礼中唱，不是只存在于录音里。

## When and where

The She live in Ningde and Fuzhou in Fujian; Lishui and Wenzhou in Zhejiang; Shangrao and Ganzhou in Jiangxi; Chaozhou and Meizhou in Guangdong and Ningguo in Anhui. The mountains of eastern Fujian and southern Zhejiang form the main area. She belongs to the Hmong-Mien family; in areas close to Hakka speech many people are bilingual, and there is no widely used traditional script. The third-day-of-the-third-month gathering is the main song event, and weddings, tea picking and harvest also bring songs. Elders teach in the village.

## How it came about

She songs, also called song words, are the core of oral tradition. The Gaohuang epic tells of Pan Hu and the third princess of Emperor Gaoxin and records She origins and migration; it is long and sung at ancestor rites, weddings and song gatherings. Wedding songs tell of the bride leaving home, parental care and sisterly feeling, with a weeping tone. Tea-picking songs are short-phrased and follow the work. From the late twentieth century the songs were edited into text, and song gatherings entered tourism and festival stages, while village antiphony continues. Complete Gaohuang texts differ by place, and published records centre on eastern Fujian and southern Zhejiang.

## The sound's frame

She songs are mostly unaccompanied solo, alternating falsetto and chest voice, with a high opening, rising phrase ends and refrain syllables such as luo and li. Verses are usually seven-syllable couplets with tight rhyme, stretched by speech rhythm. Melody is mostly pentatonic and narrow in range; one tune can carry many texts. Wedding songs use a weeping tone with falling, trembling ends; tea-picking songs are lighter and shorter. Instruments are secondary, with flute and erhu used for accompaniment and stage work.

## What to listen for

Hear how the falsetto opening holds, then the rising end and refrain. Chinafolk-001265 is explicitly a She song with falsetto and phrase endings; chinafolk-001564 is the Gaohuang epic with long narrative phrasing; chinafolk-001569 is a gathering song with address and answer; chinafolk-001575 is a wedding song with weeping tone and blessings. All four are study-zone recordings, not stage arrangements.

## What is often mistaken

She songs are often classified as a branch of Hakka mountain song. She and Hakka have been in contact, but the falsetto, phrase endings and refrains follow their own rules. The Gaohuang epic is not an ordinary folk song; it is a charter of origins. Nor does every She area sing the same tunes; eastern Fujian, southern Zhejiang and southern Jiangxi differ. She songs are still sung at the third-month gathering and at weddings.
