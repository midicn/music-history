## 时间坐标

十一世纪，阿雷佐；十三至十六世纪，巴黎、佛罗伦萨与威尼斯。圭多推广四线谱，巴黎的圣母院乐派发展有量记谱，威尼斯的印刷商把谱表变成可以贩卖的商品。

## 事情经过

圭多用四线、线间与唱名把音高固定下来，歌手不必每次重新猜旋律。十三世纪的有量记谱给音值一套符号，复调各声部可以对齐。十三至十五世纪，五线谱逐渐出现，音域比四线更宽；十六世纪印刷术统一线距、谱号与版式，五线谱逐渐压过地方记谱与 tablature。到十七世纪，高音与低音谱号的五线谱成为通用写法。

## 改写了什么

音高与节奏第一次能被精确复制，复调可以并排阅读，器乐音乐不再只靠记忆。印刷让乐谱成为商品，作曲家能跨地区传播作品，演奏者也能买到同一份谱。五线谱因此不只是记谱工具，它成了西方音乐共同的文字。

## 听一段转折

先听《Ut queant laxis》的管风琴 verset，圭多的唱名正取自这首赞美诗，旋律在谱表里有了清楚的落点。再听 Morales 的《Puer natus est》，圣咏固定旋律被放进谱表，其他声部围着它走。最后听 Palestrina 的经文歌，各声部在印刷谱表上对齐，听觉上像一束被整理过的光。听不出线数，但能听出多个声部如何被同一张谱协调。

## On the timeline

Eleventh-century Arezzo; Paris, Florence and Venice from the thirteenth through sixteenth centuries. Guido promoted the four-line staff, the Notre Dame school developed measured notation, and Venetian printers made the staff a commodity.

## The sequence of events

Guido used four lines, spaces and solmisation to fix pitch, so singers no longer guessed melodies. Thirteenth-century measured notation gave note values their own signs and let polyphonic voices align. A five-line staff emerged in the later Middle Ages, with a wider range; sixteenth-century printing standardised spacing, clefs and layout, and the five-line staff gradually displaced local notations and tablature. By the seventeenth century, a five-line staff with treble and bass clefs was the normal notation.

## What it rewrote

Pitch and rhythm could now be copied exactly, polyphony could be read side by side, and instrumental music no longer depended on memory alone. Printing made scores into goods, composers could circulate works across regions, and players could buy the same copy. The staff became more than a tool, the common script of Western music.

## Hear the turn

Begin with the organ verset on Ut queant laxis, the hymn from which Guido took his solmisation syllables; the melody now has clear resting points on the staff. Then hear Morales's Puer natus est, where a chant cantus firmus sits inside the staff and the other voices gather around it. Finally Palestrina's motet, its voices aligned on a printed page, sounds like light brought into order. You cannot hear the lines, only voices coordinated by one sheet.
