## 时间与地点

1791年9月30日，维也纳维登自由屋剧院。席卡内德经营这家郊区剧场，莫扎特为他写出这部德语歌唱剧，并亲自指挥最初几场。

## 发生了什么

剧本由席卡内德编写，讲王子塔米诺、捕鸟人帕帕基诺与夜后的女儿帕米娜。对白与唱段交替，舞台上有机械的蛇、飞行的场景与祭司的仪式。夜后的两首咏叹调用极高的花腔，萨拉斯特罗的咏叹调则靠低沉的男低音压住场面。共济会的入会仪式、试炼与启蒙语汇贯穿全剧。首轮演出超过一百场，成为维也纳的卖座戏。

## 改变了什么

德语歌剧不再只是小规模喜剧，也能用通俗故事、舞台机关与严肃寓意吸引普通观众。歌唱剧从市集娱乐变成可以承载启蒙思想的体裁，德国的民族歌剧传统由此有了一个高点。莫扎特在同年十二月去世，这部作品成了他最后一部完整歌剧。

## 听这个转折

先听一段《魔笛》选段，听对白如何把观众带进故事、唱段如何接住情绪；再听夜后的《Der Hölle Rache》，花腔高音像武器一样向上刺；最后听钢琴改编，旋律离开乐队与舞台后仍然站得住。通俗与深刻可以在同一晚发生。

## Time and place

30 September 1791, at the Freihaus-Theater auf der Wieden in Vienna. Emanuel Schikaneder ran this suburban theatre, and Mozart wrote the German Singspiel for his company and conducted the first performances.

## What happened

Schikaneder wrote the libretto, which follows Prince Tamino, the bird catcher Papageno and Pamina, daughter of the Queen of the Night. Spoken dialogue alternates with musical numbers, and the stage carried a mechanical serpent, flying scenes and ritual. The Queen's two arias demand coloratura, while Sarastro's arias are anchored by deep bass. Masonic initiation, trials and Enlightenment language run through the whole work. The first run passed a hundred performances and became a hit.

## What changed

German opera could now be more than a comedy. A Singspiel built from popular story, stage machinery and serious allegory could draw an ordinary audience. The genre moved from fairground entertainment to a vehicle for Enlightenment ideas, and German opera gained an early high point. Mozart died that December, and this became his last opera.

## Listen to the turn

Begin with a Magic Flute excerpt, where dialogue draws the audience in and the sung numbers catch the emotion. Then the Queen's Der Hölle Rache, coloratura rising like a weapon. Finally the piano reduction, where the melody stands without stage or orchestra. Popular appeal and serious meaning could share an evening.
