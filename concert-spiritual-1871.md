::: zh
1871年，纳什维尔的一所学校派出一队歌手北上筹款。灵歌从教堂与田间被搬上音乐厅舞台，成了可以印在节目单上的体裁。

## 年代与城市

1871年，纳什维尔，随后北上波士顿。菲斯克大学的朱比利歌手为学校筹款巡演，把黑人灵歌以合唱形式唱给北方听众。此后数十年，灵歌进入印刷乐谱、学校教材与独唱音乐会，巴黎与伦敦也出现同类巡演。

## 当时发生了什么

灵歌原本是口头传统，领唱即兴、会众应答，旋律与和声在每次演唱中变化。巡演需要固定的曲目、统一的音准和能被听众接受的呈现，于是出现了记谱的合唱改编，去掉过强的节奏与呼喊，保留五声音阶的旋律与应答结构。巡演曲目里既有灵歌，也有当时的通俗歌曲，灵歌常被安排在压轴。这一过程带有妥协，为了被当时的听众接受，编配被柔化；但它也让灵歌第一次以"作品"的形式被保存与传播。巡演时领唱由团员轮流担任，合唱按四部排列，钢琴或风琴给音，节目单把灵歌与通俗歌曲分开。

## 后来变了什么

灵歌成为音乐会体裁与出版品类。伯利在1910年代把它改编成艺术歌曲，钢琴伴奏写成独立声部，灵歌由此进入独唱音乐会的曲目。体裁的路径是"口头、合唱、独唱、教材"，每一步都改变它的听法，学校与教会的唱诗班随后把灵歌编进教材，成为美国合唱传统的一部分，教材里的版本通常比现场更慢、更整齐，和声逐渐固定，即兴空间缩小，也持续提出一个问题：当一种社群音乐被搬上舞台，谁有权决定它的"正确"版本。

## 听这一刻

先听传统形态的《Nobody Knows》，旋律朴素，句尾下沉；再听伯利的改编，钢琴有了前奏与和声色彩，人声被写成独唱；最后听《Deep River》与《Swing Low》，比较合唱版本与旋律谱的差别。库中两种形态都有，正好听"搬上舞台"前后的距离。

::: en
In 1871 a college in Nashville sent a choir north to raise money. Spirituals moved from church and field onto the concert platform and became a genre that could be printed in a programme.

## Year and city

Nashville in 1871, then Boston on the first northern tour. The Jubilee Singers of Fisk University toured to raise funds for the school, singing Black spirituals in choral arrangement for northern audiences. Over the following decades spirituals entered printed scores, school books and solo recitals, and similar tours reached Paris and London.

## What happened then

Spirituals were oral: a lead sang improvised lines, the congregation answered, and melody and harmony changed at each performance. Touring required fixed programmes, steady intonation and a presentation audiences would accept, so notated choral arrangements appeared, softening the strongest rhythm and shout while keeping pentatonic melody and call-and-response. The process involved compromise, since arrangements were smoothed for the listeners of the day. It also let the songs be preserved and circulated as works for the first time.

## What changed after

Spirituals became concert repertoire and a publishing category. In the 1910s Burleigh arranged them as art songs with an independent piano part, and they entered the solo recital. The path runs from oral practice to choral arrangement to solo song to textbook, and each step changes how the music is heard. It also raises a question that has not gone away: when a community's music is staged, who decides the correct version.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with the traditional Nobody Knows, the melody plain and the phrases sinking at the end. Then Burleigh's arrangement, with a piano introduction and harmonic colour and the voice written as a solo. Finally Deep River and Swing Low, comparing the choral version with the melody sheet. Both forms are in the catalogue, which is exactly the distance worth hearing.
