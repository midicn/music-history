## 问题

仪式需要音乐来标记“现在不同了”。婚礼、葬礼、加冕与弥撒都靠声音划出边界。音乐在仪式里是装饰，还是让参与者相信事情真的发生了？

## 历史脉络

中世纪的弥撒把经文与圣咏绑成固定程序，婚礼与葬礼各有专用段落。巴洛克宫廷用《感恩赞》迎接国王，音乐成为权力的声音布景。19世纪婚礼进行曲进入教堂，门德尔松与瓦格纳的段落被剪成标准流程。20世纪录音让仪式音乐可以播放，也让它脱离现场。

## 关键案例

莫扎特《安魂曲》把葬礼的恐惧与安慰写进同一套拉丁经文；门德尔松《仲夏夜之梦》的婚礼进行曲被19世纪婚礼采用，成为入场信号；夏庞蒂埃《感恩赞》的前奏长期被当作欧洲电视网颁奖礼的开场，说明仪式音乐可以脱离原场合。

## The question

Ritual needs music to mark that something is now different. Weddings, funerals, coronations and masses all use sound to draw a boundary. Is music decoration, or does it make participants believe that the change has really happened?

## Historical arc

Medieval masses bound scripture and chant into a fixed order, with separate music for weddings and funerals. Baroque courts greeted kings with a Te Deum, making music the soundscape of power. Wedding marches entered churches in the nineteenth century, and excerpts by Mendelssohn and Wagner were cut into a standard sequence. Recording made ritual music playable anywhere, and also detachable from the rite.

## Key cases

Mozart's Requiem writes fear and consolation into one Latin text. Mendelssohn's wedding march from A Midsummer Night's Dream was adopted by nineteenth-century weddings as an entrance signal. Charpentier's Te Deum prelude became a television awards theme, showing that ritual music can leave its original occasion.

## What to listen for

Hear how the Dies irae of the Requiem uses brass and timpani to sound like judgment. Hear how the dotted rhythm of the wedding march pushes people forward. Hear whether the Te Deum fanfare sounds like an announcement.
