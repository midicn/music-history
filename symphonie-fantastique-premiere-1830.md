::: zh
一份节目单被交给听众，交响曲从此可以开口讲一个具体的故事，而且讲的是作曲者自己。

## 时间与地点

1830年12月5日，巴黎音乐学院音乐厅。阿贝内克指挥，柏辽兹二十六岁。同一年的夏天，他刚拿到罗马大奖。

## 发生了什么

作品有五个乐章，附一份用第一人称写的节目单。艺术家服鸦片过量，在梦中看见爱人，随后是舞会、田野、断头台与女巫的安息日。一个反复出现的主题贯穿五个乐章，柏辽兹称它为主要乐思，后来通称固定乐思，每一次出现都换一副性格。首演之后他多次修改，1831年与1845年的版本在配器与乐章顺序上都有出入。

## 改变了什么

标题音乐从给音乐配解说，变成用形式讲故事。固定乐思让主题承担记忆与叙事的双重功能，乐章之间靠它保持连续。乐队被当成音色调色板，英国管、竖琴、大鼓与钟声进入交响曲的常规编制。作曲家自己写节目单，也让作品的意义部分地移到文字里，此后听交响曲常常先读一段说明。听众的记忆方式也被改写，人们开始在一部作品内部追踪同一条线索的变形。这条思路后来被电影配乐大量借用，一个动机反复出现，观众就知道是谁来了。李斯特的交响诗与瓦格纳的乐剧都在这条延长线上。

## 听这个转折

曲库没有《幻想交响曲》的录音，这里用同一位作曲家的三首作品建立坐标。先听《安魂曲》的末日经，铜管与合唱把空间一层层撑开。再听《维拉内尔》，旋律轻盈，伴奏却不断离调，像回忆里被扭曲的舞曲。最后听《玫瑰幽灵》，人声几乎被器乐托着走。三首共享同一套配器与和声趣味，听完再读那份节目单，会更容易理解1830年发生了什么。

::: en
A programme was handed to the audience, and the symphony could now tell a specific story, one about its own composer.

## Time and place

5 December 1830, the hall of the Paris Conservatoire. Habeneck conducted, and Berlioz was twenty-six. In the summer of that same year he had finally won the Prix de Rome.

## What happened

The work has five movements and came with a first-person programme. An artist takes too much opium, sees his beloved in a dream, and passes through a ball, a field, the scaffold and a witches' sabbath. One theme runs through all five movements; Berlioz called it the idee fixe, and each appearance changes its character. After the premiere he revised the score repeatedly, and the versions of 1831 and 1845 differ in orchestration and movement order.

## What changed

Programme music stopped being music with an explanation attached and became a way of telling a story through form. The fixed idea carries memory and narrative at once, holding the movements together. The orchestra became a palette, with cor anglais, harps, bass drum and bells entering the normal ensemble. Because the composer wrote the programme himself, part of the work's meaning moved into words, and audiences began to read a note before hearing a symphony. Liszt's symphonic poems and Wagner's music dramas stand on this extension.

## Listen for the turn

The library holds no recording of the Symphonie fantastique, so these three works by the same composer set the coordinates. Begin with the Dies irae of the Requiem, where brass and chorus open the space in layers. Then Villanelle, light in melody but restless in harmony, like a dance remembered wrong. Finally Le spectre de la rose, where the voice is carried almost entirely by the instruments. All three share the orchestral and harmonic taste of 1830, and hearing them before rereading the programme makes that year easier to understand.
