## 时间与地点

民族管弦乐队是 20 世纪的产物。1920 年代上海大同乐会以丝竹乐队改编《春江花月夜》，开合奏编制之先；1950 年代上海民族乐团、中国广播民族乐团先后成立，中央民族乐团 1960 年组建，彭修文等人的改编让编制稳定下来。它活跃于音乐厅、广播与唱片。

## 音乐特征

乐队按吹、拉、弹、打四部组织。吹管有笛、笙、唢呐、管；拉弦有二胡、中胡、革胡；弹拨有琵琶、中阮、大阮、扬琴、筝；打击有鼓、锣、钹、木鱼、云锣。乐器经过改良，加键笙、转调筝、低音革胡扩大了音域与音量。合奏靠指挥与总谱，声部按和声与配器分层。

## 代表曲目

《春江花月夜》《金蛇狂舞》《彩云追月》《瑶族舞曲》是常演的改编曲，二胡协奏曲《长城随想》与合奏《丝绸之路》代表新创作。

## 听什么

先听弹拨乐的颗粒与拉弦乐的线条怎样叠在一起，再听吹管在强奏时的音准。打击乐不只打拍子，还负责段落之间的色彩转换。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把民族管弦乐队当成传统乐种的原样放大。它是新编制，有指挥、总谱与配器；也不是只演奏改编曲，专为它写的作品已经很多。

## Time and place

The Chinese orchestra is a twentieth-century construction. In the 1920s the Datong Music Society in Shanghai arranged Spring River in the Flower Moon Night for a silk-and-bamboo band, an early attempt at a large fixed ensemble. The Shanghai and China Broadcasting orchestras followed in the 1950s, the Central Chinese Orchestra in 1960; Peng Xiuwen's arrangements stabilised the layout. It now belongs to concert halls, radio and recordings.

## Musical features

The band is organised in four sections: winds, bowed strings, plucked strings and percussion. Winds include dizi, sheng, suona and guan; bowed strings erhu, zhonghu and gehu; plucked strings pipa, zhongruan, daruan, yangqin and zheng; percussion drums, gongs, cymbals, wooden fish and yunluo. Reformed instruments such as the keyed sheng, the modulating zheng and the bass gehu widened range and volume. A conductor and full scores hold the parts together.

## Repertoire

Spring River in the Flower Moon Night, Jin She Kuang Wu, Cai Yun Zhui Yue and Yao Zu Wu Qu are standard arrangements, while the erhu concerto Great Wall Capriccio and the ensemble work Silk Road represent newer writing.

## What to listen for

Hear how the grain of the plucked strings sits against the line of the bowed strings, then check the intonation of the winds at full volume. Percussion does more than keep time; it marks the colour changes between sections.

## Common misunderstandings

The Chinese orchestra is often taken as a traditional genre simply scaled up. It is a new ensemble with a conductor, scores and orchestration. Nor does it only play arrangements; a substantial repertory has been written for it.
