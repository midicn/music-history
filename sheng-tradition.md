::: zh
笙是中国吹管乐器里少见的和声乐器。它靠一片片自由簧发声，吹也响，吸也响，能把笛子、唢呐与打击乐黏在一起。

## 历史

笙在先秦已出现，曾侯乙墓出土的笙斗与笙管说明当时形制已相当完整。周代八音把笙归入匏类，历代雅乐、燕乐与民间合奏都用它。明清时笙进入江南丝竹、吹打与戏曲场面，17至18世纪随传教士与商路传入欧洲，对自由簧乐器的发展产生过影响。20世纪以后出现加键笙与三十六簧笙，笙也能独奏与协奏。

## 乐器与演奏

笙由笙斗、笙苗与簧片组成，一簧一音，按孔与吹吸配合取音。传统笙多奏八度、五度与四度的和音，现代笙可奏更完整的和弦。吹吸皆鸣让乐句不必换气，循环换气又把它推得更长。笙在合奏里常奏和声背景，也常与笛子、唢呐齐奏旋律。

## 代表曲目

《凤凰展翅》《孔雀开屏》《晋调》《草原骑兵》《十八板》。这些曲目多从民间曲牌与地方吹打提炼，独奏时听的是簧片的金属味与和声的厚度。

## 听什么

先听一个长音里的簧片振动，再听吹吸切换有没有痕迹。笙的和声不是伴奏的填充，而是把旋律托起来的第二层。库中《金蛇狂舞》是钢琴改编，《姑苏风光》是江南民歌，只能对照合奏位置与江南语气，不是笙录音。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把笙当成唢呐的同类，或以为它只能伴奏。笙是自由簧，唢呐是双簧；笙能同时奏几个音，唢呐主要奏单线。笙也不只属于宫廷雅乐，江南丝竹、民间吹打与少数民族的芦笙都有它的亲戚。

::: en
The sheng is one of the few Chinese wind instruments that plays harmony. Free reeds sound on both the breath in and the breath out, and the instrument binds dizi, suona and percussion into a single wind band.

## History

The sheng existed by the pre-Qin period; the wind chest and pipes found in the tomb of Marquis Yi of Zeng show an already mature form. The Zhou eight-timbre system classed it with gourd instruments, and it served court ritual, banquet music and folk ensembles through the dynasties. In the Ming and Qing it entered Jiangnan sizhu, wind-and-percussion bands and opera. In the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries it travelled to Europe and influenced the development of free-reed instruments. Keyed and thirty-six-reed sheng appeared in the twentieth century, making the instrument a solo and concerto voice.

## The instrument and its playing

A sheng consists of a wind chest, pipes and reeds, one reed per pitch. Finger holes and breath direction select the notes. Traditional instruments favour octaves, fifths and fourths; modern sheng can play fuller chords. Because it sounds on both blow and draw, a phrase need not break for breath, and circular breathing extends it further. In ensemble the sheng usually supplies harmonic backing, and often doubles the melody with dizi or suona.

## Repertoire

Major pieces include The Phoenix Spreads Its Wings, The Peacock Displays Its Tail, Jin Melody, Prairie Cavalry and Eighteen Beats. Most are refined from folk qupai and local wind music; in solo performance one listens for the metallic reed and the thickness of the chords.

## What to listen for

Hear the reed vibration inside a long note, then whether the change from blow to draw leaves a seam. Sheng harmony is not filler: it is a second layer that lifts the melody. The library's Dance of the Golden Snake is a piano arrangement and Gusu Fengguang is a Jiangnan folk song; they show ensemble position and local tone, not the sheng itself.

## Common misunderstandings

The sheng is often grouped with the suona, or assumed to be only an accompanist. The sheng uses free reeds, the suona double reeds; the sheng sounds several notes at once, the suona mostly one line. Nor is it confined to court ritual: Jiangnan sizhu, folk wind bands and the lusheng of minority cultures are all relatives.
