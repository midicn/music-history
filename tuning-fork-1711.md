::: zh
在音叉出现之前，音高属于房间。管风琴的管子有多长，教堂里的 A 就有多高，换一座城市就换一个标准。一根钢叉把这件事变成了可以装进口袋的物件。

## 年代与城市

1711年前后，伦敦。传统说法把音叉的发明归给王室小号手约翰·肖尔，具体日期没有确证。十九世纪，舍伊布勒在1834年用一组钢叉组成音高计，1859年法国以法令规定标准音高，1885年维也纳会议再次确认，音叉始终是这些工作的工具。

## 当时发生了什么

音叉是两根同长的钢臂，敲击后发出几乎纯的正弦音，频率由长度与钢材决定。它不受温度与湿度的影响像管乐器那样大，也不占地方，可以随身携带。器乐制造者用它给自己的产品定音，歌手用它找音，乐团用它统一各声部。十九世纪的制造者把音叉做成可调式，加一个滑动砝码微调频率，音高于是可以被测量、被比较、被写进表格。

## 后来变了什么

音高从地方习惯变成可以运输的标准。乐器制造开始有跨城市的参照，管风琴、钢琴与管乐器可以在同一份合同里约定音高。标准化的另一面是冲突，各地乐团的音高在十九世纪一路走高，歌手抱怨唱不上去，会议与法令因此一再出现。音叉也把听音这件事部分交给了物件，乐手仍然用耳朵，但争论时可以拿出一个共同的证据。

## 听这一刻

先听亨德尔的管风琴协奏曲，管子的音高砌在建筑里，换一座教堂就换一个音高，音叉出现之前的标准就是这样地方性的。再听普赛尔的三重奏鸣曲，弦乐与数字低音互相对音，屋里的乐器就是标准。最后听贝多芬的小提琴协奏曲，弦乐按五度自己调，音高靠耳朵在乐手之间传递。三首都不是音叉的录音，听的是音高如何被某一件固定的乐器锚住。

::: en
Before the tuning fork, pitch belonged to the room. The length of an organ pipe fixed the A of a church, and another city meant another standard. A steel fork turned this into an object you could carry in a pocket.

## Year and city

London, around 1711. Tradition credits the invention to John Shore, a royal trumpeter, though the exact date is not documented. In the nineteenth century Scheibler built a tonometer from a set of forks in 1834, France fixed a standard pitch by decree in 1859, and a conference in Vienna confirmed it in 1885. The fork was the tool behind all of this work.

## What happened then

A tuning fork is two steel arms of equal length that sound an almost pure tone when struck, its frequency set by length and steel. It is less affected by temperature and humidity than a wind instrument, and it takes up no room, so it travels. Instrument makers used it to set the pitch of what they built, singers used it to find a note, ensembles used it to align their sections. Nineteenth-century makers added a sliding weight for fine adjustment, and pitch became something that could be measured, compared and written into tables.

## What changed after

Pitch turned from a local habit into a transportable standard. Instrument making acquired a reference that worked across cities, and organs, pianos and winds could be ordered to an agreed pitch in one contract. Standardisation had another face. Orchestral pitch climbed through the nineteenth century, singers complained that they could not reach the top, and conferences and decrees followed one another. The fork also moved part of listening into an object. Players still used their ears, but an argument could now produce a shared piece of evidence.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with Handel's organ concerto, where pitch is built into the pipework and changes with the building, the local standard that existed before the fork. Then Purcell's trio sonata, strings and continuo finding a common pitch, the instrument in the room setting the reference. Finally Beethoven's Violin Concerto, strings tuning in fifths by ear, pitch passing from player to player. None of these recordings uses a fork; what you hear is pitch anchored to a fixed instrument.
