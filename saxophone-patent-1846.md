## 时间，与当时的地点

1846年6月，巴黎。比利时工匠阿道夫·萨克斯在法国申请专利，把一套从高音到低音的萨克斯管写进同一份文件。

## 当时的场面

萨克斯把单簧片吹嘴装到圆锥形铜管上，用按键控制音孔。圆锥管让泛音更完整，音色比单簧管丰满，投射又比双簧管强。专利覆盖整个家族，也引来同行诉讼；1845年法国军乐队采用他的乐器，1857年巴黎音乐学院开设萨克斯班，由萨克斯本人执教。

## 影响与后果

乐队多了一种介于木管与铜管之间的声音，既能唱旋律也能填和声。柏辽兹与比才把它写进管弦乐，军乐队把它带到户外，后来爵士与流行音乐让它成为主角。萨克斯的专利家族最终稳定为高音、中音、次中音与上低音四种常用编制。

## 听一段转折

专利时代没有录音，这里用三首后来的萨克斯曲听这副嗓子。先听中音萨克斯的《小协奏曲》，听簧片音头与圆润的连奏；再听 Manookian 的奏鸣曲，听它从低吟冲到高音区的爆发；最后听《Danny Boy》的次中音萨克斯改编，听同一件乐器如何在通俗旋律里说话。专利保护的是结构，真正流传的是音色。

## When, and where

Paris, June 1846. The Belgian maker Adolphe Sax applied for a French patent that described a whole family of saxophones, from high to low.

## The scene at the time

Sax fitted a single-reed mouthpiece to a conical brass tube and controlled the tone holes with keys. The conical bore gave a fuller overtone series, a tone wider than the clarinet and a projection stronger than the oboe. The patent covered the family and also drew lawsuits from rivals; French military bands adopted his instruments in 1845, and the Paris Conservatoire opened a saxophone class in 1857 with Sax himself teaching.

## Effects and aftermath

Bands gained a voice between woodwind and brass, able to sing a melody or fill a harmony. Berlioz and Bizet wrote it into orchestral music, military bands carried it outdoors, and jazz and popular music later made it a star. The patented family settled into the four common sizes, soprano, alto, tenor and baritone.

## Hear the turn

The patent era left no recordings, so these three later works let you hear the voice it created. Begin with the Concertino for alto saxophone and piano, for the reed's attack and rounded legato. Then Manookian's Sonata, rising from a low murmur to a high-register outburst. Finally the tenor saxophone transcription of Danny Boy, the same instrument speaking through a popular melody. The patent protected a structure; what travelled was the timbre.
