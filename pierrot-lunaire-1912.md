## 时间与地点

1912年10月16日，柏林合唱厅。勋伯格的《月迷彼埃罗》由女演员策梅朗诵，作曲家指挥五人小乐队。

## 发生了什么

作品由二十一首短曲组成，歌词是吉罗的法语诗，经哈特莱本译成德语。长笛、单簧管、小提琴与大提琴轮换乐器，钢琴撑起和声。勋伯格要求朗诵者用说唱嗓音，音高写在谱上，却不唱满时值；旋律在无调性空间里跳来跳去，歌词里的月光、酒与死亡被拆成碎片。

## 改变了什么

人声不再只有唱与说两种状态，说唱成为一种可记谱的表情。无调性从理论变成舞台经验，表现主义的尖锐、怪诞与抒情可以同时出现。贝尔格的《沃采克》、魏尔的剧院音乐、后来的电影配乐与流行唱腔，都能在这部作品里找到先例。

## 听这个转折

先听《组曲》Op.25，十二音写作在钢琴上仍然有舞曲的骨架；再听《五首钢琴小品》Op.23，无调性靠音色、停顿与力度成形；最后听早期艺术歌曲，调性还在，但已经站在边缘。三步之后，耳朵会换一种听法。

## Time and place

16 October 1912, at the Choralion-Saal in Berlin. Schoenberg's Pierrot lunaire was recited by the actress Albertine Zehme, with the composer conducting a five-player ensemble.

## What happened

The work consists of twenty-one short pieces on French poems by Albert Giraud, translated into German by Otto Erich Hartleben. Flute, clarinet, violin and cello change instruments from number to number, and the piano anchors the harmony. Schoenberg asked the reciter for a speech-song voice, with pitches written in the score but not held for full value. The melody leaps through an atonal space, and the moon, wine and death of the poems are broken into fragments.

## What changed

The voice was no longer limited to singing or speaking; speech-song became a notation for expression. Atonality moved from theory into stage experience, and expressionist sharpness, grotesquerie and lyricism could appear together. Berg's Wozzeck, Weill's theatre music, later film scores and popular vocal styles all find a precedent here.

## Listen to the turn

Begin with the Suite Op. 25, where twelve-tone writing still keeps a dance-like frame at the piano. Then the Five Piano Pieces Op. 23, where atonality takes shape through colour, silence and dynamics. Finally an early song, still tonal but already standing at the edge. After three steps, the ear listens differently.
