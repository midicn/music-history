十九世纪后半叶，钢琴从奢侈品变成家具，家庭开始在客厅里自己制造音乐。

## 时间与地点

1850 至 1900 年，欧洲与北美。伦敦、巴黎、纽约与波士顿的琴厂用铸铁框架、毛毡槌与机器绕弦批量生产，立式琴塞进不大的房间。百货公司与邮购目录卖琴，琴行兼卖乐谱与琴凳，钢琴成为中产家庭的标配。

## 发生了什么

工业化把成本压低，立式琴取代三角琴进入普通住宅。买琴附送琴凳、谱架与教材，教师上门授课，女儿被要求学琴，钢琴课成为家庭开支的固定项。琴凳、节拍器、谱架与琴罩成套出售，客厅的一角被布置成小型琴房。四手联弹把交响曲与歌剧缩成两个人能弹的版本，家庭音乐会按礼拜天下午安排。乐谱按难度分级，练习曲、抒情小品与舞曲各有市场，出版商还专门编“少年曲集”。钢琴摆在客厅最显眼的位置，既是乐器也是家具与身份标志。

## 改变了什么

家庭演奏成为音乐市场的群众基础，多数人第一次听到贝多芬或瓦格纳是通过自己的手。体裁跟着房间缩小，短曲、无词歌、抒情小品与舞曲成为主流。钢琴也把性别分工写进音乐教育，弹琴被视为女性的教养，考级与比赛随后把这种教养变成可测量的成绩。录音与广播兴起后，钢琴的播放功能被取代，它变成家具、教学工具，后来又成为复古的对象。

## 听这个转折

先听格里格《抒情小品》第一册的《小咏叹调》，两句旋律，伴奏简单，踏板一换就成立。再听门德尔松《无词歌》，歌唱性旋律压在均匀的伴奏上，是客厅里的标准款式。再听肖邦《马祖卡》Op.33，舞曲节奏被写成独奏小品，家庭也能弹。最后听舒曼《童年情景》，标题短曲，技术不难，情感却复杂。转折前，这类音乐属于沙龙与宫廷；转折后，它属于买了琴的普通家庭。

In the later nineteenth century the piano turned from a luxury into furniture, and families began making music in the drawing room.

## Time and place

Europe and North America, 1850 to 1900. Piano factories in London, Paris, New York and Boston used cast-iron frames, felt hammers and machine-wound strings for mass output, and the upright fitted a small room. Department stores and mail-order catalogues sold instruments, shops sold scores and stools alongside, and the piano became standard equipment for the middle-class home.

## What happened

Industrial production lowered cost, and the upright replaced the grand in ordinary houses. A purchase came with stool, music desk and tutor, teachers called at home, daughters were expected to learn, and lessons became a fixed item in the household budget. Four-hand arrangements reduced symphonies and operas to what two people could play, and family concerts filled Sunday afternoons. Scores were graded by difficulty, with studies, lyrical pieces and dances each finding a market. The piano stood in the most visible corner of the parlour, an instrument and also furniture and a sign of standing.

## What changed

Domestic playing became the mass base of the music market, and most people first heard Beethoven or Wagner through their own hands. Genres shrank to the room, with the short piece, song without words, lyrical piece and dance becoming the norm. The piano also wrote a gender division into music education, playing being treated as a woman's accomplishment. When recording and radio arrived, the piano lost its playback function and became furniture, a teaching tool, and later an object of revival.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the Arietta from Grieg's first Lyric Pieces, two phrases and a simple accompaniment that stands up with one change of pedal. Then Mendelssohn's Song without Words, a singing line over an even accompaniment, the standard parlour model. Then Chopin's Mazurkas Op. 33, dance rhythm written as a solo piece a household could play. Finally Schumann's Scenes from Childhood, titled miniatures, technically easy and emotionally complex. Before the turn such music belonged to salon and court; afterwards to any family that had bought a piano.
