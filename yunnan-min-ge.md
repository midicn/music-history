## 时间与地点

云南民歌散布在滇中、滇西、滇南的坝子与山区。汉族聚居的弥渡、昆明、昭通多山歌与小调，彝族、白族、傣族等各有歌种。赶马、插秧、婚嫁与节庆都唱歌，山野对歌常见。

## 音乐特征

汉族山歌多用五声，羽调式与徵调式常见，起句高、句幅长，句尾常下滑。旋律爱用装饰音与滑音，衬词撑开句子，节拍自由。小调结构规整，常有四句与固定落音。云南花灯把民歌与歌舞搬上小戏舞台；少数民族歌种另有语言、声部与仪式功能。

## 代表曲目

《小河淌水》《弥渡山歌》《放马山歌》《耍山调》《大河涨水小河清》《猜调》流传较广。《小河淌水》由弥渡山歌整理改编，与原调已有距离。

## 听什么

先听《放马山歌》的第一声，注意歌手从低处跳进高音，再用衬词拉长句子。听《耍山调》的句尾下滑，那是山地语言的自然落势。再听《大河涨水小河清》，注意同一曲调怎样换词反复。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把云南民歌等同于少数民族民歌。云南汉族人口众多，山歌、小调与花灯同样成体系。另一个误解是把整理改编的《小河淌水》当作原生态，它的旋律与唱法都经过加工。

## Time and place

Yunnan's folk songs belong to basins and mountains across the province. Han areas such as Midu and Kunming favour mountain songs and xiaodiao, while Yi, Bai and Dai communities keep their own types. Work, weddings and festivals call for singing, and antiphonal singing across valleys is common.

## Musical features

Han mountain songs are mostly pentatonic, often in yu or zhi mode, with a high opening, long phrases and a falling close. Ornament and portamento are frequent, refrain syllables stretch the line, and rhythm is free. Xiaodiao are more regular, usually in four phrases. Yunnan flower-lantern theatre takes such tunes onto the stage, while minority repertoires carry their own languages and ritual duties.

## Repertoire

Known pieces include Small River Flowing, Midu Mountain Song, Herding Horses, Shua Shan Diao and The Big River Rises. Small River Flowing was arranged from a Midu tune and has moved from the original.

## What to listen for

Start with the first call of Herding Horses, where the singer leaps from low to high and stretches the line with refrain syllables. Follow the fall at the end of Shua Shan Diao, a natural cadence of mountain speech. Then hear how The Big River Rises repeats one tune with new words.

## Common misunderstandings

Yunnan folk song is often equated with minority music. Han communities are numerous, and their mountain songs, xiaodiao and flower-lantern tunes form a system of their own. Nor is the arranged Small River Flowing a field recording; its melody and delivery were reshaped.
