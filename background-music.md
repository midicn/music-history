1917 年，萨蒂提出“家具音乐”，第一次明确要求音乐不要被认真听。

## 时间与地点

1917 与 1920 年，巴黎。萨蒂在画展与午餐会上安排这种音乐，题为《家具音乐》，由小乐队在观众谈话时演奏。1934 年，美国出现以电话线传输录音的公司，后来被称为缪扎克，把音乐送进工厂、商店与电梯。扬声器装在墙角与天花板，音量由物业统一控制，个人无法挑选曲目。

## 发生了什么

萨蒂的想法是让音乐像壁纸与椅子一样，填补空间的空白，不要求注意力。他写的段落短、重复、没有戏剧高潮，演出时甚至允许观众走动与交谈，乐手不被当作舞台中心。萨蒂的乐队编制很小，几件管乐器加钢琴，短句重复，观众可以继续吃饭与谈话。缪扎克把这种功能工业化，用线路与扬声器把录音送到公共空间，按时间段与情绪编排节目，上午提神，午后放缓，录音接成连续节目带，中间不留掌声与报幕。两者都避免强对比，避免让人停下手里的事。

## 改变了什么

听觉分成两种模式，专注的听与环境中的听。音乐成为基础设施，扬声器取代了演奏者，作曲与编排开始以“不打扰”为目标。背景音乐也引发批评，有人说它侵占安静，有人说它把劳动与消费调节成统一的节奏。这种写法后来回到前台，环境音乐、lo-fi 与流媒体歌单都在用同一套原则，商场、健身房与视频的背景音也照此编排。

## 听这个转折

先听《裸体歌舞》第一首，速度慢，和声几乎不变，没有需要记住的主题发展。再听《梨形曲三段》，四手联弹，句子松散，像客厅里的谈话。最后听《烦恼》，同一片段反复几百次，听者要么被逼到注意，要么彻底把它当空气。转折前，音乐厅要求安静与专注；转折后，音乐被设计成可以忽略，连演奏者的存在都可以忘掉。

In 1917 Satie proposed furniture music, the first explicit request that music not be listened to seriously.

## Time and place

Paris, 1917 and 1920. Satie placed this music at an exhibition and at a lunch, calling it furniture music and having a small band play while the audience talked. In 1934 an American company began sending recorded music over telephone lines, later known as Muzak, into factories, shops and lifts. Loudspeakers sat in corners and ceilings under one building-wide volume, and no individual could choose the programme.

## What happened

Satie's idea was that music should fill a space like wallpaper or a chair, without asking for attention. His sections were short, repetitive and without dramatic climax, and listeners were allowed to walk and talk during them, the players not treated as the centre of a stage. His band was small, a few wind instruments and piano repeating short phrases while the meal went on. Muzak industrialised the function, sending recordings by wire and loudspeaker into public rooms and arranging programmes by hour and mood, bracing in the morning and slower after lunch. It joined the recordings into a continuous band with no applause or announcements between. Both avoided strong contrast and anything that might stop a person working.

## What changed

Listening split into two modes, attentive and ambient. Music became infrastructure, the loudspeaker replaced the performer, and composition and programming began to aim at not disturbing. Background music also drew criticism, for occupying silence and for tuning labour and shopping to one rhythm. The manner later returned to the foreground, and ambient music, lo-fi and streaming playlists all work from the same principles, as do the soundtracks of shops, gyms and video.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Gymnopédie No. 1, slow, its harmony almost unchanging, with no thematic development to remember. Then the three Morceaux en forme de poire, for four hands, their phrases loose like talk in a drawing room. Finally Vexations, one passage repeated hundreds of times, forcing the listener either to attend or to treat it as air. Before the turn the hall demanded silence and focus; afterwards music was designed to be ignored.
