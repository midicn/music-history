## 一个人的来历

**乔治·怀特菲尔德·查德威克**（1854–1931）生于马萨诸塞州洛厄尔，先在新英格兰音乐学院学习，后赴莱比锡与慕尼黑深造，跟雅达松、赖内克与莱因贝格学过作曲。回国后他在母校任教，1897 年起担任院长三十余年，同时写下交响曲、序曲、交响素描、室内乐、合唱与大量歌曲，还写过喜剧歌剧。

## 他所在的时代

查德威克与佩因、富特、麦克道尔、比奇、帕克一起，被后世称为“第二新英格兰乐派”。这一代人把欧洲音乐学院的技术带回美国，建立起可以教学、可以出版、可以演出本土作品的体系。他的音乐语言基本是德国的：奏鸣曲式、半音化和声、动机发展；但他常在里面放进美国的节奏与场景，比如《交响素描》里的狂欢节与万圣节。**他既是作曲家，也是把“美国作曲”变成一种职业的人。**

## 先听这三首

先听**两副面孔**。《两首随想曲》一快一慢，快的那首节奏有棱角，重音常常错位；慢的那首和声更厚，半音在里面慢慢移动。再听**旋律的来路**。《五首小品》里的抒情段落接近民歌或赞美诗，句子短、音域窄，但下面的和声并不朴素，常有出乎意料的转位与经过和弦。最后听改编后的线索。《三首情歌》Op.8 原是声乐作品，钢琴版把歌声交给右手，听旋律怎样在不换气的情况下被拉长；对照声乐版，能听出钢琴做了多少呼吸上的妥协。

## 作品与写法

- 管弦乐：《交响素描》、序曲《墨尔波墨涅》、第二交响曲
- 室内乐：弦乐四重奏、钢琴五重奏
- 声乐：合唱与独唱歌曲、赞美诗曲调
- 歌剧：《塔巴斯科》《教父》

## 留下的痕迹

查德威克长期执掌新英格兰音乐学院，把这所学校变成美国作曲教育的重要基地；他的行政工作与作品一起，让“在美国靠作曲与教学为生”成为可能。二十世纪后半叶他的音乐一度被遗忘，后来通过录音与乐团巡演重新出现。他的意义不在于发明风格，而在于证明美国也能建立自己的专业作曲传统。

## Life

**George Whitefield Chadwick** (1854–1931) was born in Lowell, Massachusetts, studied at the New England Conservatory, then in Leipzig and Munich with Jadassohn, Reinecke and Rheinberger. Back home he taught at his old school and served as its director from 1897 for more than three decades, while writing symphonies, overtures, symphonic sketches, chamber music, choral works and many songs, plus comic operas.

## His place

With Paine, Foote, MacDowell, Beach and Parker, Chadwick belongs to what later writers called the Second New England School. This generation brought European conservatory technique home and built a system in which American works could be taught, published and performed. His language is essentially German: sonata form, chromatic harmony, motivic development. Into it he often puts American rhythms and scenes, as in the carnival and Halloween movements of the Symphonic Sketches. **He was a composer and also the man who made American composition a profession.**

## What to hear

Hear first **two faces**. The Two Caprices set a fast piece against a slow one: the fast one has angular rhythm and displaced accents, the slow one thicker harmony with chromatic lines moving inside. Then hear **where the melody comes from**. The lyrical passages of the Five Pieces sit close to folk song or hymn: short phrases, narrow range, yet the harmony beneath is not plain, with unexpected inversions and passing chords. Finally hear the trail left by arrangement. The Three Love Songs, Op. 8, began as vocal music; in the piano version the song sits in the right hand, and you can hear where the line is stretched without a singer's breath. Compare a vocal recording to hear the compromises.

## Key works

- Orchestral: Symphonic Sketches, Melpomene overture, Symphony No. 2
- Chamber: string quartets, piano quintet
- Vocal: part-songs, solo songs, hymn tunes
- Opera: Tabasco, The Padrone

## Influence and misreadings

As long-serving director of the New England Conservatory, Chadwick turned the school into a base for American composition teaching; his administration and his works together made it possible to live by composing and teaching in the United States. His music faded after his death and returned through recordings and orchestral programming later in the century. **His importance lies less in inventing a style than in proving that America could build its own professional compositional tradition.**
