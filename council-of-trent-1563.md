::: zh
十六世纪中叶，教会内部有人担心复调把礼拜歌词唱得听不清。特伦托会议没有禁止复调，却让"歌词清楚、音响节制"成为宗教音乐的规范，也间接塑造了帕莱斯特里纳那一路风格。

## 时间与地点

1545 至 1563 年，意大利特伦托。会议断续开了十八年，处理教义、礼仪与音乐等议题。关于音乐的讨论集中在 1562 年前后，随后各地按照会议精神整顿教堂音乐。

## 发生了什么

会议本身没有留下禁止复调的条文，讨论记录里有改革者主张简化音乐，也有主张保留复调的声音。最终的方向是要求歌词清晰、避免世俗旋律混入、保持庄重。传说帕莱斯特里纳用一首弥撒"救下"复调，这个故事流传很广，但更接近后来的美化叙述。

## 改变了什么

此后一个多世纪，教堂音乐以清晰咬字与节制音响为标准，作曲家写作时先考虑词句能否被听见。复调没有被消灭，而是被要求服从礼拜。这套标准也影响了后世对"文艺复兴教堂风格"的想象，让帕莱斯特里纳成为教科书式的典范。

## 听这个转折

在垂怜经里听声部怎样在不同音区轮流出现，让每个词都能被听清。对比更早的复调，声部更密、词句更容易被淹没。会议之后的风格不是变简单，而是把复杂度放在不干扰歌词的位置。

::: en
In the middle of the sixteenth century some voices inside the Church worried that polyphony made the words of the liturgy unintelligible. The Council of Trent did not ban polyphony, but it made clear diction and restraint the standard for sacred music, and in doing so shaped the style associated with Palestrina.

## Time and place

1545 to 1563, in Trent in northern Italy. The council sat on and off for eighteen years, dealing with doctrine, liturgy and music. Musical discussion concentrated around 1562, and dioceses afterwards tidied their church music along the lines the council set.

## What happened

The council itself left no decree banning polyphony. Reformers in the debate wanted simpler music, others defended the contrapuntal tradition. The direction that emerged asked for intelligible words, no secular tunes and a dignified manner. The famous story that Palestrina saved polyphony with a single Mass is widely repeated but closer to later embellishment than to the record.

## What changed

For more than a century afterwards, sacred music was measured by clear diction and restrained sound. Composers wrote with the audibility of the text in mind. Polyphony was not abolished but required to serve the liturgy. The standard also shaped later ideas of a Renaissance church style, turning Palestrina into the textbook model.

## Listen for the turn

In the Kyrie settings, listen for voices taking turns across registers so every word comes through. Compared with earlier polyphony, the texture is no less skilful but far less crowded around the text. The post-council style is not simpler; it places complexity where it does not obscure the words.
