::: zh
第一次世界大战之后的巴黎，六个年轻作曲家被评论家打包成一个名字。他们并不共享一种风格，却共享一种态度：反对瓦格纳式的庞大与浪漫主义的自我抒情，赞成清晰、轻巧与日常。

## 年代与城市

1920 年前后的巴黎。米约、普朗克、奥涅格、奥里克、迪雷与塔耶费尔被评论家科莱在同一篇文章里并称。他们常在同一批沙龙与演出场合出现，也一起写过集体作品。

## 当时发生了什么

战后巴黎的音乐生活转向小型编制、爵士节奏与马戏、酒馆的趣味。六人团借用流行舞曲、进行曲与街头旋律，篇幅短、织体清、语气直白。萨蒂被视为精神上的先行者。几年后各人道路分化，这个标签更多属于 1920 年代初的巴黎气氛。

## 后来变了什么

"严肃音乐"与"通俗娱乐"的边界被有意放松。新古典主义在法国获得一种轻盈的版本，作曲家可以用舞厅节奏与马戏团音色写音乐会作品，而不必先证明其深刻。这套态度影响了后来的电影配乐、轻音乐与跨界写作。

## 听这一刻

在普朗克的即兴曲里听轻快的语调与不复杂的和声，在米约的巴西小品里听舞曲节奏的用法，在奥涅格的小品里听同一群体内部的严肃一极。转折不在于技法革新，而在于允许音乐轻。

::: en
In Paris after the First World War, a critic packaged six young composers under one name. They did not share a style but shared an attitude: against Wagnerian scale and romantic self-expression, in favour of clarity, lightness and the everyday.

## Year and city

Paris around 1920. Milhaud, Poulenc, Honegger, Auric, Durey and Tailleferre were grouped together in a single article by the critic Collet. They appeared in the same salons and concerts and even wrote a collective piece.

## What happened then

Post-war Paris turned to small ensembles, jazz rhythm and the spirit of the circus and the bar. Les Six borrowed popular dances, marches and street tunes, writing short, clear, plain-spoken music. Satie was treated as a spiritual precursor. Within a few years the members diverged and the label came to describe a mood of the early twenties rather than a school.

## What changed after

The border between serious music and popular entertainment was deliberately loosened. French neoclassicism gained a light version in which a composer could use ballroom rhythm and circus colour in a concert piece without first proving depth. That attitude fed later film scoring, light music and crossover writing.

## Listen for this moment

In Poulenc's improvisations, the light accent and uncomplicated harmony; in Milhaud's Brazilian pieces, dance rhythm in concert dress; in Honegger's set, the serious pole inside the same group. The turn is not technical novelty but permission for music to be light.
