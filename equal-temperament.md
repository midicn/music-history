::: zh
把八度切成十二个相等的半音，听上去是数学决定，实际上让所有调都能在同一件乐器上自由出现。巴赫用两卷《平均律》把这件事变成可听的日常。

## 时间与地点

十八世纪欧洲，1722年莱比锡。巴赫出版《平均律键盘曲集》第一卷，为二十四个大小调各写一首前奏曲与赋格。严格十二平均律的普及要到十九世纪，随钢琴制造与调律标准化完成。

## 发生了什么

十二平均律把八度按2的十二次方根分成十二份，每个半音频率比相同。纯五度略窄、大三度略宽，换来的是任何调都不需要重新调音，转调与等音变换畅通无阻。巴赫的well-tempered并不等于严格的十二平均律，而是让所有调都可用的循环调律；但它证明远调可以像C大调一样写作和聆听。

## 改变了什么

调性网络变成一张均匀的地图，作曲家可以从任何调出发，也可以在任何调上停留。钢琴、管风琴与吉他的品丝据此定音，现代和声的转调、半音化与等音关系都建立在此。代价是纯律的纯净三度被牺牲，收益是十二个调的自由。

## 听这个转折

先听C大调前奏曲，再听升C大调、升F大调和降A大调的赋格。远调不再有走音的紧张，主题在五六个升降号里照样成立。库中版本是钢琴，听调性与音准的稳定，而不是古乐器的调律差异。

::: en
Dividing the octave into twelve equal semitones sounds mathematical. In practice it let every key appear freely on one instrument, and Bach made the change audible in two books of preludes and fugues.

## Time and place

Eighteenth-century Europe, Leipzig in 1722. Bach published Book I of the Well-Tempered Clavier, with a prelude and fugue in all twenty-four keys. Strict equal temperament spread only in the nineteenth century, with piano manufacture and standardised tuning.

## What happened

Equal temperament divides the octave by the twelfth root of two, so every semitone has the same ratio. Fifths are slightly narrow and major thirds slightly wide; in return, no key needs retuning, and modulation works everywhere. Bach's well-tempered tuning was not strict equal temperament but a circulating system that made all keys usable. It proved that remote keys could be written and heard as naturally as C major.

## What changed

The network of keys became an even map. Composers could start anywhere and stay anywhere, and guitars, organs and pianos were tuned accordingly. Modern modulation, chromaticism and enharmonic relations rest on this base. The price was the pure third of just intonation; the gain was the freedom of all twelve keys.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the C major prelude, then hear the fugues in C sharp major, F sharp major and A flat major. Remote keys no longer carry the tension of being out of tune; the subject holds in five or six sharps or flats. The versions are piano; listen for stable intonation, not old instruments' tuning differences.
