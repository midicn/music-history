## 核心问题

乐谱写下的是结果，即兴发生的是过程。古典音乐常被想象成完全照谱演奏，但数字低音、华彩段和管风琴前奏都依赖演奏者当场决定。即兴为何从中心退到边缘，又如何在爵士与实验音乐里回来？

## 时间线

中世纪与文艺复兴的演唱者会在固定旋律上添加装饰；巴洛克数字低音只给低音与和弦标记，演奏者负责织体；古典协奏曲的华彩段留给独奏者自由发挥。19世纪乐谱越来越细，即兴空间缩小，华彩段被写成固定文本。20世纪爵士把即兴重新置于核心，拉格泰姆与布鲁斯提供句式，比博普加快和声。当代实验音乐又用图形谱和开放式指令恢复选择。

## 几个例子

巴赫《平均律》前奏曲以分解和弦铺路，接近即兴练习；肖邦《幻想即兴曲》把即兴姿态写成固定作品；胡梅尔为莫扎特协奏曲写的华彩证明“自由”也会被记谱保存。三例展示即兴如何被写下来。

## 听这几处

听前奏曲的和弦是否像在试探调性；听幻想即兴曲的右手是否抢在拍点之前；听华彩段的和声是否离开主题又回到主调。

## The core question

A score records a result; improvisation records a process. Classical music is often imagined as exact score-reading, yet figured bass, cadenzas and organ preludes depended on decisions made in the moment. Why did improvisation move from centre to edge, and how did it return in jazz and experimental music?

## On the timeline

Medieval singers added ornaments to a fixed melody. Baroque figured bass supplied only a bass line and chord signs, leaving texture to the player. Concertos left the cadenza open to the soloist. In the nineteenth century scores grew more detailed, improvisation shrank, and cadenzas were written out. Jazz returned improvisation to the centre in the twentieth century, while ragtime and blues supplied phrases and bebop sped up harmony. Experimental music restored choice through graphic scores and open instructions.

## A few examples

Bach's Well-Tempered prelude builds from broken chords like an exercise. Chopin's Fantaisie-Impromptu fixes an improvisatory gesture as a finished work. Hummel's cadenza for Mozart shows that even freedom can be written down.
