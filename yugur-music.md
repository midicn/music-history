## 年代与地方

裕固族聚居在甘肃肃南裕固族自治县，酒泉、张掖与祁连山北麓也有。肃南的康乐、皇城、明花是主要分布区，牧场与绿洲相间。裕固语分西部裕固语与东部裕固语，前者属突厥语族，后者属蒙古语族，两种语言不互通，没有传统文字，通用汉文。婚礼、剪马鬃、祭鄂博与春节是主要场合。

## 它是怎么来的

裕固族的音乐以歌为主。牧歌在草原与山场唱，句幅长，节奏自由；酒歌在婚礼与待客时唱，主客对唱，唱词祝福；婚礼歌分女方与男方，唱离别、迎亲与祝福。裕固族信仰藏传佛教，寺院唱诵与民歌并行；萨满的遗存保留在祭鄂博等习俗里。二十世纪后期，裕固族民歌被采录，部分曲目进入舞台。两种语言都在流失，能完整唱老歌的人越来越少，具体曲目的语言归属需要逐首核对。

## 声音的骨架

牧歌起句高，句尾长拖，装饰音细，节奏随呼吸伸缩。酒歌的句幅整齐，主客接腔，结尾常一起上扬。婚礼歌用真声，句尾带颤，唱到离别处近于哭腔。旋律多五声，音域宽。牧歌的歌词多咏叹家世与草原，句尾常带衬词。乐器在传统中不占主导，笛子、二胡多用于伴奏；现代演出加手风琴与吉他。

## 听什么

先听牧歌的长句怎样在呼吸处断开，再听酒歌结尾的上扬。essen-000885 标题写明裕固族，能听到高腔与句尾；essen-008750 是撒拉族民歌，essen-000830 是土族民歌，只作河西与河湟的声腔参照。三首都是创作或整理过的录音，不是牧场上的现场。

## 常被误会的地方

常见误解是把裕固族当成维吾尔族的一支，或以为裕固族只说一种语言。西部裕固语属突厥语族，东部裕固语属蒙古语族，两种语言并存。也不是所有裕固族歌都是牧歌，酒歌、婚礼歌与宗教唱诵各有场合。裕固族音乐在婚礼与节庆中仍在唱，但传承的压力是真实的。

## When and where

The Yugur live in Sunan Yugur Autonomous County in Gansu, with communities around Jiuquan, Zhangye and the northern Qilian foothills. Kangle, Huangcheng and Minghua in Sunan are the main areas, where pasture and oasis alternate. Yugur has two languages: Western Yugur belongs to Turkic and Eastern Yugur to Mongolic; they are not mutually intelligible, have no traditional script, and Chinese is used for writing. Weddings, horse-mane cutting, obo sacrifices and Spring Festival are the main occasions.

## How it came about

Yugur music is mainly song. Pastoral songs are sung on grassland and mountain pasture, long-phrased and free in rhythm. Drinking songs are sung at weddings and when hosting guests, in antiphony between host and guest with words of blessing. Wedding songs divide between the bride's and groom's sides, telling parting, welcome and blessing. The Yugur follow Tibetan Buddhism, so monastic chant and folk song coexist; shamanic traces remain in obo sacrifice. From the late twentieth century songs were recorded and some entered stage programmes. Both languages are losing speakers, fewer people can sing the old songs in full, and the language of each item needs case-by-case checking.

## The sound's frame

Pastoral songs open high, hold long phrase ends and use fine ornament, with rhythm stretching to the breath. Drinking songs are even-phrased, answered between host and guest, often rising together at the close. Wedding songs use chest voice with a tremble at the end, close to weeping at parting. Melodies are mostly pentatonic and wide in range. Instruments are secondary; dizi and erhu accompany, and modern performances add accordion and guitar.

## What to listen for

Hear how the pastoral long phrase breaks at the breath, then the rising close of a drinking song. Essen-000885 is explicitly Yugur, with high register and phrase endings; essen-008750 is a Salar song and essen-000830 a Tu song, regional references from the Hexi corridor and Yellow River highlands. All three are composed or arranged recordings, not pasture field recordings.

## What is often mistaken

The Yugur are often taken as a branch of the Uyghur, or assumed to speak one language. Western Yugur is Turkic and Eastern Yugur is Mongolic, and both survive. Nor is every Yugur song pastoral; drinking songs, wedding songs and religious chant have separate occasions. Yugur music is still sung at weddings and festivals, but the pressure on transmission is real.
