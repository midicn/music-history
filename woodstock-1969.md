::: zh
1969年8月，四十多万人涌进纽约州伯塞尔的农场。原定几万人的场地被冲垮，雨水与泥泞把三天演出变成一代人的集体记忆。

## 时间与地点

1969年8月15日至18日，纽约州伯塞尔附近的亚斯格农场。迈克尔·兰等四位主办者把音乐节从伍德斯托克镇搬到伯塞尔。原定售票，因人流失控改为免费入场，到场人数超过四十万。

## 发生了什么

三十二组艺人轮番上台，包括琼·贝兹、桑塔纳与吉米·亨德里克斯。暴雨把场地变成泥塘，食物与医疗告急，附近居民开车运送物资。8月18日清晨，亨德里克斯用反馈与失真把美国国歌拆成噪音。现场专辑与电影随后发行。

## 改变了什么

摇滚从俱乐部与体育馆升级为大规模户外仪式，音乐节成为产业。现场录音、电影与新闻报道把地方演出变成全球符号，也暴露了后勤、安全与商业化的矛盾；同年年底的阿尔塔蒙特事件让免费音乐节的理想退潮。

::: en
In August 1969 over 400,000 people crowded onto a farm in Bethel, New York. A site planned for a few tens of thousands was overrun, and rain and mud turned three days into a collective memory.

## Time and place

15 to 18 August 1969, Max Yasgur's farm near Bethel. Michael Lang and three partners moved the event from Woodstock village; tickets were dropped, the gates opened free, and attendance passed 400,000.

## What happened

Thirty-two acts appeared, among them Joan Baez, Santana and Jimi Hendrix. Rain turned the fields to mud, food and medical care ran short. At dawn on 18 August Hendrix tore the anthem into feedback and distortion. A live album and film followed.

## What changed

Rock moved from clubs to a mass outdoor ritual, and the festival became an industry. Recording, film and news turned a local event into a global symbol, while exposing conflicts of logistics, safety and commerce; Altamont at the end of the year cooled the free-festival ideal.

## Listen for the turn

The library holds no Woodstock recordings and no rock-band versions; these four are other recordings of festival songs. Begin with Oh Happy Day, which Baez used to open; then Swing Low, Sweet Chariot and The Star-Spangled Banner beside Hendrix's closing solo; finally The House of the Rising Sun.
