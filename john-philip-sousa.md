## 出身与师承

**约翰·菲利普·苏萨**（1854–1932）生于华盛顿，父亲是海军陆战队乐队的长号手。他十三岁进乐队当学徒，1880 年起担任这支乐队的指挥，十二年后辞职组建自己的管乐团，带着它在美国与欧洲巡演。他一生写下一百多首进行曲，还有轻歌剧与大量改编曲，其中《星条旗永不落》几乎成了美国的第二国歌。

## 时代与位置

苏萨活动的年代，管乐队是美国城镇公共生活的主角：游行、竞选、博览会、舞会都靠它。他把这种实用音乐提高到可以巡演、可以卖票的水平，并参与改良了低音号（苏萨号）以便行进时携带。**他的进行曲不是军乐的附属品，而是十九世纪末美国娱乐工业与城市文化的产物**，同时继承了欧洲进行曲的曲式。他的乐队编制庞大，一场音乐会从序曲、独奏到进行曲，像一次综艺演出。

## 先听什么

先听**曲式的骨架**。《星条旗永不落》由引子、两个乐段、三声中部与断裂段组成，三声中部通常转到下属调，情绪突然变亮；最后一遍三声中部时，短笛的固定音型浮在整支乐队之上，把结尾一层层锁紧。再听**低音与旋律的问答**。《华盛顿邮报》原为报纸征稿而写，节奏轻快，低音常提前半拍接住旋律的尾音。最后听改编谱的局限。目录里的三首都是钢琴改编，只能听清旋律、低音与和声骨架；铜管的爆发力与短笛的穿透力，要到管乐队原版才完整。

## 作品与写法

- 进行曲：《星条旗永不落》《自由之钟》《华盛顿邮报》《船长》
- 轻歌剧：《船长》（1896）
- 其他：《跨越海洋》《棉花王》《无敌之鹰》

## 影响与误读

苏萨的乐队在二十世纪初是最早的“流行音乐巡演”之一，他的曲目通过录音与乐谱进入全世界的军乐队与学校乐队。他把管乐队从仪式工具变成音乐会媒介，也让进行曲成为一种可以反复演奏、经得起分析的曲式；今天学校乐队的训练曲目里，仍有他的位置。

## Training and teachers

**John Philip Sousa** (1854–1932) was born in Washington, DC, the son of a trombonist in the Marine Band. He joined that band as an apprentice at thirteen, led it from 1880, and twelve years later resigned to form his own concert band, which he took on tour across America and Europe. He wrote more than a hundred marches, along with operettas and a great deal of arrangement work; The Stars and Stripes Forever became something like a second national anthem.

## His place

In Sousa's lifetime the wind band was central to American public life: parades, campaigns, fairs and dances all depended on it. He raised this functional music to a level that could tour and sell tickets, and he helped develop the sousaphone so that a bass instrument could be carried while marching. **His marches are not a by-product of military music but a product of late nineteenth-century American entertainment and city culture**, built on European march forms.

## Where to start

Hear first **the skeleton of the form**. The Stars and Stripes Forever runs introduction, two strains, trio and break strain; the trio usually moves to the subdominant and the mood suddenly brightens. On the final trio the piccolo obbligato rides above the whole band and locks the ending down layer by layer. Then hear **the dialogue of bass and melody**. The Washington Post was written for a newspaper, its rhythm light, its bass often catching the end of a melodic phrase half a beat early. Finally hear **what an arrangement cannot give**. All three tracks here are piano reductions: melody, bass and harmonic frame survive, but the brass punch and piccolo edge need the full band.

## Works and craft

- Marches: The Stars and Stripes Forever, The Liberty Bell, The Washington Post, El Capitan
- Operetta: El Capitan (1896)
- Others: Hands Across the Sea, King Cotton, The Invincible Eagle

## Influence and misreadings

Sousa's band was one of the first popular-music touring enterprises of the twentieth century, and his repertoire reached bands and school ensembles worldwide through recordings and printed parts. He turned the wind band from a ceremonial tool into a concert medium, and made the march a form that can be replayed and analysed; school band programmes still train on his music.
