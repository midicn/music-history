::: zh
后摇滚把摇滚乐队的编制用来做长段结构、音色变化与渐强，而不是主歌副歌，1990 年代初在芝加哥与伦敦成形。

## 起源与年代
1991 年，Slint 在路易斯维尔发行《Spiderland》，Talk Talk 在伦敦发行《Laughing Stock》，两张唱片都把摇滚乐器推向安静与长结构。1994 年 Bark Psychosis 的《Hex》让乐评人 Simon Reynolds 在《Melody Maker》上写下“后摇滚”一词。芝加哥的 Tortoise、格拉斯哥的 Mogwai 与蒙特利尔的 Godspeed You! Black Emperor 随后把这一思路扩展。

## 听辨特征
曲长常超过六分钟，结构靠渐强、重复与音色变化推进，而不是副歌。吉他多用清音、延迟与滑棒，鼓从简单节拍逐渐加密，贝斯重复短句。人声常缺席，若有也当作音色使用。动态范围大，从几乎听不见到全乐队齐奏，段落之间没有明显的终止式，收尾常靠音量自然衰减。吉他手用延迟与滑棒制造持续音，段落靠配器逐层加入；一段音乐常在几分钟里只做一件事。很多作品不设主旋律，重复的动机靠配器变化获得新意。

## 代表人物
Tortoise 的多乐器编制把爵士与电子带进来，Mogwai 用吉他渐强建立现场模板，Godspeed You! Black Emperor 用弦乐、磁带与长曲结构。Bark Psychosis 与 Talk Talk 提供了更安静的原型，Slint 把低声诵读与突然爆发结合，后来的 Explosions in the Sky 把这一套推向更明亮的版本。

## 谱系
上承后朋克、自赏、极简主义与实验摇滚，下接氛围金属、后古典与 2000 年代的器乐摇滚。它把“渐强”变成一种可识别的形式手段，也让器乐重新获得叙事能力。2000 年代，这一套手法被广告、电影与体育节目大量使用，逐渐变成一种通用的配乐语言。

## 常见误解
常见说法是后摇滚就是没有人唱的摇滚，其实人声有时出现，只是不作为叙事中心。另一个误解是这个标签从一开始就指吉他渐强，Simon Reynolds 最初用它描述更接近电子与采样的音乐。把它当成一个固定的音色，也会忽略芝加哥、格拉斯哥与蒙特利尔之间的差别。把它当成一种编制同样不准确，弦乐、铜管与电子设备都可以加入。

::: en
Post-rock used rock instrumentation for long structures, shifting texture and crescendo rather than verse and chorus, forming in Chicago and London in the early 1990s.

## Origins and era
In 1991 Slint released Spiderland in Louisville and Talk Talk released Laughing Stock in London, both pushing rock instruments toward quiet and long form. In 1994 Bark Psychosis's Hex led the critic Simon Reynolds to write the word post-rock in Melody Maker. Tortoise in Chicago, Mogwai in Glasgow and Godspeed You! Black Emperor in Montreal then widened the approach.

## What you hear
Tracks often pass six minutes, and structure moves through crescendo, repetition and changes of texture rather than a chorus. Guitars use clean tones, delay and slide, drums thicken gradually from a simple beat, and bass repeats short figures. Vocals are often absent, and when present they work as colour. The dynamic range is wide, from near silence to the full band, sections avoid clear cadences, and endings often fade by volume. Guitar uses delay and slide for sustain, layers enter one at a time, and a passage may do only one thing for several minutes. Many pieces have no lead melody; a repeated motif gains interest from changes of instrumentation.

## Key figures
Tortoise brought jazz and electronics in through a multi-instrumental line-up, Mogwai built a live template from guitar crescendo, and Godspeed You! Black Emperor used strings, tape and long forms. Bark Psychosis and Talk Talk supplied quieter prototypes, Slint joined low spoken words to sudden eruption, and later Explosions in the Sky made a brighter version of the same set of moves.

## Lineage
Post-punk, shoegaze, minimalism and experimental rock feed in; ambient metal, post-classical and 2000s instrumental rock come out. It turned the crescendo into a recognisable formal device and gave instrumental music a narrative voice again. In the 2000s the device was used heavily in advertising, film and sports broadcasts, and it became a common scoring language.

## Common misunderstandings
The common claim is that post-rock means rock without singing, when voices do appear, only not as the centre of the story. Another is that the label always meant guitar crescendo; Reynolds first used it for music closer to electronics and sampling. Treating it as one fixed tone also ignores the differences between Chicago, Glasgow and Montreal. Treating it as a fixed line-up is equally wrong, since strings, brass and electronics all join in.
