## 问题

音乐能治病吗？它能止痛、安抚、组织集体哀悼，却很少单独治愈疾病。问题不在于“有没有效”，而在于它在什么场合、由谁、对谁起作用。

## 历史脉络

中世纪修道院用圣咏规训呼吸与时间，希尔德加德的作品把身体不适写进祈祷。南意大利的塔兰泰拉舞曲被当作蜘蛛咬伤后的舞蹈疗法，节奏与出汗被赋予排毒意义。19世纪医院与沙龙把音乐当镇静剂，20世纪音乐治疗成为专业，临床用它改善疼痛、焦虑与沟通。录音与耳机又把自我疗愈变成日常。

## 关键案例

巴伯《弦乐柔板》在公共哀悼中反复出现，慢速级进让悲伤有秩序；马尔图齐《塔兰泰拉》用加速的六八拍模拟舞蹈治疗；希尔德加德《O Virtus Sapientiae》以单声旋律把注意力收进词句。三例分别对应哀悼、舞蹈与祈祷。

## 听什么

听《弦乐柔板》的旋律如何一次次上升又落下；听塔兰泰拉的节拍是否越跑越快；听圣咏的音域是否窄到适合集体吟唱。

## The question

Can music heal? It can soothe, ease pain and give shape to public mourning, but it rarely cures disease by itself. The question is not whether it works, but where, by whom and for whom.

## Historical arc

Medieval monasteries used chant to regulate breath and time, and Hildegard wrote bodily discomfort into prayer. In southern Italy the tarantella was danced as a cure for a spider bite, with rhythm and sweat given purgative meaning. Hospitals and salons used music as a sedative in the nineteenth century. Music therapy became a profession in the twentieth, used clinically for pain, anxiety and communication. Recordings and headphones made self-treatment a daily habit.

## Key cases

Barber's Adagio for Strings recurs in public mourning, its slow steps giving grief an order. Martucci's Tarantella imitates dance therapy with an accelerating six-eight pulse. Hildegard's O Virtus Sapientiae draws attention into the words through a single melodic line. Mourning, dance and prayer, one case each.

## What to listen for

Hear how the Adagio rises and falls. Hear whether the Tarantella pulse keeps speeding up. Hear whether the chant's range is narrow enough for a congregation to join.
