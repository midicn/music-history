::: zh
交响诗是单乐章的管弦乐叙事体裁，用主题变形和配器讲文学、绘画或风景，不靠人声与布景。

## 起源与年代
李斯特在 1848 至 1854 年的魏玛时期写下《前奏曲》《塔索》《哈姆雷特》，把标题音乐推向管弦乐舞台。十九世纪后半，斯美塔那、理查·施特劳斯、西贝柳斯与德彪西各自扩展它的边界。

## 听辨特征
只有一个连续乐章，段落靠速度、调性与配器切换，而不是靠乐章之间的停顿。核心动机常被变形，同一旋律换调、换乐器、换节奏，代表人物或情节的转折。结尾可以突然安静，也可以堆到全奏。听的时候先抓反复出现的短动机，再看它怎样改变性格。

## 代表人物
李斯特确立体裁，斯美塔那写《我的祖国》，施特劳斯写《唐璜》《死与净化》，西贝柳斯写《芬兰颂》，德彪西写《牧神午后前奏曲》。

## 谱系
上承音乐会序曲与标题交响曲，下接音诗、电影配乐与二十世纪单乐章管弦乐。

## 常见误解
交响诗不是配了文字的管弦乐，文字只提供线索。它也不等于随意拼贴，主题变形通常有严密的结构计划。

::: en
A symphonic poem is a one-movement orchestral form that tells a story, paints a scene or follows a literary idea without voices or staging.

## Origins and dating
Liszt coined the form during his Weimar years, writing Les Préludes, Tasso and Hamlet between 1848 and 1854. Later in the century Smetana, Richard Strauss, Sibelius and Debussy each stretched its boundaries.

## Listening features
There is one continuous movement, and its sections are marked by changes of tempo, key and orchestration rather than by separate movements. A short motive is transformed, changing key, instrument or rhythm to signal a character or a turn of plot. The ending may fade or build to full orchestra. Listen first for the recurring motive, then for how its character changes.

## Figures
Liszt established the genre. Smetana wrote Má Vlast, Strauss wrote Don Juan and Death and Transfiguration, Sibelius wrote Finlandia, and Debussy wrote Prélude à l'après-midi d'un faune.

## Lineage
It grows from the concert overture and the programmatic symphony and leads to the tone poem, film score and single-movement orchestral works of the twentieth century.

## Misconceptions
A symphonic poem is not orchestral music with a text attached, since the program only offers a clue. Nor is it loose patchwork, because its transformations usually follow a firm structural plan.
