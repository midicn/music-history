## 时间与地点

彝族分布在四川凉山，云南楚雄、红河、文山、大理，贵州毕节、六盘水与广西隆林。凉山是最大的聚居区，云南石林、弥勒、泸西一带是阿细支系的家园，石屏异龙湖畔流行海菜腔。彝语属汉藏语系藏缅语族，分六大方言，传统彝文在毕摩经书中使用。

## 发生了什么

彝族音乐按支系与场合分得很细。阿细跳月在月夜或节庆的场院进行，男女围圈，大三弦与笛子领舞；海菜腔在石屏的湖上与田间对唱，分领唱与帮腔，篇幅可长可短；凉山的毕摩在仪式中诵经，声调近于吟诵。二十世纪后期，这些歌被搬上舞台，海菜腔进入全国性比赛，阿细跳月成为旅游与节庆的固定节目。具体曲目的形成年代多靠口传，文献证据有限。

## 音乐特征

旋律多用五声，音域宽，跳进多。海菜腔以真假声交替，句首高起，句尾长拖，帮腔在句末插入；阿细跳月的歌短，强拍明显，配合大三弦的切分。月琴多为四弦，定弦与拨奏决定舞曲的骨架，口弦以口腔共鸣出泛音，音量小，贴近说话。酒歌与哭嫁歌用不同的发声位置，前者响亮，后者带哭腔。对歌多在节庆与赶集时进行，男女分站两边，轮流起句。

## 听什么

先听海菜腔的高音怎样从假声滑向真声，再听帮腔在句尾的插入。essen-008776 是跳月歌，听强拍与重复；essen-000392 是路南山歌，听高腔与句读；essen-000399 是寻甸山歌，听装饰音与收束。这三首都不是舞台改编版，能听到山野间真实的呼吸。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把海菜腔当成所有彝族的民歌，或把阿细跳月当成彝族的统一歌舞。它们各有支系与地点。也并不是所有彝族音乐都只有五声，口弦与月琴的泛音会带出五声之外的音。彝族音乐至今仍在婚礼、节庆与仪式中使用，并不只是舞台节目。

## Time and place

The Yi live in Liangshan in Sichuan, in Chuxiong, Honghe, Wenshan and Dali in Yunnan, in Bijie and Liupanshui in Guizhou, and in Longlin in Guangxi. Liangshan is the largest concentration; Shilin, Mile and Luxi in Yunnan are home to Axi branches, and haicaiqiang singing is centred on the shores of Yilong Lake in Shiping. Yi belongs to the Tibeto-Burman branch of Sino-Tibetan and has six major dialect groups; the traditional Yi script is used in bimo ritual books.

## What happened

Yi music is organised closely by branch and occasion. Axi moon dancing takes place in courtyards on moonlit nights or festivals, with men and women in a circle led by the large three-string and flute. Haicaiqiang is sung antiphonally on the lake and in the fields around Shiping, with a lead voice and answering chorus, in spans long or short. In Liangshan, bimo priests chant in rituals close to speech. From the late twentieth century these genres were staged, haicaiqiang entered national competitions, and Axi moon dancing became a fixture of festivals and tourism. Dates of individual tunes rest on oral transmission, and documentary evidence is limited.

## Musical features

Melodies are mostly pentatonic, wide in range, with many leaps. Haicaiqiang alternates falsetto and chest voice, rises high at the phrase start, holds the end, and lets the chorus enter at the close. Axi dance songs are short, with clear strong beats against the three-string's syncopation. The yueqin's tuning and plucking set the dance frame; the mouth harp uses the mouth cavity to select overtones and is quiet, close to speech. Drinking songs and wedding laments use different vocal placements, one bright and one tearful.

## What to listen for

Hear how the haicaiqiang high note slides from falsetto into chest voice, then how the chorus enters at the phrase end. Essen-008776 is a moon-dance song with strong beats and repetition; essen-000392 is a Lunan mountain song with high register and clear phrasing; essen-000399 is a Xundian mountain song with ornament and close. None is a stage arrangement, so the breath of the outdoors remains.

## Common misunderstandings

Haicaiqiang is often treated as the song of all Yi people, and Axi moon dancing as a unified Yi dance. Each belongs to particular branches and places. Nor is all Yi music pentatonic; the overtones of mouth harp and yueqin bring other pitches. Yi music is still used at weddings, festivals and rituals, not only on stage.
