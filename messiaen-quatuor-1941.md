## 时间与地点

1941年1月15日，西里西亚格尔利茨的战俘营 Stalag VIII-A。梅西安作为法军战俘被关在这里，首演在一间冰冷的营房里举行，听众是战俘与看守。

## 发生了什么

梅西安在1940年被俘，狱中遇到小提琴手、单簧管手与大提琴手，于是为这三件乐器加钢琴写了八首曲子。营地没有像样的琴，大提琴缺弦，钢琴踏板与琴键都不灵。作品以《启示录》第十章为框架，乐章标题写鸟鸣、深渊与永恒；第五乐章《赞美耶稣的永恒》让大提琴在钢琴的长音上缓慢歌唱。

## 改变了什么

极端处境下的写作不必宏大，几件破损乐器也能承载完整的时间观。梅西安把有限移调调式与附加节奏放进同一部作品，战后它成为他走向国际的起点。室内乐也被证明可以是一份见证，而不只是沙龙或音乐厅的节目。

## 听这个转折

先听第五乐章，大提琴与钢琴把时间拉得很慢；再听韦伯恩的钢琴变奏曲，同代人在极短篇幅里放进静止；最后听巴托克《在户外》的夜音乐，听夜色与异样音色如何被想象。三种安静，三种对时间的回答。

## Time and place

15 January 1941, at Stalag VIII-A, a prisoner-of-war camp near Görlitz, Silesia. He was held there as a French soldier, and the premiere was in a cold barracks before prisoners and guards.

## What happened

Captured in 1940, he met a violinist, a clarinettist and a cellist in the camp and wrote eight movements for them and piano. The camp had no proper instruments. The cello was missing a string, the piano pedals and keys were unreliable. The work takes Revelation 10 as its frame, with movements titled after birdsong, the abyss and eternity. The fifth movement, Louange à l'Éternité de Jésus, lets the cello sing over long piano chords.

## What changed

Music written under extreme conditions did not need to be grand; damaged instruments could carry a whole sense of time. Messiaen brought his modes of limited transposition and added rhythms into one work, which became his international starting point. Chamber music proved it could be a testimony, not a salon or concert-hall item.

## Listen to the turn

Begin with the fifth movement, where cello and piano stretch time. Then Webern's piano variations, where a contemporary packed stillness into a short span. Finally the night music of Bartók's Out of Doors, where darkness and strange colour are imagined. Three kinds of quiet, three answers to time.
