::: zh
乐团开演前，双簧管常给出一个 A。它听起来像自然法则，其实只是 1939 年国际会议留下的约定；在此之前，欧洲各城市的音高相差很远。

## 问题

音高一致，乐器才能合奏，制造才有统一尺寸。但以谁为准？教堂、宫廷与歌剧院各有传统，管乐器还随温度变化。标准音高既是声学问题，也是权力与市场问题。

## 历史脉络

十八世纪以前，音高由当地教堂或宫廷决定。十九世纪乐队与音乐厅扩大，制造商和指挥把音高往上推，歌手开始抱怨。1859 年法国规定 A=435，1885 年维也纳会议重申。1939 年伦敦会议建议 A=440，战后广播与唱片把它推向全球，1955 年 ISO 立项，1975 年定为 ISO 16。今天柏林、维也纳仍常用 442 或 443，古乐团则回到 415。

## 关键转折

转折不在某个频率被“发现”，而在标准化机构接管了听觉。广播与唱片让一个音高传遍各地，工厂按 440 生产，学院按 440 教学。440 并不比 435 更正确，它只是让合奏与交易更便宜。

## 听什么

先听维瓦尔第《春》，它诞生在没有国际标准的年代；再听贝多芬第九，写于标准未统一的维也纳；继而马勒第九，音高仍在各地游移；最后肖斯塔科维奇第十，战后 440 已随广播铺开。库中 MIDI 按现代十二平均律回放，听的是作品位置，不是历史音高。

::: en
Before an orchestra plays, an oboe often gives an A. It sounds natural, but it is a convention left by a 1939 conference. Before that, pitch varied from city to city.

## The question

Instruments must agree, and makers need fixed sizes. But agree on whose pitch? Church organs, court bands and opera houses had traditions, and winds drift with temperature. A pitch standard is acoustics and power.

## Historical arc

Before 1700, local churches and courts set pitch. As orchestras and halls grew, makers pushed pitch upward, and singers complained. France fixed A=435 in 1859, and Vienna reaffirmed it in 1885. A 1939 London conference recommended A=440; radio and records spread it after the war. ISO took it up in 1955 and set ISO 16 in 1975. Berlin and Vienna still tune to 442 or 443, while early-music groups return to 415.

## The turning point

The shift was not a discovery but a transfer of listening to standards bodies. Radio and records carried one pitch everywhere; factories built to 440 and conservatories taught it. A=440 is not more correct than 435, only cheaper for trade.

## What to listen for

Hear Vivaldi's Spring, born before any international standard; Beethoven's Ninth, written in a Vienna without one; Mahler's Ninth, when pitch still drifted; and Shostakovich's Tenth, in the postwar world of 440.
