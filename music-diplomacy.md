::: zh
乐团出国、钢琴家得奖、国歌在机场响起，音乐常替国家说话。

## 问题

音乐没有台词，为什么能承担外交？它传递的是善意、优越感，还是两者兼有？当艺术家成为国家代表，谁在利用谁？

## 历史脉络

十九世纪苏萨乐队巡演欧洲，铜管进行曲成了美国的名片。冷战把音乐纳入文化竞赛，1955年《波吉与贝丝》巡演，1958年范·克莱本在莫斯科夺冠，1973年费城交响乐团访问中国。1989年柏林墙倒塌后，伯恩斯坦率东西两方乐手演奏《欢乐颂》，把庆典变成外交时刻。

## 关键转折

转折在传播技术与国家资助。广播、唱片与电视让一场演出变成全球事件；政府学会用艺术家绕过外交僵局，艺术家也借国家资源获得舞台。但音乐的和解姿态常掩盖利益计算。

## 听什么

先听柴可夫斯基第一钢琴协奏曲的宏大开场，想象莫斯科赛场；再听贝九《欢乐颂》的合唱，比较柏林墙前后的语气；最后听苏萨《星条旗永不落》的巡演式光彩。

::: en
Orchestras travel and pianists win prizes. Music often speaks for a state.

## The question

Why can wordless music carry diplomacy? Does it offer goodwill, superiority, or both?

## Historical arc

Sousa's band toured Europe in the nineteenth century, and brass marches became an American calling card. The Cold War turned music into cultural competition. Porgy and Bess toured in 1955, Van Cliburn won in Moscow in 1958, and the Philadelphia Orchestra visited China in 1973. In 1989 Bernstein conducted the Ode to Joy with musicians from East and West after the Wall fell, turning a celebration into a diplomatic occasion.

## The turning point

Broadcast technology and state funding changed the scale. Radio, records and television made one concert a global event. Governments used artists to bypass deadlock, and artists used state resources to reach stages.

## What to listen for

Hear the grand opening of Tchaikovsky's First Piano Concerto and imagine the Moscow competition hall. Then compare the choral Ode to Joy before and after the Berlin Wall. Finally take the touring shine of Sousa's Stars and Stripes Forever.
