十八世纪的伦敦出现一种新习惯，听众买票去听几十年前的旧音乐，而不是等着听新作。

## 时间与地点

1726 年，伦敦一家酒馆，贵族业余爱好者成立古乐学会。1776 年，古代音乐会开始以订票方式演出。1784 年，威斯敏斯特教堂举办亨德尔纪念演出，用大型合唱队与乐队。三件事相隔半个世纪，指向同一种场所与同一批曲目。

## 发生了什么

古乐学会的成员抄录并演唱十六、十七世纪的牧歌、经文歌与教堂音乐，用分声部谱册，不用总谱。他们称这些作品为“古代”的，意思是有别于当下的流行风格。曲目以牧歌与教堂音乐为主，当时的意大利歌剧很少进入节目，这个取舍本身就是立场。旧作被当作需要复原的对象，而不是可以随手改写的素材。职业歌手与业余绅士同台，排练在酒馆楼上，入会要交年费，演出季按周排定。亨德尔 1759 年去世后，他的清唱剧也迅速变成古代曲目，纪念演出把合唱队与乐队扩到很大。出版商开始翻印旧乐谱，私人藏书与抄谱成为风气，学会的抄本后来成了编订者的依据。

## 改变了什么

“过去”第一次成为可重复消费的曲目库，节目单分成古代与现代两部分。演奏旧乐需要编辑、校勘与考据，音乐学的前身由此出现。经典的概念成形，帕莱斯特里那与亨德尔被列为大师，新作曲家有意识地在他们之后写作。听音乐也多了历史维度，观众开始关心一首作品原本是什么样子。

## 听这个转折

先听拉索的《夫人，请听我说》，轻薄的五声部织体，句子跟着歌词走，这是古乐学会抄进谱册的那类曲子。再听伯德的《求主怜悯》，调式终止与长句，英国旧教的声音。最后听亨德尔的《祭司撒督》，加冕典礼的管弦与合唱，它比前两首晚一百多年，却被同一套古代机制收编。三首的差别，就是“旧”从风格描述变成历史标签的过程。转折前的音乐会以新作为主，旧曲只是穿插；转折后，旧曲单独成场，观众带着年代感去听。

Eighteenth-century London acquired a new habit: listeners bought tickets for music decades old instead of waiting for the new.

## Time and place

London, 1726, where noble amateurs founded the Academy of Ancient Music in a tavern. In 1776 the Concert of Ancient Music began selling subscriptions. In 1784 Westminster Abbey held a Handel commemoration with a large choir and orchestra. Half a century separates the three, yet they point to one kind of venue and one body of repertory.

## What happened

Members of the Academy copied and sang sixteenth- and seventeenth-century madrigals, motets and church music from partbooks, without a full score. They called these works ancient, meaning distinct from the fashionable style of the day. Madrigals and church music dominated, Italian opera of the moment rarely appeared, and that choice was itself a position; an old work was treated as something to recover rather than rewrite. Professional singers shared the platform with amateur gentlemen; rehearsals took place above a tavern, membership carried an annual fee, and seasons ran week by week. After Handel died in 1759 his oratorios quickly became ancient too, and commemorations expanded choir and orchestra. Publishers began reprinting old music, private collecting and copying became a fashion, and the Academy's copies later served editors as sources.

## What changed

The past became a repertory that could be consumed again and again, and programmes split into ancient and modern halves. Performing old music required editing, collation and research, and the ancestor of musicology appeared. The idea of a canon took shape, with Palestrina and Handel installed as masters and new composers writing consciously after them. Listening gained a historical dimension, as audiences began to care what a work had originally been.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Lasso's Matona mia cara, a light five-voice texture whose phrases follow the words, the kind of piece the Academy copied into its partbooks. Then Byrd's Miserere mei Deus, with modal cadences and long lines, the sound of English recusant polyphony. Finally Handel's Zadok the Priest, a coronation piece for orchestra and choir, more than a century younger yet absorbed by the same machinery of the ancient. The difference between them is how old stopped being a description of style and became a historical label. Before the turn, concerts were mostly new music with an old piece slipped in; afterwards the old had evenings of its own, and audiences listened with a sense of period.
