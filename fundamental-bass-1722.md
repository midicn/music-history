::: zh
数字低音告诉你手该按什么，却不告诉你为什么。拉摩把每一个和弦归到一个根音，再让根音按规则移动，和声从此有了自己的语法。

## 年代与城市

1722年，巴黎。拉摩出版《和声学》，书名说要把和声化约为自然原则。他此前以键盘作曲家与管风琴师为生，此后逐渐转向理论，十八世纪四十年代又与卢梭就旋律与和声谁更重要展开争论。

## 当时发生了什么

拉摩提出基础低音的概念，认为听到的低声部只是表面，真正的低音是和弦的根音，转位只是同一个和弦换了低音。由此，七和弦与各种转位被归入少数类型，和弦之间按五度、三度与级进移动，形成可以预测的进行。他用弦长的比例与泛音来解释协和，把和声的合法性挂到自然上。这套体系把数字低音从演奏工具降为记谱方式，把和弦本身立为分析对象。

## 后来变了什么

和声成为一门独立学科，教学从在琴上补声部转向在纸上分析功能。作曲家可以用功能描述段落，理论家后来发展出罗马数字标记，调性内部的紧张与解决有了说法。拉摩与卢梭的争论也定下了此后两百年的一个话题，旋律与和声谁更根本。数字低音没有消失，但它的地位从作曲训练的核心变成伴奏与实现的技能。

## 听这一刻

先听拉摩 e 小调组曲，舞曲的低音线清楚，能听出根音按五度与级进移动。再听《Les Sauvages》，低音反复同一进行，旋律在上面变化，根音与旋律的分工一目了然。最后听《La poule》，模仿母鸡的动机架在极简单的和声上，装饰与和声各管一层。听的时候把低音唱出来，再听上方和弦换了几次，拉摩说的基础低音就在这两层之间。

Figured bass told the hands what to play but not why. Rameau assigned every chord to a root and made the roots move by rule, and harmony acquired a grammar of its own.

## Year and city

Paris, 1722. Rameau published the Traite de l'harmonie, whose title promised to reduce harmony to natural principles. He had earned his living as a keyboard composer and organist, turned increasingly to theory, and in the 1750s argued with Rousseau over whether melody or harmony mattered more.

## What happened then

Rameau proposed the fundamental bass. The bass you hear, he argued, is only a surface; the true bass is the root of the chord, and an inversion is the same chord with another note underneath. Seventh chords and their inversions were thus sorted into a few types, and chords moved by fifths, thirds or step to form progressions that could be predicted. He explained consonance through string lengths and the harmonic series, tying the legitimacy of harmony to nature. The system demoted figured bass from a tool of performance to a way of writing, and made the chord itself the object of analysis.

## What changed after

Harmony became an independent discipline, and teaching moved from filling in voices at the keyboard to analysing functions on paper. Composers could describe a passage by function, theorists later developed Roman numerals, and tension and resolution inside a key acquired a vocabulary. The quarrel with Rousseau also fixed a question that lasted two centuries, whether melody or harmony is primary. Figured bass did not vanish, but it fell from the centre of composition training to a skill of accompaniment and realisation.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with Rameau's Suite in E minor, where the dance basses are clear enough to follow roots moving by fifth and by step. Then Les Sauvages, whose bass repeats one progression while the melody changes above it, the division between root and tune laid bare. Finally La poule, a hen-calling motive over very simple harmony, ornament and harmony each on their own level. Sing the bass as you listen, then count how often the chord above it changes; Rameau's fundamental bass lives between those two layers.
