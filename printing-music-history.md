## 问题

手抄本时代，一部复调作品要由抄写员逐份复制，价格高、错误多，传播限于教堂与宫廷。印刷要解决复制速度与一致性，一旦谱面可以批量生产，音乐就进入市场。

## 历史脉络

1450年前后，活字印刷在欧洲成熟。1501年，佩特鲁奇在威尼斯出版《音乐谐调百歌集》，用三次印刷叠出谱线、音符与歌词。1520年代，阿泰尼昂在巴黎改用一次印刷，成本下降，尚松与舞曲大量流出。十六世纪，苏萨托在安特卫普印舞曲集；十七世纪，普莱福德在伦敦印舞曲手册，把乡村舞曲变成城市读物。

## 关键转折

第一次转折是一次印刷，谱线、音符、歌词同版压印，印量上去，价格下来，业余演奏者成为市场。第二次转折是版权，印刷特权与出版合同出现，作曲家能主张作品归属。第三次转折是教育，廉价谱让音乐学院、家庭与业余乐团共享同一套曲目。

## 听什么

先听佩特鲁奇时代的尚松，四声部被排在同一页上，买回家就能唱；再听阿泰尼昂的舞曲，节奏清楚、和弦重复，为业余演奏而写；最后听苏萨托的舞曲集，同一旋律可换乐器与场合。库中版本是合成音色，注意印本如何简化织体。

## The question

In the manuscript age, a polyphonic work had to be copied by hand, one copy at a time. Prices were high, errors multiplied, and circulation stayed close to churches and courts. Printing had to solve speed and consistency, and once pages could be produced in quantity music entered a market.

## Historical arc

Movable type matured in Europe around 1450. In 1501 Petrucci published the Harmonice musices odhecaton in Venice, printing staff lines, notes and text in three passes. In the 1520s Attaingnant moved to single-impression printing in Paris, cut costs and released chansons and dances in quantity. Susato printed dance collections in Antwerp, Praetorius compiled large sacred collections in Germany, and Playford turned country dances into household reading in seventeenth-century London.

## The turning point

The first turn was single-impression printing. Staff, notes and text were pressed at once, runs grew, prices fell, and amateur players became a market. The second turn was copyright. Printing privileges and publishing contracts appeared, and composers gained clearer rights to their work. The third turn was education. Cheap scores gave conservatories, homes and amateur ensembles a common repertory.

## What to listen for

Begin with a chanson from Petrucci's circle, its four voices set on one page and ready to sing at home. Then hear an Attaingnant dance, with clear rhythm and repeated chords plainly written for amateurs. Finally hear a Susato dance collection, where one tune can change instruments and occasions. The library versions are synthesised, so notice how print simplified or fixed the texture.
