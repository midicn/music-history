::: zh
五个音能走多远？从中国编钟到苏格兰舞曲，五声音阶一直在旅行。

## 问题

五声音阶是自然规律，还是文化选择？为什么缺两个音反而能跨文化通行？

## 历史脉络

中国古代以宫商角徵羽配五行，十二律则由竹管与钟磬的测算逐步形成。五声并非中国独有，凯尔特、非洲与美国原住民音乐也有五声框架。十九世纪民族乐派把五声当作民族标记，二十世纪作曲家又用它制造异域感或简化语言。理论家用五度相生解释其生成，但实际音乐常混合三声、四声与七声。

## 关键转折

转折在解释权。五声先被当作宇宙秩序，后被当作民族特征，再被当作可借用的色彩。每一次定义都缩小了它的实际用法。

## 听什么

先听《茉莉花》的级进与跳进，再听塔雷加《五声前奏曲》的吉他分解，最后听苏格兰五声吉格的舞步，比较同一音阶的不同口音。

::: en
How far can five notes travel? From Chinese bells to Scottish dances, the pentatonic scale has been on the move.

## The question

Is the pentatonic scale a law of nature or a cultural choice? Why does the absence of two notes let it travel so widely?

## Historical arc

Ancient China linked the five tones gong, shang, jiao, zhi and yu to the five elements, while the twelve pitches took shape through bamboo pipes and bells. The frame is not Chinese alone; Celtic, African and Native American musics also use it. Nineteenth-century national schools made it a marker, and twentieth-century composers used it for exotic colour or simplified language. Theorists explain it by the cycle of fifths, but real music often mixes three, four and seven notes.

## The turning point

The turn concerns who explains the scale. Five notes were first a cosmic order, then a national trait, then a borrowable colour. Each definition narrowed its use.

## What to listen for

Hear the steps and leaps of Jasmine Flower, the guitar arpeggios of Tárrega's Preludio pentatonica, and a Scottish pentatonic jig.
