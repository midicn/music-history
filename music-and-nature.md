## 要回答的问题

自然本身没有旋律，鸟叫也不是十二平均律。作曲家凭什么说自己写的是自然？他们写的其实是听自然的方式。

## 脉络

文艺复兴牧歌用牧羊人布景，巴洛克用暴风雨与鸟鸣的固定音型；19世纪浪漫主义把自然当成内心投射，田园交响曲与森林小品大量出现；20世纪录音技术把真实自然声带进作品，环境音乐又把它变成背景。自然在这里既是对象，也是镜子。

## 几个例子

维瓦尔第《冬》以弦乐震音与十六分音符写寒风；贝多芬《田园》的钢琴改编保留溪流与鸟鸣的段落；舒曼《森林情景》的《入林》用低音与回声制造树林的纵深。三首从天气、风景走到心理空间。

## 听这几处

听模仿鸟鸣的高音是不是短促而重复，听低音是否像脚步或树影，听风暴过后有没有突然的静。

## Question

Nature has no melody, and a bird does not sing in equal temperament. What gives a composer the right to call a piece natural? What is written is a way of hearing nature, not nature itself.

## Historical background

Renaissance madrigals staged shepherds, and Baroque music used stock figures for storms and birds. Romanticism made landscape a mirror of the self, producing pastoral symphonies and forest pieces. In the twentieth century, recording brought actual natural sound into music, and ambient styles turned it into background.

## A few examples

Vivaldi's Winter uses tremolo strings and rapid figures for cold wind. The piano reduction of Beethoven's Pastoral keeps the brook and bird calls, and Schumann's Entering the Forest builds depth from low steps and echoes. The three move from weather to landscape to inner space.

## Listen to these

Hear whether bird calls are short and repeated in the high register, whether the bass walks like footsteps or shadows, and whether silence follows the storm.
