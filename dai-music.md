## 时间与地点

傣族聚居在云南西双版纳、德宏，临沧与普洱也有。西双版纳以景洪、勐海、勐腊为中心，德宏以瑞丽、芒市、遮放为中心。村寨有佛寺，泼水节在傣历新年，赞哈在佛寺庆典、婚礼、贺新房与节庆上唱。傣语属侗台语族，方言分西双版纳傣泐与德宏傣那。

## 发生了什么

赞哈是职业或半职业的叙事歌手，师徒相传，唱本包括佛本生故事、地方传说与即兴祝词。过去赞哈在土司与佛寺的赞助下演出，二十世纪后期进入歌舞团与旅游舞台，也仍在村寨的仪式里。葫芦丝在1953年采风时被记录为现在的名字，此后从德宏传遍全国。象脚鼓旧时用于佛寺法事，后来成为孔雀舞的领舞鼓。具体唱本的年代与作者大多不可考，这里只述及通行的传承方式。

## 音乐特征

赞哈的旋律窄，常在一个五度内回绕，句尾下滑，带鼻音。唱词按傣语声调行腔，长短句交替，伴奏用玎或筚，有时清唱。民歌分山歌、情歌、劳动歌与摇篮曲，情歌多用真假声转换。赞哈的唱本有长有短，婚礼与贺新房多唱祝福段。赞哈的唱词讲究押韵，句尾常以套语收束。葫芦丝主管旋律，两根副管发持续音；象脚鼓以手掌与指节击出高低音，铓锣与镲稳住拍点。

## 听什么

先听赞哈的句尾怎样下滑，再听伴奏在句间留出的空隙。essen-008778 是德宏遮放的情歌，音域不宽，可听傣语声调与旋律的贴合。chinafolk-008417 与 chinafolk-001217 是与象脚鼓、歌舞有关的曲调，可听鼓点怎样领舞。essen-000035 是云南汉族山歌，只作地域参照。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把葫芦丝当成傣族唯一的乐器，或把傣族音乐都听成轻柔的舞曲。赞哈是叙事传统，佛寺唱诵与情歌各有场合；葫芦丝也为阿昌、佤、德昂、布朗等族所用。傣族音乐不是为游客准备的表演，它仍嵌在村寨的仪式与农事里。

## Time and place

The Dai live in Xishuangbanna and Dehong in Yunnan, with communities in Lincang and Pu'er. Jinghong, Menghai and Mengla form the Xishuangbanna centre; Ruili, Mangshi and Zhefang the Dehong centre. Villages have Buddhist temples, the water festival marks the Dai new year, and zanha singers perform at temple festivals, weddings, house blessings and seasonal rites. The Dai languages belong to the Kra-Dai family, with Tai Lue in Xishuangbanna and Tai Nuea in Dehong.

## What happened

Zanha is a professional or semi-professional narrative singer, trained through apprenticeship. Repertoire includes Jataka stories, local legends and improvised blessings. Patronage once came from local rulers and temples; from the late twentieth century zanha moved into song-and-dance troupes and tourism, while continuing in village rites. The hulusi was recorded under its present Chinese name during a 1953 collecting trip and later spread nationwide from Dehong. The elephant-foot drum once served temple ritual and later led peacock dances. The dates and authors of individual sung texts are mostly untraceable, so this entry describes transmission rather than a fixed canon.

## Musical features

Zanha melody is narrow, circling within about a fifth, with falling phrase ends and a nasal tone. Words follow Dai speech tones in long and short phrases, accompanied by the ding lute or the bi reed pipe, sometimes unaccompanied. Folk songs divide into mountain songs, love songs, work songs and lullabies; love songs shift between chest and falsetto. The hulusi carries the melody over two drone pipes, while the elephant-foot drum produces high and low strokes with palm and fingers, steadied by gongs and cymbals.

## What to listen for

Hear how a zanha phrase falls at the end, then listen for the gaps the accompaniment leaves between phrases. Essen-008778 is a love song from Zhefang in Dehong, narrow in range, where Dai speech tone and melody meet. Chinafolk-008417 and chinafolk-001217 are tunes connected with the drum and dance; listen for how the drum leads the steps. Essen-000035 is a Han mountain song from Yunnan, a regional reference only.

## Common misunderstandings

The hulusi is often taken as the Dai instrument, and all Dai music as soft dance music. Zanha is a narrative tradition, temple chant and love songs have separate occasions, and the hulusi is also played by Achang, Wa, Deang and Blang musicians. Dai music is not staged for visitors alone; it still belongs to village ritual and farm work.
