## 时间与地点

三分损益法见于先秦至汉的文献，《管子·地员》用它求五音，《吕氏春秋·音律》与《史记·律书》推到十二律。计算以律管长度为依据，在宫廷与乐官体系中传承。

## 音乐特征

以黄钟管长为准，三分损一取其三分之二，得上方五度；三分益一取其三分之四，得下方四度。两法交替，依次生出林钟、太簇、南吕、姑洗、应钟、蕤宾、大吕、夷则、夹钟、无射、仲吕。生到十二次时，仲吕不能正好回到黄钟，留下古代律学的核心难题。

## 代表曲目

三分损益是生律方法，没有专属曲目。传统五声、七声旋律的骨干音常以五度相生关系组织，可借民歌听这种关系。

## 听什么

先听《茉莉花》《紫竹调》《蓝花花》，找旋律中的四度、五度跳进与句尾落音，再想这些音怎样由一条五度链生成。注意五声旋律并不等于只用五个音，偏音与转调会在段落中出现。

## 常见误解

常有人把三分损益等同于十二平均律，或以为五声音阶只有五个音。三分损益生出的十二律彼此不等，仲吕与黄钟之间有差距；五声是骨干，实际音乐常用六声、七声与旋宫。

## Time and place

Sanfen sunyi appears in texts from the pre-Qin to the Han. The Guanzi uses it to derive five tones, while the Lüshi Chunqiu and the Shiji extend it to twelve. It works from the length of pitch pipes and was transmitted through court and music-official circles.

## Musical features

Taking the Huangzhong pipe as the base, sanfen sunyi removes one third to obtain the upper fifth, then adds one third to obtain the lower fourth. Alternating the two operations yields Linzhong, Taicu, Nanlü, Guxi, Yingzhong, Ruibin, Dalü, Yize, Jiazhong, Wuyi and Zhonglü. After twelve steps, Zhonglü cannot return exactly to Huangzhong, leaving the central puzzle of ancient tuning theory.

## Repertoire

Sanfen sunyi is a method of generating pitches, not a repertoire. The structural notes of traditional pentatonic and heptatonic melodies are often organised by fifth relations, and folk song lets one hear that.

## What to listen for

Hear Jasmine Flower, Purple Bamboo Melody and Blue Flower first, finding fourth and fifth leaps and phrase-ending notes, then imagine those notes generated by a chain of fifths. A pentatonic melody does not use only five pitches; passing tones and modulations appear in performance.

## Common misunderstandings

Sanfen sunyi is often equated with twelve-tone equal temperament, or the pentatonic scale assumed to have only five notes. Its twelve pitches are unequal, and a gap separates Zhonglü from Huangzhong; five tones are a frame, while real music uses six or seven and changes mode.
