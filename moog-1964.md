::: zh
1964年，罗伯特·穆格做出电压控制的模块合成器。振荡器、滤波器与包络能用键盘稳定演奏，电子声音走进录音棚。

## 年份与坐标

1964年，纽约州杜鲁门斯堡。穆格靠特雷门琴套件谋生，1963年与赫布·多伊奇搭出原型。1968年卡洛斯用穆格录制《Switched-On Bach》，1970年 Minimoog 上市。

## 当时的场面

穆格的突破是电压控制：音高、滤波与包络都用电压调节，键盘、序列器与调制源可以互相连接。演奏者用跳线决定信号走向，一台合成器成为可重排的电子乐器。卡洛斯逐轨录制巴赫对位，再在磁带上叠成合奏。

## 之后不一样了

合成器从此可以独奏、巡演、进流行乐队。音色开始被设计，而不再只是从现成音色里挑选；流行唱片里的低音、主奏与效果声越来越多来自振荡器。巴赫改编把古典曲目带进电子音乐，也让合成器进入古典听众的视野。

::: en
In 1964 Robert Moog built a voltage-controlled modular synthesizer. Oscillators, filters and envelopes could be played steadily from a keyboard, and electronic sound entered the studio.

## Year and coordinates

Trumansburg, New York, 1964. Moog made a living from theremin kits, and with the composer Herb Deutsch he assembled a prototype in 1963. In 1968 Wendy Carlos recorded Switched-On Bach on a Moog, and in 1970 the portable Minimoog arrived.

## The scene at the time

Moog's breakthrough was voltage control: pitch, filter cutoff and envelope times were set by voltages, so keyboards, sequencers and modulation sources could be patched together. The player chose the signal path with cables, and one synthesizer became a reconfigurable instrument. Carlos recorded Bach's counterpoint one line at a time and stacked the tracks on tape.

## What was different after

The synthesizer could now solo, tour and join a pop band. Timbre became something to design rather than select, and bass lines, leads and effects on pop records increasingly came from oscillators. Bach arrangements carried classical repertoire into electronic music and into classical listeners' homes.
