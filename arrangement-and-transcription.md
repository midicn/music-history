## 问题

一首曲子换一件乐器、换一个编制，还是同一首吗？改编让作品旅行，也让原作变形。听者该把改编当作翻译、复制，还是新的创作？

## 历史脉络

文艺复兴的弥撒常借用现成旋律，巴洛克把协奏曲改成键盘曲。19世纪出版商需要家庭版，李斯特把交响曲与歌剧缩成钢琴谱，观众不必进音乐厅也能“拥有”大作。20世纪广播与电影需要配乐改编，拉威尔把穆索尔斯基的钢琴组曲配器，斯特拉文斯基又重写旧作。改编始终在传播与作者权之间拉扯。

## 关键案例

穆索尔斯基《图画展览会》的“漫步”原为钢琴，拉威尔的配器让同一旋律获得管弦色彩；李斯特把贝多芬第五改成钢琴独奏，检验一台琴能否承担交响；布拉斯把巴赫《d小调托卡塔与赋格》改成钢琴曲，让管风琴语言进入客厅。

## 听什么

听“漫步”的旋律在钢琴与乐队中谁更突出；听李斯特版第五如何用琶音补上缺失的低音；听布拉斯版托卡塔的踏板与八度是否在模仿管风琴。

## The question

If a piece changes instrument or ensemble, is it still the same? Arrangement lets a work travel and makes the original change. Should a listener hear it as translation, copy or new creation?

## Historical arc

Renaissance masses borrowed existing melodies, and Baroque concertos were reset for keyboard. Nineteenth-century publishers needed domestic versions, so Liszt reduced symphonies and operas to piano scores and households could own a masterpiece without entering a hall. Radio and film needed arrangements in the twentieth century. Ravel orchestrated Mussorgsky's piano suite, and Stravinsky reworked his own older music. Arrangement pulls between circulation and authorship.

## Key cases

The Promenade from Pictures at an Exhibition began as piano music, and Ravel's orchestration gave the same tune orchestral colour. Liszt turned Beethoven's Fifth into a piano solo, testing whether one instrument could carry a symphony. Brassin's piano version of Bach's D minor Toccata and Fugue brought organ language into the parlour.

## What to listen for

Hear whether the Promenade melody stands out more clearly on piano or in the orchestra. Hear how Liszt's Fifth uses arpeggios to replace missing bass. Hear whether the pedals and octaves of Brassin's Toccata imitate an organ.
