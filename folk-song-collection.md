::: zh
民歌原本在田间、酒馆与仪式里，采集者把它带到纸面与舞台。

## 问题

谁拥有民歌？记录、改编与版权如何改变它？采集者是保存者，还是把集体记忆变成个人作品的人？

## 历史脉络

赫尔德与格林兄弟把民歌当作民族记忆。十九世纪采集者用记谱保存旋律，二十世纪录音机让声音进入档案。巴托克与柯达伊在东欧村庄录音，再把素材写成钢琴曲与合唱。沃恩·威廉斯编《英国民歌组曲》，格里格整理挪威民歌，格兰杰把民谣改成炫技钢琴曲。中国在二十世纪也组织大规模采风。

## 关键转折

转折在媒介。记谱把流动的变体固定成一个版本，录音又保存了呼吸与方言；版权与改编把集体遗产变成作者作品。采集既拯救也占有。

## 听什么

先听巴托克罗马尼亚舞曲的节拍，再听格里格挪威民歌的钢琴化，最后听沃恩·威廉斯组曲里的民歌主题，注意配器如何改变乡土气。

::: en
Folk song lived in fields, taverns and rituals. Collectors carried it onto paper and into the concert hall.

## The question

Who owns a folk song? What do notation, arrangement and copyright do to it? Is the collector a preserver, or someone who turns collective memory into a personal work?

## Historical arc

Herder and the Brothers Grimm treated folk song as national memory. Nineteenth-century collectors used notation; twentieth-century recorders put voices into archives. Bartók and Kodály recorded in villages, then turned the material into piano pieces and choruses. Vaughan Williams arranged the English Folk Song Suite, Grieg edited Norwegian tunes, and Grainger made folk ballads into brilliant piano music. China also organised large collecting campaigns.

## The turning point

The medium was decisive. Notation fixed a flowing variant into one version, while recording preserved breath and dialect. Copyright and arrangement turned collective inheritance into authored work. Collecting saved and possessed at once.

## What to listen for

Hear the beat of Bartók's Romanian dance, Grieg's piano setting of a Norwegian tune, and the folk theme in Vaughan Williams's suite.
