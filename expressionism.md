::: zh
表现主义音乐是二十世纪初维也纳的写作方式，把内心焦虑、恐惧与痉挛直接写进和声与音色。它不描摹外部世界，而把主观状态推到前台。

## 起源与年代

1908 年前后，勋伯格在《三首钢琴小品》与《六首钢琴小品》里放弃调性中心。贝尔格与韦伯恩把这种语言写得更极端。第一次世界大战前后，绘画、诗歌与音乐共享同一股焦虑。1920 年代，十二音技法为它提供了秩序。

## 听辨特征

听不到主音回家，和弦不解决。旋律大幅跳进，音域被拉到两端。力度突变，演奏法尖锐，加弱音器、靠近琴桥。作品常很短，一个乐思说完就走。人声用念唱，介于说与唱之间。

## 代表人物

勋伯格、贝尔格、韦伯恩。贝尔格《沃采克》把表现主义带进歌剧，韦伯恩把篇幅压缩到几十秒。

## 谱系

上承瓦格纳的半音阶与理查·施特劳斯的配器，下接十二音与整体序列，也影响恐怖片配乐与自由爵士。

## 常见误解

表现主义不等于噪音，也不等于没有情感。它未必很响，很多段落极轻。无调性也不是随便写，音程关系仍被严格控制。

::: en
Expressionism was a Viennese way of writing in the early twentieth century that put anxiety, fear and spasm directly into harmony and timbre. It did not picture the outer world; it pushed a subjective state to the front.

## Origins and dates

Around 1908 Schoenberg gave up a tonal centre in the Three Piano Pieces and the Six Little Piano Pieces. Berg and Webern pushed the language further. Before and after the First World War, painting, poetry and music shared the same unease. In the 1920s the twelve-tone method gave it an order.

## Listening features

The tonic never comes home and chords do not resolve. Melodies leap widely and the range is pulled to both extremes. Dynamics change abruptly, playing is sharp, with mutes or bowing near the bridge. Works are often very short, one idea and gone. The voice uses Sprechstimme, halfway between speech and song.

## Composers and works

Schoenberg, Berg and Webern. Berg's Wozzeck carried expressionism into opera; Webern compressed it into seconds.

## Lineage

It grows from Wagner's chromaticism and Strauss's orchestration and leads to twelve-tone and total serialism, and it reaches horror-film scores and free jazz.

## Misconceptions

Expressionism is not noise, nor is it emotionless. It is not always loud; many passages are extremely quiet. Atonality is not writing anything at random, since intervals are still strictly controlled.
