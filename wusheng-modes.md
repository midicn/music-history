## 时间与地点

五声体系先秦已见于编钟与文献，宫商角徵羽的名目出自《国语》《管子》一系。它不只属于古代，各地的民歌、戏曲、器乐至今仍在用。同一条五声骨架，换一个主音，色彩就变了。

## 音乐特征

宫调以 do 为主，沉稳开阔；商调带一点紧张；角调游移，常作过渡；徵调明亮上扬；羽调偏暗，句尾下沉。偏音清角与变宫多作经过，不改变骨架。调式由句尾落音与主音反复决定，旋法常用三音列与四度跳进。

## 代表曲目

《茉莉花》《小白菜》《小河淌水》《春江花月夜》都以五声为骨，落音不同，明暗也不同。

## 听什么

先找每句的落音，再听主音在句中怎样被反复强调。偏音出现的位置往往就是色彩的转折点，短促一现又回到五声。落音一变，同样的旋律就换了表情。

## 常见误解

常见误解是以为五声调式只有五个音，或以为它中国独有。五声是骨架，偏音可以加入；苏格兰、日本、非洲也有五声旋律。一首曲子也可以中途换主音。

## Time and place

The pentatonic system appears in bronze bells and texts by the pre-Qin period; the names gong, shang, jue, zhi and yu come from the Guoyu and Guanzi tradition. It is not only ancient: folk songs, opera and instrumental music still use it. Keep the same five notes and change the tonic, and the colour changes.

## Musical features

Gong, on do, is steady and open; shang carries a little tension; jue is unstable and often transitional; zhi is bright and rising; yu is darker, with phrases sinking at the close. The pien tones qingjiao and biangong usually pass by without disturbing the frame. Mode is decided by the final note and the repeated tonic, and the lines often move in three-note cells and fourths.

## Repertoire

Molihua, Xiao Baicai, Xiaohe Tangshui and Spring River in the Flower Moon Night are all built on five notes, but their different finals give different light and shade.

## What to listen for

Find the final note of each phrase, then hear how the tonic is pressed again inside the line. Pien tones often mark the turning point of colour, flashing briefly before the five notes return.

## Common misunderstandings

A common error is to think a pentatonic mode uses only five notes, or that it is uniquely Chinese. The five notes are a frame to which passing tones may be added; Scottish, Japanese and African music also use pentatonic lines. A piece may even change tonic midway.
