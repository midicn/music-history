## 年份与坐标

1889 年，伦敦。皇家音乐学院与皇家音乐专科学院联合成立考试委员会，次年在英伦三岛与殖民地举行分级考试。

## 事情经过

委员会把钢琴、小提琴、声乐与理论拆成八级，每级规定音阶、练习曲、曲目与视奏。考官从伦敦派往各地，用同一套标准评分。通过者获证书，高级别可衔接教学文凭。教材由出版社发行，曲目几年一换。

## 留下的变化

音乐学习有了统一进度表，家长与教师可以比较程度，乐器教学从口传变成可考核的课程。考试曲目带动乐谱销售，出版社按级别编选曲集，巴赫《小前奏曲》、克莱门蒂《小奏鸣曲》成为几代琴童的共同记忆。分级体系随英语教育扩散到澳大利亚、加拿大、东南亚与中国香港，至今仍是规模最大的考级机构之一。

## 听一段转折

巴赫《三部创意曲》第十首是常见的五六级曲目，听三个声部怎样各走各的；克莱门蒂《六首小奏鸣曲》作品 36 是初级曲库常客，听阿尔贝蒂低音与右手音阶；库劳《C 大调小奏鸣曲》作品 20 之 1 同样在初级曲目里，听两个乐章的对比。三首都来自考试与教学曲库。

## Year and coordinates

London, 1889. The Royal Academy and the Royal College founded the Associated Board, which held its first graded exams in 1890 across Britain and the colonies.

## The sequence of events

The board divided piano, violin, singing and theory into eight grades, each with scales, studies, set pieces and sight-reading. Examiners travelled from London and marked to one standard. Passes brought certificates, and upper grades led toward teaching diplomas.

## What it left behind

Music study acquired a national ladder, and instrumental teaching turned from apprenticeship into a course that could be examined. Set pieces drove sheet-music sales, publishers compiled graded albums, and Bach's Little Preludes and Clementi's Sonatinas became shared memories for generations of children. The system followed English-language education to Australia, Canada, Southeast Asia and Hong Kong, China, and remains a large exam board.

## Hear the turn

Bach's Three-part Invention No. 10 is a common grade five or six piece; hear the three voices keep their own paths. Clementi's Six Sonatinas Op. 36 are early-grade staples, with Alberti bass under a running right hand. Kuhlau's Sonatina in C, Op. 20 No. 1 also sits in the early books.
