## 时间与地点

散板是板眼体系里的自由节拍，明清戏曲与器乐中已普遍使用。它出现在全国各剧种的唱段里，也见于山歌、长调、古琴与吹打的散起段落。戏曲中的导板、哭头，往往就是散板性质。

## 音乐特征

散板无板无眼，不划均分拍子，时值靠句读、气口与重音决定。记谱只写大致长短，实际演唱演奏由表演者掌握。散板常作引子，散起之后转入慢板或原板，也可以在段落结束时散收。它与有板段落形成松紧对比。

## 代表曲目

京剧的散板唱段、秦腔的散板、陕北的信天游、内蒙古的长调，都以自由节拍见长。古琴曲多在散起中定下全曲气息。

## 听什么

听呼吸在哪里断，句尾在哪里落。散板不是随便拖，句子有内在的轻重；几件乐器合奏时，听它们怎样在无拍的情况下一起换句。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把散板当成没有节奏或随意处理。它只是不划均分拍，句法仍严格；也不是只用于悲情，叙事、写景、开场都用得上。

## Time and place

Sanban is free time within the banyan system of metre, already common in Ming and Qing opera and instrumental music. It appears in arias across the country and in the free openings of mountain songs, long song, guqin pieces and wind-and-percussion suites. The daoban and kutou of opera are usually sanban in character.

## Musical features

Sanban has no ban and no yan, no evenly divided beat. Duration is decided by phrasing, breath and accent. Notation records only approximate lengths; the performer controls the rest. It often serves as an introduction, moving from free time into slow or original metre, and can close a section in the same loose way. Its tension lies in the contrast with measured passages.

## Repertoire

Free-time passages in Peking opera and Qin opera, northern Shaanxi xintianyou and Mongolian long song all rely on it. Many guqin pieces set the breath of the whole work in a free opening.

## What to listen for

Listen for where the breath breaks and where each phrase lands. Sanban is not random lingering; the sentence has its own weight. When several instruments play together, hear how they change phrases without a beat.

## Common misunderstandings

Sanban is often taken as music without rhythm, or as arbitrary handling. It merely avoids an even beat; the phrasing remains strict. Nor is it reserved for sadness; narration, landscape and openings all use it.
