::: zh
末日金属把布鲁斯摇滚降到接近静止的速度，用三全音、降调吉他音色与长段反复，1970 年前后在伯明翰成形。

## 起源与年代
1968 年，Black Sabbath 在伯明翰组队，1970 年 2 月发行首张专辑，标题曲以三全音开场，速度远慢于同期摇滚。1980 年代，芝加哥的 Trouble、洛杉矶的 Saint Vitus 与斯德哥尔摩的 Candlemass 把这条路线整理成独立场景。1990 年代，Sleep 与 Electric Wizard 加入石人摇滚与迷幻元素，速度更慢、段落更长。

## 听辨特征
速度常落在 50 到 70 BPM，吉他降调，常降到 C 标准或更低。riff 以两三个音的反复为主，三全音是标志性音程，失真厚而钝。鼓按半速感走，军鼓落在三拍或略微拖后，镲片用得节省。人声在嘶吼与清唱之间切换，段落之间插入清音吉他或管风琴式的持续音。录音强调低频与混响，听感像在一个很大的空间里，音符之间留出空气。吉他手在强力和弦里加一个高八度或五度，让失真更饱满；贝斯跟着最低音走，几乎与吉他同一条线。段落之间常留一小节近乎静止的拍子，再重新落下。这种静止让下一次落下显得更重。

## 代表人物
Black Sabbath 的 Tony Iommi 用降调与三全音写下模板，Ozzy Osbourne 的嗓音把旋律留在重压之上。Candlemass 的 Messiah Marcolin 用接近歌剧的唱腔，Trouble 把宗教意象带进这一类型，Sleep 则用长段重复把时间拉长。Electric Wizard 把失真与迷幻噪声推到更极端的位置。

## 谱系
上承布鲁斯摇滚、迷幻摇滚与 Black Sabbath 的慢速段落，下接石人摇滚、葬礼末日与后金属。它让“慢”成为一种结构手段，而不只是速度选择，也把音色与音量当作作曲材料。

## 常见误解
常见说法是 Black Sabbath 自称末日金属，其实这一标签到 1980 年代才被乐迷与杂志固定下来。另一个误解是末日金属只有慢速翻版，实际上葬礼末日、石人与后金属在编制、音色和曲长上差别很大。把它当成金属里最吵闹的一支，也低估了它大量清音与留白。

::: en
Doom metal slows blues rock to a near standstill and works with tritones, down-tuned guitars and long repetition, forming around Birmingham in 1970.

## Origins and era
Black Sabbath formed in Birmingham in 1968 and released their debut in February 1970; its title track opens on a tritone and moves far slower than the rock of the day. In the 1980s, Trouble in Chicago, Saint Vitus in Los Angeles and Candlemass in Stockholm turned the approach into distinct scenes. In the 1990s Sleep and Electric Wizard added stoner rock and psychedelic elements, with slower tempos and longer sections.

## What you hear
Tempos usually sit between 50 and 70 BPM, guitars are tuned down, often to C standard or lower. Riffs repeat two or three notes, the tritone is the signature interval, and distortion is thick rather than sharp. Drums feel half-time, the snare lands on three or slightly late, and cymbals are used sparingly. Vocals alternate between a wail and clean singing, with clean guitar or organ-like sustained tones between sections. Recordings push low frequency and reverb, so the music seems to occupy a large room with air between notes. The guitarist adds an octave or fifth to the power chord to thicken the distortion, and the bass follows the lowest note almost in unison. Sections often leave a bar of near silence before the next drop, and that stillness makes the drop land harder.

## Key figures
Tony Iommi of Black Sabbath wrote the template with down-tuning and the tritone, and Ozzy Osbourne's voice kept melody above the weight. Candlemass's Messiah Marcolin sang in an almost operatic register, Trouble brought religious imagery into the genre, and Sleep stretched time through long repetition. Electric Wizard pushed distortion and psychedelic noise further.

## Lineage
Blues rock, psychedelia and the slow passages of Black Sabbath feed in; stoner rock, funeral doom and post-metal come out. It made slowness a structural device rather than a tempo choice, and treated tone and volume as compositional material.

## Common misunderstandings
The common claim is that Black Sabbath called themselves doom, when the label was fixed by fans and magazines in the 1980s. Another is that doom is only slow Sabbath imitation, though funeral doom, stoner and post-metal differ widely in line-up, tone and length. Treating it as the loudest branch of metal also underrates its clean passages and silence.
