::: zh
1913年5月29日，巴黎香榭丽舍剧院。斯特拉文斯基的《春之祭》首演，观众在剧场里争吵、吹口哨，警察进场。音乐史的首演骚乱有了代名词。

## 时间与地点

1913年5月29日，巴黎香榭丽舍剧院。俄罗斯芭蕾舞团演出，尼金斯基编舞，蒙特指挥。斯特拉文斯基与尼金斯基把春天献祭的舞剧搬上舞台。

## 发生了什么

舞剧分两部分，大地的崇拜与献祭。音乐用短促的动机、不断变化的拍号、叠加的调性与沉重的重音，把春天的仪式写成粗粝的节奏。舞蹈放弃外开的脚尖与优雅的手臂，舞者内扣双腿、身体下沉。首演当晚，观众从窃笑到争吵，有人向舞台扔东西，警察进入维持秩序。第二场演出平静得多。

## 改变了什么

节奏从背景变成主角，不协和不再是需要解决的东西，而是可以持续的状态。芭蕾不再只属于优雅的古典语汇，现代舞与后来的交响音乐都从这场演出里取得许可。它也让首演事件成为现代艺术的一部分，名声先由争议建立，再由音乐会检验。

## 听这个转折

库中没有《春之祭》本身。先听斯特拉文斯基的《四首练习曲》，首演前五年的钢琴语言已经有硬边；再听《钢琴拉格音乐》，看他把流行节奏折进现代主义；最后听《五根手指》，听和声的刺如何留在简单旋律里。

::: en
On 29 May 1913 the Théâtre des Champs-Élysées in Paris presented Stravinsky's The Rite of Spring. The audience argued and whistled, and the police came in. The premiere scandal had found its synonym.

## Time and place

29 May 1913, Théâtre des Champs-Élysées, Paris. The Ballets Russes performed it, with choreography by Nijinsky and Pierre Monteux conducting. Stravinsky and Nijinsky put a ballet about a spring sacrifice on the stage.

## What happened

The ballet has two parts, the Adoration of the Earth and the Sacrifice. Short motives, changing metres, stacked tonalities and heavy accents turn the spring ritual into coarse rhythm. The choreography gave up turned-out toes and graceful arms; dancers turned their legs inward and sank into the body. On the first night the audience moved from laughter to argument, some threw objects at the stage, and the police entered to keep order. The second performance was much calmer.

## What changed

Rhythm moved from background to protagonist, and dissonance stopped being something that had to resolve. Ballet no longer belonged only to a graceful classical vocabulary, and modern dance and later orchestral music took permission from this evening. It also made the premiere event part of modern art: reputation could be built by scandal, then tested in the concert hall.

## Listen for the turn

The Rite itself is not in the library. Begin with Stravinsky's Four Etudes, whose piano language already has hard edges five years before the premiere; then Piano-Rag-Music, where popular rhythm is folded into modernism; finally Les cinq doigts, where the harmonic sting remains inside a simple melody.
