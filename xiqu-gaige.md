## 时间与地点

戏曲改革从1949年开始，1950年代全面展开，中心在北京，各省市设戏曲改进机构。戏改与同时期的剧团国营化、戏曲教育改制同步进行。1951年5月5日政务院发布《关于戏曲改革工作的指示》，通称五五指示。1956年浙江昆苏剧团带《十五贯》进京，成为戏改中的标志性事件。

## 发生了什么

戏改的核心是改戏、改人、改制。改戏指整理传统剧目、删除迷信与色情内容、分批禁演部分旧戏；整理本讲究主题完整，删除鬼戏、色情与侮辱性情节，也改动唱词。改人指艺人学习与组织化；改制指剧团由班社制转为国营或集体制。各地整理出大批传统戏，也创作现代戏，导演制、剧本制与舞台美术逐渐取代旧戏班的角儿制。

## 音乐特征

戏改后的舞台版讲究剧本完整、导演统一与音乐设计。唱腔仍按剧种的板腔或曲牌组织，但常由作曲者记谱、定腔、配器，加入西洋乐器与和声。新编现代戏的唱腔要照顾人物，常打破原有板式顺序。同一出戏两次演出，速度与过门也比过去更接近。乐队从三五人的文场武场扩为中西混合编制，音量与色彩更丰富，也改变了演员与乐师即兴配合的关系。

## 听什么

先听《花鼓》，花鼓小戏的歌舞曲调，能听出地方小戏进入城市舞台前的样子；再听《皮影调》，影戏的板腔上下句，可对照戏改整理地方戏时的素材；最后听《河北梆子号》，北方梆子的高亢唱风。三例都不是戏改后的舞台录音。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把戏改等同于文革。戏改始于1949年，文革是1966年后的另一场运动，两者政策与后果不同。也有人以为戏改只是禁戏，其实整理、改编、创作与剧团改制同样重要；更不是要消灭传统，许多传统剧目正是经过戏改才重新上演。

## Time and place

Xiqu reform began in 1949 and unfolded through the 1950s, centred on Beijing with opera-reform offices in every province. On 5 May 1951 the Government Administration Council issued its directive on opera reform, known as the May 5 Directive. In 1956 the Zhejiang Kunju company brought Fifteen Strings of Cash to Beijing, a landmark of the reform.

## What happened

The reform had three strands: repertory, personnel and institutions. Repertory meant sorting traditional plays, removing superstition and erotic matter, and banning some old pieces in stages. Reform committees argued over which plays were feudal and which could be saved. Personnel meant study and organisation for performers. Institutions meant turning private troupes into state or collective companies. Traditional plays were edited, modern plays commissioned, and director, script and stage design replaced the old star system.

## Musical features

The reformed stage prized a complete script, unified direction and designed music. Aria still followed the genre's banqiang or qupai structure, but composers now notated, fixed and orchestrated the tunes, adding Western instruments and harmony. New scores fixed the tempo, so two performances sounded more alike than before. Bands grew from a handful of melody and percussion players to mixed Chinese-Western ensembles, richer in volume and colour, changing the old improvisation between singer and accompanist.

## What to listen for

Hear the Beijing Huagu, a flower-drum skit tune, for how local song-and-dance sounded before city stages. Then hear the shadow-play tune, with its banqiang upper and lower phrases, as source material for reformed local opera. Finally hear Hebei Bangzi Hao, with its high northern delivery. None is a post-reform stage recording.

## Common misunderstandings

Xiqu reform is often equated with the Cultural Revolution. It began in 1949, while the later movement came after 1966, with different policies and results. Others think reform meant only banning plays, though editing, adaptation, new writing and institutional change mattered equally. Nor was it meant to erase tradition; many old plays returned to the stage through reform.
