::: zh
1935年，德国 AEG 把第一台可实用的磁带录音机推到柏林广播展上。声音变成塑料带上的一层磁粉，可以剪开、接回、叠录。

## 时间与地点

1935年，柏林。AEG 的 Magnetophon K1 使用塑料带基与羰基铁粉涂层，在柏林广播展亮相，德国广播机构随即采用。1941年交流偏磁提高音质，战后机器流入美国，1948年 Ampex 200 上市。

## 发生了什么

磁带把录音变成可编辑的介质。工程师用刀片剪开带子，把段落调换、拼接或循环；多轨机让同一卷带上的不同轨分别录制，再合成混音。德国广播用磁带延时播出，战后美国电台预录节目，唱片公司用磁带制作母带。录音棚从捕捉一次演奏的现场，变成可以反复修改的制作车间。

## 改变了什么

录音从记录变成创作。剪接让演奏可以拼接，多轨让一个人完成合奏，回声与延迟成为常规音色。电台节目可以预制，唱片母带可以复制，音乐制作的成本与门槛重新分配。

::: en
In 1935 AEG showed the first practical magnetic tape recorder at the Berlin radio exhibition. Sound became magnetic particles on plastic tape, ready to be cut, joined and stacked.

## Time and place

Berlin, 1935. AEG's Magnetophon K1 used plastic tape coated with carbonyl iron powder; German broadcasters soon used it. AC bias improved the sound in 1941, postwar machines reached the United States, and the Ampex 200 arrived in 1948.

## What happened

Tape made recording editable. Engineers cut tape with a blade and reordered, joined or looped passages; multitrack machines recorded separate tracks and mixed them later. German radio used tape for delayed broadcasts, American stations pre-recorded shows, and labels cut masters on tape. The studio became a workshop that could revise a performance.

## What changed

Recording became composition. Splicing assembled performances, multitrack let one player build an ensemble, and echo and delay became ordinary colours. Radio programmes could be pre-recorded, masters copied, and production costs and access redistributed.

## Listen for the turn

The library holds no 1935 tape recording or tape-music original. Begin with the Goldberg aria, whose Gould 1955 recording was spliced from takes; then the Brandenburg Concerto No. 3, assembled by Carlos from multitrack tape; finally Oh Happy Day, and hear the thickness of overdubbing.
