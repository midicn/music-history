::: zh
鞭击金属把英国重金属新浪潮的 riff 与朋克的速度拼在一起，1981 到 1986 年在加州与纽约成形。

## 起源与年代
1981 年，Metallica 在洛杉矶组队，1983 年迁往旧金山湾区并发行《Kill 'Em All》。同一年，Slayer 在亨廷顿公园组队，Megadeth 在 Dave Mustaine 离队后成立，纽约的 Anthrax 也已完成编制。德国的 Sodom 与 Kreator 在鲁尔区同期出现，形成另一条路线。这些乐队最初靠磁带交换与地下演出传播，1980 年代中后期才进入更大的场地。

## 听辨特征
速度常在 160 到 220 BPM。吉他用下拨与手掌闷音弹出连续的十六分音符 riff，段落靠突然停顿与加速切换。主唱是喊叫或半唱，独奏技术性强，常用和声小调与半音跑动。音色比硬核厚，制作更接近重金属，节奏吉他与独奏吉他分层录制，低频被压紧。鼓手在 riff 的强拍上加双踩，过门用快速的托姆鼓滚奏；吉他在低音弦上反复同一动机，独奏时突然转到高把位。主唱多在段落之间留白，让 riff 自己说话。节奏吉他常叠录两次，左右声道各一。

## 代表人物
Metallica 的 James Hetfield 把下拨 riff 变成标志，Slayer 的 Kerry King 与 Jeff Hanneman 用半音与快速交替独奏，Megadeth 的 Dave Mustaine 偏爱复杂编曲与变速。Anthrax 把纽约硬核的节奏感带进来，德国的 Kreator 与 Sodom 把攻击性推到更极端。

## 谱系
上承英国重金属新浪潮、硬核朋克与 1970 年代重金属，下接死亡金属、金属核与 1990 年代的极端金属。它是朋克与金属第一次真正合流，也让“速度”成为可以单独讨论的技术指标。1980 年代末，这一类型的技术标准被写进吉他教学与鼓教材，成为极端金属的共同语汇。它的现场文化也确立了金属乐队的巡演模式。1990 年代，这一类型分裂出更旋律化与更极端的两个方向。

## 常见误解
常见说法是鞭击金属属于英国，其实主要场景在加州、纽约与德国。另一个误解是它只追求速度，中速的节奏段落与旋律同样重要，许多名曲的核心是慢速重压而不是冲刺。

::: en
Thrash metal joined the riffs of the New Wave of British Heavy Metal to punk speed, forming in California and New York between 1981 and 1986.

## Origins and era
Metallica formed in Los Angeles in 1981, moved to the San Francisco Bay Area in 1983 and released Kill 'Em All. That same year Slayer formed in Huntington Park, Megadeth began after Dave Mustaine left Metallica, and Anthrax was already complete in New York. Sodom and Kreator appeared in Germany's Ruhr region around the same time, forming another line. These bands spread at first through tape trading and underground shows, reaching larger rooms only in the second half of the 1980s.

## What you hear
Tempos often run from 160 to 220 BPM. Guitar plays continuous sixteenth-note riffs with downpicking and palm muting, and sections switch through sudden stops and accelerations. The singer shouts or half-sings, solos are technical, and harmonic minor and chromatic runs are common. The tone is thicker than hardcore and closer to metal production, with rhythm and solo guitars recorded in layers and the low end compressed. The drummer adds double kick on the riff's strong beats and fills with fast tom rolls, while guitar repeats one motif on the low strings before the solo jumps to a higher position. The singer leaves gaps between phrases so the riff can speak. Rhythm guitar is usually tracked twice, one take per side.

## Key figures
James Hetfield of Metallica made the downpicked riff a signature, Kerry King and Jeff Hanneman of Slayer used chromatic lines and fast alternate picking, and Dave Mustaine of Megadeth favoured complex arrangements and tempo changes. Anthrax brought New York hardcore rhythm, while Kreator and Sodom in Germany pushed the attack further.

## Lineage
The New Wave of British Heavy Metal, hardcore punk and 1970s metal feed in; death metal, metalcore and 1990s extreme metal come out. It was the first real merger of punk and metal, and it made speed something that could be discussed as a technique on its own. By the late 1980s its technical standards had entered guitar and drum instruction and become a shared language for extreme metal. Its live culture also set the touring pattern for metal bands.

## Common misunderstandings
The common claim is that thrash belongs to Britain, when the main scenes were in California, New York and Germany. Another is that it is only about speed; mid-tempo passages and melody matter just as much, and many well-known songs turn on a slow heavy riff rather than a sprint.
