::: zh
艺术摇滚把古典音乐的和声与形式借用、奇数拍和剧场化的呈现带进摇滚，1960 年代末在英国成形。

## 起源与年代
1967 年是起点，Procol Harum 的“A Whiter Shade of Pale”与 Moody Blues 的《Days of Future Passed》把管风琴、弦乐与组曲结构带进流行单曲与专辑。1969 年 King Crimson 的《In the Court of the Crimson King》确立了更重的版本，Yes、Genesis 与 Emerson, Lake and Palmer 在 1970 年代初把它推向大型场馆。Roxy Music 1972 年的首张专辑从另一端加入，用拼贴与形象代替技巧展示。

## 听辨特征
形式先于音色。组曲、概念专辑、套曲式的长篇结构取代主歌副歌；拍号常在同一首里切换，5/4、7/8 与不规则小节并不罕见。键盘用 Mellotron 或管风琴铺出持续和弦，吉他承担主题与变奏，古典动机被引用或改写。歌词偏文学与寓言，专辑封面与舞台布景是作品的一部分，现场常配幻灯片与服装。录音室被当作乐器，叠录与音效的层数远超同期摇滚；唱腔介于吟诵与歌唱之间，有时接近念白，有时突然拔高。

## 代表人物
King Crimson 的 Robert Fripp 把不协和与奇数拍变成语言，Yes 的 Jon Anderson 与 Rick Wakeman 把高声部与键盘技巧推到前台。Genesis 的 Peter Gabriel 用服装与叙事把演出变成剧场，Roxy Music 的 Bryan Ferry 与 Brian Eno 把艺术学校的拼贴方法带进流行歌曲。Procol Harum 则证明管风琴与古典和声可以在三分钟内成立。

## 谱系
上承 1960 年代的迷幻摇滚、古典音乐教育与英国艺术学校传统，下接前卫摇滚、后朋克中的实验支线，以及 1990 年代的后摇滚。它把“专辑作为完整作品”的观念固定下来，也把舞台呈现纳入音乐的一部分。它的听众后来扩展到金属与前卫流行，艺术学校的方法则进入音乐录影带与舞台设计。

## 常见误解
常见说法是艺术摇滚等于前卫摇滚，两者大量重叠，但艺术摇滚更宽，包含短歌、拼贴与形象操作，Roxy Music 与早期的 Bowie 都不靠长篇组曲。另一个误解是它只追求技巧，实际上不少作品的核心是概念与编排，而非独奏速度。把它当成只属于英国也不准确，美国的实验摇滚与它平行发展。

::: en
Art rock brought classical borrowings in harmony and form, odd metres and theatrical presentation into rock, forming in Britain in the late 1960s.

## Origins and era
1967 was the starting point, when Procol Harum's A Whiter Shade of Pale and the Moody Blues' Days of Future Passed brought organ, strings and suite structure into singles and albums. King Crimson's In the Court of the Crimson King in 1969 set a heavier version, and Yes, Genesis and Emerson, Lake and Palmer carried it into large halls in the early 1970s. Roxy Music's 1972 debut joined from another direction, replacing displays of technique with collage and image.

## What you hear
Form comes before tone. Suites, concept albums and long multi-part structures replace verse and chorus, and metres shift inside one piece, with 5/4, 7/8 and irregular bars common. Keyboards lay sustained chords on Mellotron or organ, guitar carries themes and variations, and classical motifs are quoted or rewritten. Lyrics lean toward literature and allegory, album covers and stage sets are part of the work, and concerts often include slides and costumes. The studio became an instrument, with more overdubs and tape effects than rock records of the period usually carried. The singing moves between speech and song, sometimes rising suddenly.

## Key figures
Robert Fripp of King Crimson made dissonance and odd metre into a language, while Jon Anderson and Rick Wakeman of Yes pushed high registers and keyboard technique forward. Peter Gabriel turned Genesis shows into theatre through costume and story, and Bryan Ferry with Brian Eno brought art-school collage into pop song. Procol Harum proved that organ and classical harmony could work inside three minutes.

## Lineage
1960s psychedelia, classical training and the British art-school tradition feed in; progressive rock, experimental strands of post-punk and 1990s post-rock come out. It fixed the idea of the album as a complete work and made stage presentation part of the music. Its audience later widened toward metal and art pop, while the art-school method moved into music video and stage design.

## Common misunderstandings
The common claim is that art rock equals progressive rock. The two overlap heavily, but art rock is wider and includes short songs, collage and image work; Roxy Music and early Bowie rely on neither suites nor length. Another is that the style is only about technique, when concept and arrangement, not solo speed, drive many of its records. Treating it as purely British is also wrong, since American experimental rock developed in parallel.
