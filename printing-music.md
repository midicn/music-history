::: zh
1501年，威尼斯印刷商佩特鲁奇把九十六首法文尚松排成一本四声部歌集。音乐从此可以像书一样被复制、定价、携带和收藏。

## 时间与地点

1501年，威尼斯。奥塔维亚诺·佩特鲁奇出版《音乐谐调百歌集》，这是第一部用活字印刷的大型复调音乐集。1520年代，阿泰尼昂在巴黎改用一次印刷，成本大降。

## 发生了什么

佩特鲁奇用三次印刷分别印上谱线、音符和歌词，工序繁复但成品清晰。歌集收录若斯坎、奥克冈等人的尚松，买主是宫廷、教堂和业余音乐家。一次印刷把谱线、音符与歌词压在同一版上，印本从此可以批量进入市场。印刷商开始挑选畅销作曲家，若斯坎的名字成为卖点。

## 改变了什么

音乐从手抄本进入商品世界。作曲家可以面向更远的读者写作，印刷特权与出版市场随之出现；演奏者能买到同一份谱，业余合奏有了共同曲目。记谱也因此趋于统一，因为印本必须让陌生人也能读。没有印刷，文艺复兴的跨国音乐风格不会传播得这么快。

::: en
In 1501 the Venetian printer Ottaviano Petrucci set ninety-six French chansons as a four-voice songbook. Music could now be copied, priced, carried and collected like a book.

## Time and place

Venice, 1501. Petrucci published the Harmonice musices odhecaton, the first large collection of polyphonic music printed with movable type. In the 1520s Pierre Attaingnant in Paris moved to single-impression printing and cut the cost.

## What happened

Petrucci printed staff lines, notes and text in three passes, a slow process with a clear result. The collection included chansons by Josquin, Ockeghem and others, and it sold to courts, churches and amateur musicians. Single-impression printing pressed lines, notes and text in one operation, so editions could enter the market in quantity. Printers began to pick best-selling composers, and Josquin's name became a selling point.

## What changed

Music moved from manuscript into the world of goods. Composers could write for readers far away, printing privileges and a publishing market appeared, and players could buy the same score, giving amateur ensembles a common repertory. Notation also became more uniform, because a printed page had to be readable by strangers. Without print, the international style of the Renaissance would not have travelled so quickly.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Josquin's Mille regretz, a chanson reprinted again and again; then hear the joke of El grillo, and finally the polyphonic frame of the Missa l'homme armé. The library versions are synthesized; listen to how four voices align on one page, not to the printing itself.
