## 这一站是做什么的

音乐史是整套知识的**时间轴**。它不孤立地讲一件作品或一件乐器，而是回答：
**在某一个时间点上，音乐里发生了什么，和它前后是什么关系。**

## 时间线里有什么

- **时期总览** —— 从早期的单声部圣咏到当代，每个时期一篇，讲清这个时期的音乐在做什么、
  为什么那样做、给后面留下了什么。
- **里程碑** —— 具体的事：一部作品的首演、一部论著、一次乐器改良、一种演出场所的出现。
  每条只讲清「它改变了什么」。
- **专题** —— 跨时期的线索，比如记谱方式的变迁、乐团编制的扩张、演奏场合从教堂到音乐厅的移动。

中国音乐的部分在这里只给**一小段对照**，说清「同一个时间，中国在做什么」，
深读放在中国音乐站 —— 同一件事不写两遍。

## 每条长什么样

- 开头是**时间与地点**，然后是「发生了什么」和「为什么重要」。
- 关键条目会给出**可以在库里直接播放的曲目**，让读者听见当时的写法。
- 中英双语，两版的事实与年代一致。

## 怎么读

1. **按时期顺序读** —— 从头走到尾，看线索怎么接上。
2. **按专题横读** —— 顺着「记谱」「乐器」「场合」这类线索跨时期看。
3. **读到中国那一段时** —— 从对照段直接进中国音乐站。

## 会按什么顺序出现

时期与里程碑正在陆续上线。这一站的内容要等时间线成形才有意义，
所以会**按时期一段一段地推进**，而不是零散地先发几条。

## What this station is for

Music history is the **timeline** of the whole library. It does not treat a work or an
instrument in isolation; it answers **what was happening in music at a given moment, and
how that relates to what came before and after.**

## What the timeline will hold

- **Period overviews** — from early monophonic chant to the present; each period gets a
  piece explaining what its music was doing, why, and what it left behind.
- **Milestones** — concrete events: a premiere, a treatise, a change to an instrument, the
  arrival of a new kind of venue. Each entry explains only **what it changed**.
- **Features** — threads that cross periods, such as the shift in notation, the growth of
  the orchestra, and the move of performance from church to concert hall.

Chinese music appears here only as **a short comparison** — what was happening in China at
the same moment. The depth lives on the Chinese music station, so nothing is written twice.

## What each entry looks like

- It opens with **time and place**, then says what happened and why it mattered.
- Key entries offer **tracks you can play straight from the library**, so you can hear the
  writing of the time.
- Written in both Chinese and English, with the same dates and facts in each.

## How to read it

1. **In order, by period** — walk it end to end and watch the threads connect.
2. **Across, by feature** — follow notation, instruments or venues across periods.
3. **At the Chinese comparison** — go straight from there to the Chinese music station.

## How it will be released

Periods and milestones are being added over time. This station only makes sense once the
timeline holds together, so it is being built **one period at a time** rather than as a
scattering of early entries.
