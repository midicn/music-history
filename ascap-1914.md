## 时间坐标

1914 年 2 月，纽约。维克多·赫伯特、约翰·菲利普·苏萨与一批词曲作者在饭店里成立美国作曲家、作家与出版商协会。

## 事情经过

此前的版权只覆盖乐谱销售。餐馆、舞厅与剧院乐队公开演奏流行歌曲，作者拿不到钱。ASCAP 代表会员向演出场所发放一揽子许可证，按曲库收年费，再按抽样分版税给词曲作者与出版商。第一年收入很小，1920 年代广播兴起后许可证扩展到电台，收入迅速增长。

## 留下的变化

写歌第一次可以靠持续播放获得收入，而不只靠卖谱。职业词曲作者形成，出版体系与 ASCAP 互相支撑。电台与演出场所开始为音乐付费，也引发长期对抗，1939 年广播业另组 BMI 与它竞争。表演权收入后来扩展到电影、电视与流媒体，成为音乐产业的主要收入之一。集体管理的费率与抽样，至今仍是版权争论的焦点。

## 听一段转折

格什温《里亚托翻筋斗拉格》是 1917 年的钢琴拉格，演出场所的常备曲目；《我当年多年轻》是 1919 年的抒情歌，靠乐谱与现场演唱传播；《再来一次》是 1922 年的舞曲，副歌短，适合乐队反复演奏。三首都在 ASCAP 早期会员的出版曲库里。

## On the timeline

New York, February 1914. Victor Herbert, John Philip Sousa and a group of songwriters founded the American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers.

## The sequence of events

Copyright then covered sheet music. Restaurants, dance halls and theatre orchestras performed popular songs, and the writers were paid nothing. ASCAP issued blanket licences to venues, collected annual fees against its repertoire, and distributed royalties from samples of what was played. When radio arrived, licences extended to broadcasters and receipts grew.

## What it left behind

A songwriter could now earn from repeated performance, not only sheet-music sales. Professional songwriting took shape, with publishers and ASCAP supporting each other. Broadcasters and venues paid for music, which produced decades of conflict; in 1939 the radio industry formed BMI as a rival.

## Hear the turn

Gershwin's Rialto Ripples Rag, from 1917, was a piano rag ready for any venue. I Was So Young, from 1919, is a ballad carried by sheet music and live singing, and Do it Again, from 1922, has a short chorus a band could repeat. All three sit in an early ASCAP member's catalogue.
