## 时间与地点

十六世纪末到十八世纪初，佛罗伦萨、曼图亚、罗马与巴黎。蒙特威尔第在曼图亚写歌剧，科雷利在罗马写协奏曲，拉莫在巴黎把和声实践写成理论。

## 发生了什么

通奏低音把和弦进行放到低音线上，作曲家以低音走向组织和声，三和弦、属到主的终止与调号逐渐固定。1700年前后，大小调与十二个调的循环成为日常语言；1722年，拉莫的《和声学》把这一实践写成体系。调性不再是声部运动的副产品，而是可以预先规划的框架。

## 改变了什么

和声从横向声部的副产物变成可规划的建筑。转调、奏鸣曲式、协奏曲的回归都依赖主音与属音的拉力；作曲家开始用调性布局整部作品，记谱也用调号代替逐个升号。此后两百年，西方音乐的基本语法就是调性。

## 听这个转折

先听蒙特威尔第《阿里安娜的哀歌》，低音一步步下行，痛苦被写成和声。再听科雷利 Op.6 的协奏曲，序列把调性推出去又拉回来。最后听巴赫《平均律》第二卷的降A大调赋格，主题在调内展开，转调有清楚的落点。三首曲子分别让你听见低音、终止与调性布局。

## Time and place

From the late sixteenth century to the early eighteenth, in Florence, Mantua, Rome and Paris. Monteverdi wrote opera in Mantua, Corelli wrote concertos in Rome, and Rameau turned harmonic practice into theory in Paris.

## What happened

Basso continuo placed chord progressions on a bass line, and composers began to organise harmony by the motion of that bass; triads, dominant-to-tonic cadences and key signatures gradually settled. Around 1700, major and minor keys and the cycle of twelve keys became everyday language. In 1722 Rameau's Traité de l'harmonie wrote the practice into a system. Tonality was no longer a by-product of voice-leading but a frame that could be planned in advance.

## What changed

Harmony became an architecture rather than a residue of horizontal voices. Modulation, sonata form and the returns of a concerto all depend on the pull between tonic and dominant; composers began to shape whole works through key plans, and notation used key signatures instead of writing every sharp. For the next two centuries, tonality was the basic grammar of Western music.

## Listen to the turn

Begin with Monteverdi's Lamento d'Arianna, where a falling bass turns grief into harmony. Then hear Corelli's Op. 6 concerto, whose sequences push the key away and pull it back. Finally Bach's A-flat major fugue from Book II of the Well-Tempered Clavier, its subject unfolding inside the key with clear points of departure. The three pieces let you hear bass, cadence and key plan in turn.
