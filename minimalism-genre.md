::: zh
极简主义是二十世纪六十年代美国的音乐潮流，用少量材料反复、叠加与错位，让听众在长时间里听细微变化。

## 起源与年代
1960 年代，拉蒙特·扬、特里·赖利、史蒂夫·赖希与菲利普·格拉斯在纽约实验音乐圈工作。赖利 1964 年《In C》用五十三个短句自由叠加，常被视作起点。1970 年代，格拉斯与赖希把重复写进歌剧、电影配乐与大型合奏。

## 听辨特征
先听重复，短音型不断回到同一形状。再听相位，两个相同音型以微小速度差错开，产生流动的节奏。和声变化很慢，常在一个和弦上停留很久，再换到邻近和弦。配器可以极简，也可以堆到很大。听的时候注意密度和层次，而不是等待旋律事件。

## 代表人物
赖利写《In C》，赖希写《六台马林巴》《十八位音乐家的音乐》，格拉斯写《沙滩上的爱因斯坦》与《时时刻刻》配乐，亚当斯写《震教徒的环》。

## 谱系
上承实验音乐、印度音乐与甘美兰，下接后极简主义、氛围音乐与电影配乐。

## 常见误解
极简主义不是简单，重复里的相位与配器变化需要精确控制。它也不等于没有情感，长时间重复常带来强烈的沉浸感。

::: en
Minimalism is an American current of the 1960s that uses a few ideas, repeated, layered and shifted, so that small changes become the main event.

## Origins and dating
In 1960s New York, La Monte Young, Terry Riley, Steve Reich and Philip Glass worked in the experimental scene. Riley's In C of 1964, with its 53 short patterns, is often treated as the starting point. In the 1970s Glass and Reich brought repetition into opera, film music and large ensembles.

## Listening features
Listen for repetition, as a short figure returns to the same shape. Then listen for phase, where two identical figures drift slightly apart and create a rolling rhythm. Harmony changes slowly, often resting on one chord before moving to a neighbour. Forces may be tiny or very large. Follow density and layering rather than waiting for melodic events.

## Figures
Riley wrote In C, Reich wrote Six Marimbas and Music for 18 Musicians, Glass wrote Einstein on the Beach and the score for The Hours, and John Adams wrote Shaker Loops.

## Lineage
It draws on experimental music, Indian music and gamelan and leads to post-minimalism, ambient music and film scoring.

## Misconceptions
Minimalism is not simple, since phase and orchestration demand exact control. Nor is it unemotional, because long repetition can create a strong sense of immersion.
