## 年份与坐标

1815年，伦敦与维也纳。梅尔策尔在伦敦取得便携节拍器的专利；他与贝多芬的来往在维也纳，自动乐器、助听器与节拍器都经过这条线。

## 先看经过

在这之前，Allegro 与 Andante 只是相对的说法，同一首曲子在教堂、宫廷与剧院可以差很多。梅尔策尔用可调重锤的双摆给出每分钟拍数，把速度写成数字。贝多芬很快采用，在第七、第八交响曲与《槌子键琴奏鸣曲》上标出节拍器数字；出版商与音乐学院随后跟进。

## 之后不一样了

速度从形容词变成可传递的参数。作曲家可以把意图写死，演奏者可以跨城市复现；节拍器也进入练习室，成为教学工具。争议同时出现，贝多芬的许多标记快得让人怀疑，今天的节拍器战争仍围绕这些数字。

## 听一段转折

先听《槌子键琴奏鸣曲》第一乐章，开头标着极快的二分音符 138，听演奏者如何在清晰与狂飙之间取舍。再听第九交响曲的急板，节拍标记把对比推到极限。最后听第七交响曲的 Allegretto，它常被奏得比标记慢，数字与呼吸在此拉扯。

## Year and coordinates

London and Vienna, 1815. Maelzel took out a patent for a portable metronome in London; his dealings with Beethoven belonged to Vienna, where he built automata and ear trumpets for the composer.

## First, the event

Before this, Allegro and Andante were relative terms, and the same piece could differ greatly between church, court and theatre. Maelzel used a double pendulum with an adjustable weight to give beats per minute and turned tempo into a number. Beethoven adopted the device quickly, marking metronome values on the Seventh and Eighth Symphonies and on the Hammerklavier Sonata; publishers and conservatories followed.

## What was different after

Tempo became a transferable parameter rather than an adjective. Composers could fix their intention, players could reproduce it in another city, and the metronome entered the practice room as a teaching tool. Disputes arrived at the same time, as many of Beethoven's marks seem impossibly fast, and today's metronome wars still circle those numbers.

## Hear the turn

Begin with the first movement of the Hammerklavier Sonata, with a very fast half-note mark of 138; hear how a player chooses between clarity and frenzy. Then the presto of the Ninth Symphony, where the mark pushes contrast to its limit. Finally the Allegretto of the Seventh, often played slower than the mark, where number and breath pull against each other.
