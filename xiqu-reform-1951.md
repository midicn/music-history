::: zh
一份政务院指示，把在市场上讨生活的戏班，变成了有编制、有审查、有排练制度的单位。

## 时间与地点

1950年11月，北京召开全国戏曲工作会议。1951年5月5日，政务院发布《关于戏曲改革工作的指示》，各地文化部门随后开始执行，1952年10月又在北京举办第一届全国戏曲观摩演出大会。

## 发生了什么

1950年11月，全国戏曲工作会议在北京召开。1951年5月5日的指示提出改戏、改人、改制，要求整理传统剧目、净化舞台形象、改造艺人思想，并把民营戏班逐步改为国营剧团。各地随后陆续禁演一批剧目，删改唱词与表演，艺人成为单位职工。

## 改变了什么

戏曲从市场里的班社变成国家文化系统的一部分。剧目要经过审查与整理，唱词要改，鬼戏与色情表演被禁，现代戏被鼓励。剧团有了编制、工资与排练制度，演员从挂头牌的角儿变成按月拿工资的演员。这套制度提高了戏曲的社会地位，也把创作的决定权从票房移到了文化部门。会演则提供了新的评价方式，评委、奖项与调演取代了戏园子里的叫好。禁戏名单后来有反复，一些剧目经修改后重新上演，审查标准随政治形势变化。戏改也让许多地方戏第一次有了全国性的能见度，原本只在乡间演出的剧种被调进北京。

## 听这个转折

先听《姑苏风光·大九连环》，曲牌一支接一支，唱的是风景与风俗，这是需要整理的旧套曲。再听《无锡景》，同一类时调被用来叙事，词一换，内容就变。最后听《一盏明灯照北京》，旧曲调装进新词与新语气，整齐、明亮、面向集体。三首都不是戏曲唱段，但能听出戏改改的不只是唱腔，还有唱腔所服务的那个人。

::: en
One government directive turned troupes that lived by the market into units with payrolls, censorship and rehearsal schedules.

## Time and place

Beijing, November 1950, where a national conference on opera met. On 5 May 1951 the State Council issued its directive on opera reform, and cultural bureaus began applying it; in October 1952 Beijing hosted the first national festival of operatic performance.

## What happened

The national conference met in Beijing in November 1950. The directive of May 1951 set out three tasks, reforming plays, reforming performers and reforming institutions. Traditional repertory was to be edited, stage images cleaned up and performers re-educated, while private troupes were gradually converted into state companies. Local authorities banned a number of plays outright, cut lyrics and stage business, and performers became salaried employees.

## What changed

Opera moved from a market institution into part of the state cultural system. Plays passed through review and editing, lyrics were revised, ghost plays and sexual display were banned, and modern-dress opera was encouraged. Companies acquired establishments, wages and rehearsal rules, and actors turned from star performers into salaried staff. The system raised the social standing of opera and moved the power of decision from the box office to cultural bureaus. Festivals supplied a new way of judging quality, with juries, prizes and official showcases replacing the shouts of the teahouse.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Beautiful Scenes of Suzhou, a suite of linked qupai describing scenery and customs, exactly the old form that needed editing. Then Wuxi Scene, where a similar tune carries narrative, and the content changes as soon as the words do. Finally A Bright Lamp Shines on Beijing, an old tune fitted with new words and a new tone, orderly, bright and addressed to a collective. None is an opera excerpt, yet together they show that the reform changed not only the tunes but the person the tunes served.
