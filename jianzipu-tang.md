::: zh
西方的五线谱告诉你音有多高、有多长，古琴的减字谱告诉你用哪根弦、按哪个徽位、用哪个手指。两种谱记录的不是同一件事，减字谱记的是动作。

## 时间与地点

唐代，八世纪前后，长安与洛阳一带。更早的琴谱是整句文字，现存唯一的文字谱《碣石调·幽兰》是六世纪的作品，唐代抄本留存至今。把文字缩成符号的减字谱，传统上归功于唐代琴家曹柔，具体年代没有定论，通称中唐。

## 发生了什么

减字谱把一句指法说明拆成几个部件，上半写左手指法，下半写弦号，旁注徽位，右边写右手的弹法，几个部件拼成一个方块字。看到这个字，琴人知道按第几弦、第几徽、用哪根手指、怎样弹，却不知道这个音有多长。节奏靠老师口传，或靠打谱者根据指法与句读重建。同一份谱，不同琴派可以打出不同的节奏与气口，同一首曲子因此有多种合法的样子。

## 改变了什么

琴曲可以跨朝代保存，一千多年前的指法今天仍能读出来，这是减字谱最大的功劳。代价是节奏没有固定，每次打谱都是一次再创作，琴派之间的差别因此有了制度上的位置。与工尺谱相比，减字谱不标相对音高，只标动作；与五线谱相比，它把音高交给弦与徽位，把节奏留白。三种记谱法各自选择记住什么、忘记什么，减字谱选择记住手。

## 听这个转折

曲库里没有古琴录音，这里用三首民歌听减字谱要面对的声音世界。先听《高山调》，标题与《高山流水》相通，听五声骨架怎样撑住旋律。再听《阳关歌》，与琴歌《阳关三叠》同题，听送别句的重复与句读。最后听《茉莉花》，五声旋律加上装饰，那些装饰的位置正是减字谱留给演奏者的空间。听完再想，如果谱上不写节奏，演奏者要补上多少东西。

::: en
Staff notation tells you how high and how long. Guqin jianzipu tells you which string, which position marker and which finger. The two scripts do not record the same thing; jianzipu records an action.

## Time and place

China in the Tang dynasty, around the eighth century, in the region of Chang'an and Luoyang. Earlier qin scores were written out in full sentences; the only surviving example, Jieshi Diao You Lan, is a sixth-century piece preserved in a Tang copy. The abbreviation into symbols is traditionally credited to the Tang player Cao Rou, though the date is not settled and is usually given only as the middle Tang.

## What happened

Jianzipu breaks a fingering instruction into parts. The upper element names the left-hand finger, the lower gives the string, the position marker sits beside it, and a side element gives the right-hand stroke; the parts are stacked into a single block-like character. From that character a player knows the string, the node, the finger and the pluck, but not how long the note lasts. Rhythm came from a teacher, or was rebuilt by a later player in the process called dapu, working from fingering and phrase marks. One score could yield different rhythms and breaths in different qin schools, so a single piece has several legitimate forms.

## What changed

Qin music could survive across dynasties, and fingering written a thousand years ago can still be read, which is the notation's great achievement. The price is that rhythm was never fixed, so every dapu is a re-creation and the differences between schools acquired an institutional place. Compared with gongchepu, jianzipu does not mark relative pitch at all, only action. Compared with staff notation, it hands pitch to strings and nodes and leaves rhythm blank. Each script chooses what to remember and what to forget; jianzipu chose the hand.

## Listen for the turn

The catalogue holds no guqin recording, so these three folk melodies stand in for the sound world the notation had to handle. Begin with Gao shan diao, whose title echoes the qin piece High Mountains and Flowing Water, and hear the pentatonic frame carrying the tune. Then Yang guan ge, on the same theme as the qin song Yang guan san die, and listen to the repeated farewell phrases and their phrasing. Finally Moli hua, a pentatonic melody thick with ornament, exactly the space jianzipu leaves to the player. Then ask how much a performer must supply when the score gives no rhythm.
