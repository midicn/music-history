::: zh
1890 到 1920 年，巴黎把音乐的重心从“讲完一个故事”挪到“留下一种气味”。和声不再急着回家，音色本身成了结构。所谓“印象派”是后来贴上的标签，德彪西并不喜欢它，拉威尔与萨蒂也各走各路。

## 时代背景

第三共和国下的巴黎是欧洲的艺术市场：沙龙、画廊、出版社与剧院密集，音乐学院仍教传统和声。1889 与 1900 年的世界博览会带来爪哇加美兰与日本版画，1909 年俄罗斯芭蕾进入巴黎，异域音阶与舞团进入作曲家的视野。象征主义诗歌强调暗示而非陈述，德彪西为马拉美《牧神午后》写前奏曲。第一次世界大战结束了这个时代。

## 音乐特征

和弦可以只为颜色存在：九和弦、十一和弦、加六和弦与平行进行不再必须解决。全音阶把八度六等分，五声音阶与教会调式提供不依赖导音的旋律；低音常停在主音或持续音上，让上方和声自由漂浮。节奏避免方正的重音，配器偏爱弱奏的铜管、木管低音区、[[instrument:harp|竖琴]]与钢片琴。旋律短小、碎片化，像水光一样闪动，[[glossary:timbre|音色]]由此成为主角。

## 体裁与形式

钢琴前奏曲、练习曲、阿拉伯风格曲、组曲与性格小品是核心，德彪西《版画集》里的《塔》用五声与加美兰式音响；拉威尔《夜之幽灵》把钢琴技巧推到极限。管弦乐方面有交响诗、夜曲三联画与芭蕾，德彪西《佩利亚斯与梅丽桑德》把歌剧变成低声的对话。室内乐与艺术歌曲也采用同样的色彩和声。

## 乐器与编制

钢琴是实验室：延音踏板混合泛音，弱音踏板改变音色，演奏者用半踏与分层触键制造雾状音响。管弦乐扩大木管与打击乐，加入竖琴、钢片琴、钟琴、古钹与弱音器铜管；弦乐分奏制造微光。加美兰的金属音色与五声调式影响了《塔》。

## 代表作曲家

- **德彪西（1862–1918）** 用全音阶、平行和弦与自由节奏拆开功能和声，把歌剧、管弦乐与钢琴都变成音色的艺术。
- **拉威尔（1875–1937）** 同样迷恋色彩，却更强调线条、舞曲与古典形式，后期走向新古典。
- **萨蒂（1866–1925）** 以《裸体歌舞》的静止和声与古怪标题预示极简与观念艺术，是德彪西的同代朋友。

## 听什么

先不要找主题：听低音是否停住，听和弦是否拒绝解决，听旋律是否由短小动机拼成。再听音色：竖琴的泛音、钢片琴的金属声、弱音器的鼻音。页末五首从加美兰式五声、月色、静止和声、水之炫技与帕凡舞曲各取一面。

## 中国对照

同一时期，中国从晚清进入民国。学堂乐歌在 1900 年代兴起，沈心工、李叔同用日本与欧美曲调填词；新文化运动推动白话与美育。京剧进入鼎盛，昆曲与地方戏曲仍在民间演出。

::: en
The years 1890–1920 moved the centre of gravity in Paris from finishing a story to leaving a scent. Harmony stops rushing home; timbre itself becomes structure. Impressionism is a later label: Debussy disliked it, and Ravel and Satie were moving elsewhere.

## The historical setting

Paris under the Third Republic was Europe's art market: salons, galleries, publishers and theatres clustered together, while the Conservatoire still taught traditional harmony. The Expositions of 1889 and 1900 brought Javanese gamelan and Japanese prints; the Ballets Russes arrived in 1909. Symbolist poetry favoured suggestion over statement, and Debussy set Mallarmé's faun as a prelude. The First World War ended the era.

## What you hear

Chords can exist for colour alone: ninths, elevenths, added sixths and parallel motion no longer have to resolve. The whole-tone scale divides the octave into six equal steps; pentatonic and church modes supply melodies that do not depend on a leading note. A bass often stays on the tonic or a pedal while harmony floats above. Rhythm avoids square accents, and scoring favours soft brass, low woodwind, harp and celesta. Melodies are short, fragmented, glinting like water, and timbre becomes the subject.

## Genres and forms

Piano preludes, études, arabesques, suites and character pieces are central; Pagodes from Debussy's Estampes uses pentatonic and gamelan-like sonority, while Ravel's Gaspard de la nuit pushes piano technique to its limit. For orchestra there are symphonic poems, the three Nocturnes and ballets; Debussy's Pelléas et Mélisande turns opera into quiet conversation. Chamber music and song use the same coloured harmony.

## Instruments and forces

The piano is the laboratory: the sustain pedal blends overtones, the soft pedal changes colour, and half-pedalling and layered touch produce haze. The orchestra expands woodwind and percussion, adding harp, celesta, glockenspiel, antique cymbals and muted brass; divided strings create shimmer. Gamelan's metallic timbre and pentatonic tuning shape Pagodes.

## Representative composers

- **Claude Debussy (1862–1918)** used whole-tone scales, parallel chords and free rhythm to loosen functional harmony, turning opera, orchestra and piano into arts of colour.
- **Maurice Ravel (1875–1937)** shared the colour but insisted on line, dance and classical form, moving towards neoclassicism.
- **Erik Satie (1866–1925)** anticipated minimalism and conceptual art with the static harmony and odd titles of the Gymnopédies; he was Debussy's contemporary and friend.

## What to listen for

Stop looking for a theme. Hear whether the bass stays put, whether chords refuse to resolve, whether melody is assembled from short cells. Then listen to colour: harp harmonics, celesta's metal, the nasal tone of mutes. The five examples at the end offer gamelan pentatonic, moonlight, static harmony, watery virtuosity and a pavane.

## China in the same years

In China the same years run from the late Qing into the early Republic. School songs rose in the 1900s, with Shen Xingong and Li Shutong fitting Chinese words to Japanese and Western tunes; the New Culture Movement promoted vernacular writing and aesthetic education. Peking opera reached its peak, while kunqu and regional opera continued in the countryside.
