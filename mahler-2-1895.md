## 时间，与当时的地点

1895年12月13日，柏林，柏林爱乐乐团，马勒亲自指挥《第二交响曲》全曲首演。同年3月，理查·施特劳斯已指挥过前三个乐章。作品写于1888至1894年间，末乐章用了克洛普施托克的《复活》颂歌，马勒自己又补写了歌词。

## 事情本身

第一乐章是一首葬礼进行曲，低音弦乐与铜管把英雄送进坟墓；第二、三乐章像短暂的间奏，舞曲与讽刺轮流出现；末乐章突然打开，远处的铜管、鼓声、管风琴与合唱一起加入，最后唱出“复活，是的，你将复活”。马勒把独唱、合唱与巨大的乐队放在同一个空间里，让交响曲承担宗教式的仪式功能。

## 之后不一样了

交响曲不再只是四个乐章的器乐形式，而可以是一部关于生死、信仰与整个人生的“世界图景”。合唱终章、远处乐队与突然的空间转换，成为马勒后来交响曲的基本语汇，也为二十世纪的肖斯塔科维奇、布里顿等人打开了门。首演票房并不好，但作品很快成为马勒最常演的交响曲之一。

## 听一段转折

先听第一乐章的低音，像脚步一步一步走向墓地。再听末乐章的远处铜管，声音从舞台外传来，像另一个世界在敲门。最后听合唱进入，所有声部一起向上，乐队在下面托住。三段音乐把死亡、距离与复活排成一条线。

## When, and where

13 December 1895, Berlin, with the Berlin Philharmonic and Mahler conducting the first complete performance of his Second Symphony. In March of the same year Richard Strauss had conducted the first three movements. The work was written between 1888 and 1894. Its finale uses Klopstock's ode Die Auferstehung, with additional words by Mahler.

## The event itself

The first movement is a funeral march, low strings and brass carrying the hero to the grave. The second and third movements are brief interludes, dance and irony taking turns. Then the finale opens suddenly: offstage brass, drums, organ and chorus enter together, and the words "Rise again, yes, you shall rise again" are finally sung. Mahler placed soloists, chorus and a huge orchestra in one space and gave the symphony a religious, ritual function.

## What was different after

The symphony was no longer only a four-movement instrumental form; it could be a "world picture" of life, death and belief. A choral finale, distant bands and sudden changes of space became basic to Mahler's later symphonies and opened a door for Shostakovich and Britten. The premiere sold poorly, yet the work soon became one of Mahler's most performed symphonies.

## Hear the turn

Begin with the low bass of the first movement, like steps walking toward a grave. Then the offstage brass in the finale, sounding from beyond the platform like another world knocking. Finally the chorus enters, every part rising while the orchestra holds it from below. Three stretches line up death, distance and resurrection.
