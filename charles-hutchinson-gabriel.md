## 生平一笔

**查尔斯·哈钦森·加布里埃尔**（1856–1932）生于爱荷华州威尔顿，基本靠自学，年轻时在各地教唱歌、在教会弹管风琴，后来到芝加哥定居，为多家出版社编赞美诗集，也大量写曲。他长期在教会唱诗班任指挥，熟悉现场需要什么。《His Eye Is on the Sparrow》（1905）、《In Lovingkindness Jesus Came》（1905）、《Brighten the Corner Where You Are》（1913）与《Since Jesus Came into My Heart》（1914）让他成为那个时代最常被唱到的福音诗歌作者之一。

## 他生活的城市

二十世纪初的美国福音诗歌，已经从布道会走进唱诗班、主日学与家庭。它比十九世纪的赞美诗更强调独唱与副歌的对比，也更愿意借用流行歌曲的节奏：附点、切分、短促的重复句。加布里埃尔正好处在这个转折上，他既写曲也编集，知道一首歌在印刷、教唱与现场演唱三个环节里要怎样存活。**他的作品是教会音乐，不是音乐会曲目**，功能先行，但写法并不粗糙。

## 听什么

先听**主歌与副歌的性格差**。《His Eye Is on the Sparrow》主歌近乎说话，音区偏低，副歌突然轻快，附点节奏让“我歌唱”这句话跳起来；这个转折就是全曲的心脏。再听**一次上行做什么**。《In Lovingkindness Jesus Came》的副歌第一句是全曲最高点，之后逐句下降，像被举起又放下，听和声在高点怎样撑住。最后听齐唱的设计。《Brighten the Corner Where You Are》像进行曲，副歌音域窄、句子短、重复多，会众不用排练也能跟上。三首都是管风琴改编，原版是独唱加钢琴或小乐队伴奏。这些歌的伴奏通常很薄，人声才是主角；副歌常由四部合唱重复，最后一遍往往加高八度。

## 代表作品

- 歌曲：《His Eye Is on the Sparrow》《In Lovingkindness Jesus Came》《Brighten the Corner Where You Are》《Since Jesus Came into My Heart》
- 编集：多部主日学与福音诗歌集
- 其他：男声四重唱与合唱改编

## 留下的痕迹

加布里埃尔的歌通过歌本、唱诗班与后来的广播进入二十世纪美国人的耳朵，《His Eye Is on the Sparrow》更成为爵士与流行歌手常唱的曲目，从教堂跨到音乐厅。他把福音诗歌的独唱与副歌写成一种可复制的模板，后来的乡村福音、南方福音与当代敬拜音乐都在这个模板上工作。他也写男声四重唱与合唱改编，供教会随时取用。

## A life in brief

**Charles Hutchinson Gabriel** (1856–1932) was born in Wilton, Iowa, and was largely self-taught. As a young man he taught singing schools and played organ in churches, then settled in Chicago, where he edited hymnals for several publishers and wrote a great deal of music. His Eye Is on the Sparrow (1905), In Lovingkindness Jesus Came (1905), Brighten the Corner Where You Are (1913) and Since Jesus Came into My Heart (1914) made him one of the most sung gospel-song writers of his era.

## The city he worked in

By the early twentieth century the American gospel song had moved from the revival meeting into choirs, Sunday schools and homes. It stressed the contrast of solo verse and refrain more than the older hymn did, and borrowed popular-song rhythm: dotted figures, syncopation, short repeated phrases. Gabriel stands exactly at that turn, writing tunes and editing books, knowing how a song survives printing, teaching and performance. **His work is church music, not concert repertoire**; function comes first, though the craft is not crude.

## What to hear

Hear first **the difference in character between verse and refrain**. The verse of His Eye Is on the Sparrow is almost speech, low in the voice; the refrain turns light, and dotted rhythm makes "I sing" jump. That turn is the heart of the song. Then hear **what one ascent does**. In In Lovingkindness Jesus Came the first line of the refrain is the highest point of the piece, and each later phrase steps down, as if lifted and set back; listen for how the harmony holds the peak. Finally hear the design for unison singing. Brighten the Corner Where You Are behaves like a march: narrow range, short phrases, much repetition, so a congregation can join without rehearsal. All three tracks are organ arrangements; the originals are solo voice with piano or small band.

## Key works

- Songs: His Eye Is on the Sparrow, In Lovingkindness Jesus Came, Brighten the Corner Where You Are, Since Jesus Came into My Heart
- Editions: several Sunday-school and gospel hymnals
- Other: male-quartet and choral arrangements

## What remains

Gabriel's songs reached twentieth-century American ears through songbooks, choirs and later radio, and His Eye Is on the Sparrow became a favourite of jazz and popular singers, crossing from church to concert hall. He turned the gospel verse and refrain into a repeatable template, and country gospel, Southern gospel and contemporary worship have all worked on it since.
