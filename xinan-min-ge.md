## 时间与地点

西南民歌分布在四川、云南、贵州与重庆的坝子、山地和河谷。汉族山歌与彝、白、苗、侗、傣等民族的歌调交错，赶马、插秧、船运与节庆各有歌种，方言与地形一起塑造了唱法。

## 音乐特征

山歌常用羽调式，句幅长，起句高亢，句尾下滑并带装饰。四川山歌多四句体，云南弥渡一带的长句常加衬词。号子一领众和，节奏紧凑。花灯调接近小调，二拍子，适合舞蹈。山歌之外，薅秧歌与船工号子也常用一领众和。

## 代表曲目

《槐花几时开》《尖尖山》《弥渡山歌》《贵州山歌》《放马山歌》是常见样本，各地唱词常随场景替换。

## 听什么

先听高腔起句与句尾下滑，再听长句中的换气。号子听领合，花灯听锣鼓与舞步。羽调式的暗淡色彩是这一带的共同底色。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把西南民歌都当成少数民族歌。汉族山歌与号子数量很大。也不能把经过现代整理、改编的歌曲当作原始民歌，旋律来源与改编版本要分开看。

## Time and place

Southwest China's folk songs are found across the basins, mountains and river valleys of Sichuan, Yunnan, Guizhou and Chongqing. Han mountain songs interlace with the traditions of Yi, Bai, Miao, Dong and Dai peoples. Horse driving, rice planting, river transport and festivals each have their own songs, with dialect and terrain shaping the singing.

## Musical features

Mountain songs often use yu mode, with long phrases, a high opening and a decorated falling close. Sichuan songs tend to have four phrases, while the long lines around Midu in Yunnan add vocables. Work songs set a lead against a chorus in tight rhythm. Huadeng tunes sit near xiaodiao, in duple time and suited to dance. Beyond mountain songs, weeding songs and boatmen's songs also set a lead against a chorus.

## Repertoire

When Will the Locust Flower Open, Pointed Mountain, Midu Mountain Song, Guizhou Mountain Song and Horse Herding Song are common examples. Words are often exchanged to fit the occasion.

## What to listen for

Hear the high opening and the falling close first, then the breath inside a long phrase. In work songs follow lead and chorus; in huadeng, the gongs and steps. The darker colour of yu mode is the shared ground of the region.

## Common misunderstandings

A common mistake is to treat all southwest song as minority music. Han mountain songs and work songs are numerous. Nor should a modern arrangement be taken for an original folk song; the melodic source and the arranged version need to be kept apart.
