::: zh
1923年，勋伯格完成作品25号钢琴组曲。十二个半音排成音列，整首作品由音列及其变形生成，调性中心第一次被彻底取消。

## 时间与地点

1923年，维也纳。勋伯格在1921至1923年间写作作品25号，1924年出版并首演。作品23号五首钢琴曲与作品24号小夜曲已逼近这条路径，作品25号是第一首完整的十二音作品。

## 发生了什么

勋伯格把十二个音级排成固定顺序，称为原型音列。作曲家可用原型、逆行、倒影与逆行倒影，再移到十二个音高，得到四十八种形式。写作不再问主音在哪里，而是问音列如何分段、对位、分配给两只手。音列可拆成三音或四音动机，和声由音列重叠形成。

## 改变了什么

音乐的组织单位从调性关系变成音列关系，不协和不再需要解决。十二音技法让无调性写作获得结构，也给整体序列主义提供起点。听众失去调性路标，只能跟着动机、音区与音色辨认形式。战后序列主义由此出发。

## 听这个转折

先听作品25号组曲，注意加沃特、缪塞特与吉格如何被音列重写。再听作品23号五首钢琴曲，它是技法成形前的临界点。最后听韦伯恩作品27号变奏曲，听十二音原则如何延伸到节奏与音色。

::: en
In 1923 Schoenberg completed the Suite for Piano, Op. 25. Twelve semitones were ordered into a row, and it grew from that row and its transformations; tonality was replaced.

## Time and place

Vienna, 1923. Schoenberg wrote Op. 25 between 1921 and 1923, publishing and premiering it in 1924. Earlier works had approached the method; Op. 25 was the first composed entirely with twelve-tone technique.

## What happened

Schoenberg fixed the twelve pitch classes in a prime row. A composer could use the prime, retrograde, inversion and retrograde inversion at twelve levels, giving forty-eight forms. The question was no longer the tonic but how the row was segmented, combined and divided between the hands. Rows broke into three- or four-note motives, and harmony arose from overlapping forms.

## What changed

The organising unit moved from tonal relations to row relations, and dissonance no longer needed resolution. Twelve-tone technique gave atonal writing structure and seeded integral serialism. Listeners lost tonal signposts and followed motives and timbre. Postwar serialism followed.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the Suite, Op. 25, and notice how gavotte, musette and gigue are rewritten by the row. Then the Five Pieces, Op. 23, the threshold before the method. Finally Webern's Variations, Op. 27, where twelve-tone discipline extends into rhythm and timbre.
