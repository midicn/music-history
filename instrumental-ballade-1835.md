::: zh
1830年代中期，肖邦把"叙事曲"这个词从带词的歌谣借到钢琴上。没有文字，却要讲一个故事。

## 时间与地点

1831至1842年，巴黎。肖邦写出四首钢琴叙事曲，第一首1835年完成、次年在巴黎与莱比锡出版，第四首1842年出版。此前"叙事曲"指有词的民歌或叙事歌，舒伯特用它讲鬼故事与传说。

## 发生了什么

肖邦保留了歌谣"讲故事"的姿态，却去掉了文字：主题先出现，经过变形、分裂、重组，在结尾以新的面貌回来；曲式不照搬奏鸣曲式，而是在变奏、回旋与三段体之间自由移动。第一首的尾声把开头温柔的旋律变成激烈的宣叙，第四首把同一动机在多个调上推来推去，最后才回到主调。肖邦本人没有留下明确的文学程序，他与密茨凯维奇的联系来自舒曼等人的说法，属于接受史，不宜当作作曲家的说明书。四首长度都在十分钟上下，靠速度、织体与调性的变化维持张力；主题的变形不靠文字提示，只靠这些手段。

## 改变了什么

器乐获得了"叙事"的合法性，不必依附标题或文字。叙事曲成为钢琴文献的独立体裁，李斯特、勃拉姆斯、格里格、福雷都写过。演奏者也被要求处理"角色"与"情节"：同一主题要在不同段落里改变性格，而不只是变奏装饰。演奏传统也随之变化，钢琴家开始把整套叙事曲当作一场音乐会的骨架。标题从不解释情节，听者自己补故事。四首之间没有共同的情节，只有共同的姿态。

## 听这个转折

先听第一号的开头与结尾，同一旋律从柔到烈；再听第二号，快慢两种性格来回切换，像对话；最后听第四号的结尾，几段材料在最后几分钟里被重新排列。库中四首都是钢琴录音，注意主题每次回来时被改了多少。

::: en
In the mid-1830s Chopin borrowed the word ballade from the sung narrative and gave it to the piano. There is no text, yet a story is expected.

## Time and place

Paris, between 1831 and 1842. Chopin wrote four piano ballades, the first completed in 1835 and published the next year in Paris and Leipzig, the fourth published in 1842. Before that, a ballade meant a sung folk narrative, and Schubert used the form for ghost stories and legends.

## What happened

Chopin kept the storytelling posture of the ballad but removed the words. A theme appears, is transformed, split and reassembled, and returns at the end in a new shape. The form does not follow sonata pattern but moves freely among variation, rondo and three-part design. The coda of the First turns the opening's gentle tune into a fierce declamation; the Fourth pushes one motive through many keys before returning home. Chopin left no explicit literary programme, and his link with Mickiewicz comes from Schumann and later writers, which belongs to reception rather than to the composer's instructions. Each lasts about ten minutes, held together by changes of speed, texture and key.

## What changed

Instrumental music gained the right to narrate without a title or a text. The ballade became an independent piano genre, taken up by Liszt, Brahms, Grieg and Faure. Players were asked to handle characters and plot, changing the personality of one theme across sections rather than merely decorating it.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the opening and coda of the First, one melody turning from soft to fierce. Then the Second, where fast and slow characters alternate like a dialogue. Finally the coda of the Fourth, its materials rearranged in the last minutes. All four are piano recordings; notice how much the theme has changed each time it returns.
