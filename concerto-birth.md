::: zh
一件乐器对着整个乐队说话，乐队再把话接回去。协奏曲把这种对抗与应答写成一种体裁，让巴洛克的器乐第一次有了舞台上的主角。

## 时间与地点

约1680至1710年，意大利。科雷利在罗马写下大协奏曲，托雷利在博洛尼亚推动独奏协奏曲，维瓦尔第在威尼斯把三乐章与全奏回归固定下来。

## 发生了什么

协奏曲最初指多个声部一起竞奏，后来分成两类。大协奏曲让一小群独奏者与全奏组对比，独奏协奏曲让一件乐器与乐队对抗。快慢快三乐章、全奏主题的回归、独奏的装饰与炫技，构成基本语法。通奏低音仍在下面托住和声，但听觉重心已经移到独奏与全奏的交替。

## 改变了什么

器乐不再只是声乐的影子，协奏曲成为公共音乐会的核心曲目。独奏家开始拥有明星地位，作曲家为特定演奏者写作；出版市场把协奏曲变成畅销体裁。它也为古典协奏曲与交响曲的对话结构铺路，乐队从此学会回答。

## 听这个转折

先听科雷利的大协奏曲，独奏组与全奏组的强弱对比；再听维瓦尔第《春》，独奏小提琴模仿鸟鸣；最后听巴赫《第五勃兰登堡》，羽管键琴的长段独奏把伴奏者变成主角。

::: en
One instrument speaks to the orchestra, and the orchestra answers. The concerto turned that exchange into a genre and gave Baroque instrumental music a protagonist on stage.

## Time and place

Italy, roughly 1680–1710. Corelli wrote concerti grossi in Rome, Torelli pushed the solo concerto in Bologna, and Vivaldi fixed the three-movement shape and the return of the tutti in Venice.

## What happened

Concerto first meant several voices competing together, then split into two types. The concerto grosso sets a small group of soloists against the full band; the solo concerto sets one instrument against the orchestra. Three movements fast-slow-fast, the return of the tutti theme, and the soloist's ornaments and display make up the grammar. Continuo still holds the harmony below, but the ear moves to the alternation of solo and tutti.

## What changed

Instrumental music stopped being a shadow of vocal music, and the concerto became a core item in public concerts. Soloists acquired star status and composers wrote for particular players; the publishing market turned concertos into a best-selling genre. It also prepared the dialogue of the classical concerto and the symphony: the orchestra learned to answer.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Corelli's concerto grosso and the dynamic contrast between the solo group and the full band; then Vivaldi's Spring, where the solo violin imitates birds; finally Bach's Brandenburg No.5, where a long harpsichord solo turns the accompanist into the protagonist.
