## 出身与师承

**亨利·考威尔**（1897–1965）生于加州门洛帕克，是作曲家、钢琴家、教师与出版人。他基本靠自学，少年时和查尔斯·西格学作曲理论。1927 年他创办《新音乐》季刊，专门出版当代作品；1930 年代因一桩道德指控入狱数年，1942 年获赦。此后他在哥伦比亚大学任教，1965 年去世。

## 时代与位置

他是**美国实验音乐的第一代**。在艾夫斯还在自费印谱的时候，考威尔已经把钢琴当成可以改造的机器：用手掌与前臂压出音簇，用手伸进琴箱拨弦、刮弦，制造出当时记谱都困难的声音。他同时是理论家，《新音乐资源》讨论节奏比例与音簇，为后来的实验音乐提供了词汇。他晚年还写过二十部左右的交响曲，语气比早期的实验作品平静得多。

## 先听什么

先听**音簇的重量**。《动态运动》里整条前臂压下去，十几个音同时响，听它怎样在没有旋律的情况下做出方向与高潮。再听**机械的重复**，《织物》的节奏像织布机，听规律本身怎样成为主题。再听琴内的声音，他的另一批作品要求在琴弦上直接操作，音色介于竖琴与打击乐之间。最后听晚年的收敛，HC 463 的两首小品把音簇放进安静的和声里，听先锋技术怎样变成语气。

## 作品与写法

- 钢琴 《动态运动》、《织物》、《两首小品》HC 463、《女妖》、《风弦琴》
- 著作 《新音乐资源》
- 其他 交响曲十余部、室内乐、出版《新音乐》季刊

## 影响与误读

考威尔直接影响了约翰·凯奇与卢·哈里森，也把「扩展技巧」变成美国作曲的合法工具。他证明钢琴不必只发出钢琴的声音，这条思路后来通向预置钢琴、琴内演奏与噪音音乐。他的《新音乐》季刊出版了艾夫斯、瓦雷兹与凯奇的作品，这份功劳不在作曲之下。他一生为别人出版乐谱，自己的作品反而长期没有系统版本。

## Training and teachers

**Henry Cowell** (1897–1965) was born in Menlo Park, California, and worked as composer, pianist, teacher and publisher. He was largely self-taught and studied theory with Charles Seeger as a teenager. In 1927 he founded the quarterly New Music to publish contemporary scores; in the 1930s he served several years in prison on a morality charge and was pardoned in 1942. He later taught at Columbia University and died in 1965.

## His place

He belongs to **the first generation of American experimental music**. While Ives was still printing his own scores, Cowell treated the piano as a machine that could be modified: clusters pressed with palm and forearm, hands reaching inside the instrument to pluck and scrape strings, producing sounds that notation could barely capture. He was a theorist too, and New Musical Resources, with its discussion of rhythmic ratios and clusters, gave later experimenters their vocabulary. In later life he wrote some twenty symphonies, their manner calmer than the early experiments.

## Where to start

Listen first for **the weight of a cluster**. In Dynamic Motion a whole forearm goes down and a dozen notes sound at once; hear how it makes direction and climax without melody. Then **mechanical repetition**, where the rhythm of Fabric works like a loom and the pattern itself becomes the subject. Then sounds from inside the piano, since another group of pieces asks for direct work on the strings, producing a colour between harp and percussion. Finally late restraint — the two pieces of HC 463 set clusters inside quiet harmony, showing technique turning into tone of voice.

## Works and craft

- Piano: Dynamic Motion, Fabric, Two Pieces HC 463, The Banshee, Aeolian Harp
- Writings: New Musical Resources
- Other: some twenty symphonies, chamber music, the New Music quarterly

## Influence and misreadings

Cowell directly shaped John Cage and Lou Harrison and made extended technique a legitimate American tool. He proved the piano need not sound only like a piano, a line of thinking that leads to prepared piano, inside-the-piano playing and noise music. His New Music quarterly published Ives, Varese and Cage, a service to the music no smaller than his own composing. For decades his own scores lacked a systematic edition, while the composers he published became famous.
