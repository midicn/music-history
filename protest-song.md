## 问题

一首歌要怎样才能让几千人同时开口？抗议歌曲的秘诀不是复杂，而是可重复：短句、易记的副歌、能一领一合的停顿。它把私人的愤怒变成公共节奏，也让本来互不认识的人在副歌里临时成为同伴。

## 历史脉络

19世纪工人歌曲、废奴歌曲与禁酒歌曲多在集会与游行中产生，靠歌本和小册子传播。20世纪录音、广播与电视让一首歌一周内传遍全国；民权、反战与东欧剧变都把歌曲放在队伍最前面。流媒体时代，抗议歌曲又回到短视频与现场直播。

## 关键案例

《国际歌》由鲍狄埃在1871年写词、狄盖特后来谱曲，副歌的上升音型适合齐唱，后来成为多国工人运动的共同曲目。《Bella Ciao》原为意大利稻田女工的歌，二战成为反法西斯游击队的歌，2010年代又被全球示威者重新填词。《自由战吼》1862年在美国内战集会上走红，20世纪被工会与民权运动借去。三首歌都简单到可以边走边唱。

## 听什么

听领唱与齐唱的交替，听副歌是不是比主歌高。抗议歌曲的力量常在“下一句大家都知道”，也在于它敢把复杂的政治说成一句能记住的话。

## Question

How does a song make thousands open their mouths at once? The secret is repetition: short phrases, a memorable chorus, and pauses that let a leader and a crowd answer. Private anger becomes public rhythm.

## Historical background

Workers', abolitionist, and temperance songs were born in nineteenth-century meetings and marches and spread through songbooks. Radio, records, and television let a song reach a country in a week; civil rights, antiwar, and Eastern European movements put songs at the front.

## Key cases

The Internationale was written by Eugène Pottier in 1871 and set by Pierre Degeyter; its rising chorus suits unison singing and became common in labor movements. Bella Ciao began as an Italian rice-field song, became an anti-fascist partisan song in the Second World War, and was reworded by global demonstrators in the 2010s. The Battle Cry of Freedom was written for a Union rally in 1862 and borrowed by unions and civil rights campaigns. All three are simple enough to sing while walking.

## What to listen for

Listen to the alternation of leader and chorus, and whether the chorus sits higher than the verse. The power of a protest song lies in the line everyone already knows.
