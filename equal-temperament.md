::: zh
把八度切成十二个相等的半音，听上去是数学决定，实际上让所有调都能在同一件乐器上自由出现。巴赫用两卷《平均律》把这件事变成可听的日常。

## 时间与地点

十八世纪欧洲。巴赫在科滕时期编成《平均律键盘曲集》第一卷，题名页署1722年；这里说的是作品编成，不是当年在莱比锡正式出版。曲集中二十四个大小调各有一首前奏曲与赋格。严格十二平均律的普及要到十九世纪，随钢琴制造与调律标准化完成。

## 发生了什么

十二平均律把八度按2的十二次方根分成十二份，每个半音频率比相同。纯五度略窄、大三度略宽，换来的是任何调都不需要重新调音，转调与等音变换畅通无阻。巴赫的 well-tempered并不等于严格的十二平均律，而是让所有调都可用的循环调律；但它证明远调可以像C大调一样写作和聆听。

## 改变了什么

严格十二平均律让各调的对应音程比例一致，便于在固定音高乐器上转调与作等音转换。钢琴和管风琴没有品丝；它们靠调弦或调管设置音高，吉他等有品乐器则借助品位安排。管风琴至今也可采用历史调律，不能把所有乐器与全部调性音乐都归为同一种调律。代价是纯律的纯净三度被牺牲，收益是十二个调的自由。

::: en
Dividing the octave into twelve equal semitones sounds mathematical. In practice it let every key appear freely on one instrument, and Bach made the change audible in two books of preludes and fugues.

## Time and place

Eighteenth-century Europe. Bach compiled the first book of The Well-Tempered Clavier during his Köthen period; its title page is dated 1722. This marks compilation, not a Leipzig publication that year. The collection pairs a prelude with a fugue in each of the twenty-four keys. Strict equal temperament spread only in the nineteenth century, with piano manufacture and standardised tuning.

## What happened

Equal temperament divides the octave by the twelfth root of two, so every semitone has the same ratio. Fifths are slightly narrow and major thirds slightly wide; in return, no key needs retuning, and modulation works everywhere. Bach's well-tempered tuning was not strict equal temperament but a circulating system that made all keys usable. It proved that remote keys could be written and heard as naturally as C major.

## What changed

Strict equal temperament gives corresponding intervals the same ratios in each key, facilitating modulation and enharmonic exchange on fixed-pitch instruments. Pianos and organs have no frets: their strings or pipes are tuned, while fretted instruments use fret placement. Organs can still use historical temperaments; neither every instrument nor all tonal music follows a single tuning system. The price was the pure third of just intonation; the gain was the freedom of all twelve keys.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the C major prelude, then hear the fugues in C sharp major, F sharp major and A flat major. Remote keys no longer carry the tension of being out of tune; the subject holds in five or six sharps or flats. The versions are piano; listen for stable intonation, not old instruments' tuning differences.
