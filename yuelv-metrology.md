## 问题

音高需要一把尺。中国古人把尺、斗、斤挂在同一根竹管上：黄钟管长九寸，管内积为一龠，所容之黍的重量成为衡的基准。音准因此不只是耳朵的事，也是收税、造器与校准的事。

## 历史脉络

《吕氏春秋》说伶伦取竹制十二筒，听凤鸣而定十二律。汉代《律历志》把律、历与度量衡编进同一套制度，用累黍定尺。后人沿三分损益继续除，京房推到六十律，南朝钱乐之推到三百六十律。明代朱载堉在《律学新说》里算出新法密率，把八度开十二次方，十二律从此等距，比欧洲发表更早。

## 关键转折

转折在于律管从乐器变成标准器。它一旦承担长度与重量，改律就等于改制度。朱载堉的方案没能在明代落地，却证明十二平均律可以被算出来。

## 听什么

先听琴歌，五声落点稳，四度五度的跳进来自弦长比例；再听《茉莉花》，同一套骨架换了地方语气；最后听巴赫赋格，十二个调自由来去，那是等分八度之后的从容。

## The question

Pitch needs a ruler. In early China the bamboo pipe that fixed a note also fixed the chi and the jin: the Huangzhong pipe was nine inches long, its bore held one yue of millet, and the weight of that millet set the standard for weight. Tuning was as much a matter of taxation and casting as of the ear.

## Historical arc

The Lüshi Chunqiu tells of Ling Lun cutting twelve pipes and naming the twelve lü after birdsong. The Han treatise tied pitch, calendar and measures into one system, with millet grains defining the ruler. Later theorists kept dividing the chain of fifths: Jing Fang reached sixty lü, Qian Lezhi three hundred and sixty. In 1584 Zhu Zaiyu took the twelfth root of two, making the twelve lü equal earlier than the European publication.

## The turning point

The turn came when the pipe became a standard instrument of the state. Once it carried length and weight, changing the tuning meant changing the system. Zhu's solution was not adopted in his lifetime, yet it proved that equal temperament could be calculated, not merely heard.

## What to listen for

Begin with a qin song, where pentatonic landing points and the fourths and fifths follow string ratios. Then hear Molihua, the same skeleton in a regional accent. Finally a Bach fugue moves through all twelve keys with the ease that equal division allows.
