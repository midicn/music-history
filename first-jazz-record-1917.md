## 时间，与当时的地点

1917年2月26日，纽约，维克多留声机公司。新奥尔良乐队“Original Dixieland Jass Band”录下《Livery Stable Blues》与《Dixieland Jass Band One-Step》。

## 事情经过

这支乐队由白人乐手组成，来自新奥尔良，在纽约的舞厅与餐馆演出。录音时他们按合奏的固定分工演奏，短号领旋律，单簧管绕行，长号滑奏，节奏组推进。两面在三月发行，销量惊人，通常被称为第一张爵士唱片。此前黑人乐手的爵士几乎没有录音机会，音乐主要靠现场与口传流动。

## 之后不一样了

爵士从地方现场变成全国商品，舞步、乐队编制与“爵士”这个名字一起被固定下来。唱片公司发现这种音乐能卖，随后才有了针对黑人听众的 race records 市场。即兴从一次性的现场事件变成可以被模仿、比较与收藏的文本，后来的乐手也从这里学会依靠唱片。

## 听一段转折

先听这首署名乐队的《Dixieland》，旋律与切分的骨架还在，只是由钢琴奏出；再听乔普林的《Elite Syncopations》，那是录音之前的拉格钢琴，切分写在谱面上；最后听《The Ragtime Dance》，听舞曲节奏怎样从钢琴移到乐队。唱片把一瞬间变成了教材。

## When, and where

26 February 1917, New York, at the Victor Talking Machine Company. The New Orleans band called the Original Dixieland Jass Band recorded Livery Stable Blues and Dixieland Jass Band One-Step.

## The sequence of events

The band consisted of white New Orleans musicians who played New York dance halls and restaurants. They used the fixed division of a collective front line. The cornet led the melody, the clarinet wound around it, the trombone slid, and the rhythm section pushed. Released in March, the two sides sold remarkably well and are usually called the first jazz records. Black musicians had almost no chance to record; their music travelled by performance and memory.

## What was different after

Jazz moved from local event to national commodity, fixing dance steps, band lineup and the name itself. Record companies learned this music could sell, and a race records market followed. Improvisation turned from an event into a text that could be imitated, compared and collected. Later players learned from records.

## Hear the turn

Begin with the Dixieland attributed to the band, where the melodic and syncopated frame survives at the piano. Then Joplin's Elite Syncopations, ragtime piano from before the recording, with syncopation in the score. Finally The Ragtime Dance, where dance rhythm moves from piano to band. A record turned a moment into a lesson.
