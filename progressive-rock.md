::: zh
前卫摇滚把专辑当作一部作品来写，用长结构、变拍与古典语汇扩展摇滚。

## 起源与年代
1960 年代末的英国，乐队在迷幻、古典与爵士的影响下写超过十分钟的组曲。1969 年的《In the Court of the Crimson King》是常见起点，1970 年代 Yes、Genesis、平克·弗洛伊德与 ELP 把它做成产业。

## 听辨特征
先听结构，段落多次切换，拍号从四四变到七八或五八。键盘铺长音，吉他少用布鲁斯推弦，更多是清音与音色实验。器乐段长，主题反复后变形。歌词常写神话、科幻与寓言。

## 代表人物
King Crimson 把铜管与不协和音带进来，Yes 用对位与高音人声，Genesis 靠叙事与舞台形象，平克·弗洛伊德把空间与录音技术当乐器。

## 谱系
上承迷幻摇滚、古典乐与爵士融合，下接新前卫、后摇与数学摇滚；它也把录音室当作作曲工具。

## 常见误解
前卫摇滚不等于炫技比赛，许多段落靠简洁动机展开。也不等于都长，三分钟的单曲同样可以前卫。把它当 1970 年代怀旧，会漏掉它对后摇的影响。

::: en
Progressive rock treats the album as one work, extending rock through long forms, shifting meters and classical vocabulary.

## Origins and era
In late-1960s Britain, bands under the influence of psychedelia, classical music and jazz wrote suites longer than ten minutes. In the Court of the Crimson King (1969) is a usual starting point; in the 1970s Yes, Genesis, Pink Floyd and Emerson, Lake & Palmer turned the approach into an album industry.

## What you hear
Listen for structure. Sections change often, and meters move from 4/4 to 7/8 or 5/8. Keyboards hold long tones, guitar avoids blues bends in favour of clean colour, and instrumental passages develop a theme until it changes shape. Lyrics often turn to myth, science fiction and fable.

## Key figures
King Crimson brought brass and dissonance, Yes used counterpoint and high voices, Genesis worked through narrative and stage characters, and Pink Floyd made space and recording technology into instruments.

## Lineage
Psychedelic rock, classical music and jazz fusion feed in; neo-prog, post-rock and math rock come out. The studio became a composing tool.

## Common misunderstandings
Progressive rock is not a technique contest, since many passages grow from a simple motive. Nor must it be long, as a three-minute single can be progressive. Treating it as 1970s nostalgia misses its hold on post-rock.
