## 问题

现场音乐消散，每次不同。录音要解决保存与重复，声音一旦能被保存，新问题就出现：录音是演奏的记录，还是独立作品？剪辑过的演奏还算演奏吗？

## 历史脉络

1877年，爱迪生用锡箔圆筒录下《玛丽有只小羊羔》。1890年代，唱盘与唱片进入市场。1902年，卡鲁索为留声机公司录下咏叹调；莱翁卡瓦洛1904年为他写的《晨曲》几乎为录音而作。1920年代，电气录音与麦克风扩大频带，爵士借广播和唱片进入家庭。1948年，密纹唱片让专辑变长，磁带让剪辑成为可能；1960年代，多轨录音把录音室变成乐器。1990年代以后，数字文件与流媒体让音乐变成可搜索的数据。

## 关键转折

第一次转折是可重复，同一段演奏可无数次播放，演奏家可跨洲成名。第二次转折是可编辑，磁带与多轨录音让演奏可以拼接、修补，录音成为独立作品。第三次转折是可携带，从唱片到流媒体，听音乐从社交事件变成私人背景。

## 听什么

先听《晨曲》的旋律，想象它为男高音与留声机而写；再听《Maple Leaf Rag》，拉格泰姆靠乐谱与唱片传播；最后听《哥德堡变奏曲》的咏叹调，比较不同年代录音的触键。库中版本是 MIDI 转录，注意演奏习惯而非音质。

## The question

Live music vanishes, and every performance differs. Recording had to solve storage and repetition. Once sound could be stored, questions appeared. Is a recording a document or a work in its own right? If a performance is spliced, is it still a performance?

## Historical arc

In 1877 Edison recorded Mary Had a Little Lamb on a tinfoil cylinder. In the 1890s discs replaced cylinders. In 1902 Caruso recorded arias for the Gramophone company, and Leoncavallo's Mattinata, written for him in 1904, was almost made for the machine. In the 1920s electrical recording widened the frequency range, and jazz and dance music entered homes through radio and discs. In 1948 the LP and tape made albums and editing possible; in the 1960s multitrack recording turned the studio into an instrument. From the 1990s digital files and streaming turned music into searchable data.

## The turning point

The first turn was repeatability. One performance could be played countless times, and a performer's name could travel across continents. The second turn was editability. Tape and multitrack recording allowed splicing, patching and layering, so a recording became an independent work. The third turn was portability. From discs to streaming, listening moved from a social event to a private background.

## What to listen for

Begin with the melody of Mattinata and imagine it written for a tenor and a gramophone. Then hear Maple Leaf Rag, which travelled through score, piano roll and disc. Finally hear the Goldberg aria and compare how recordings from different decades change touch. The library versions are MIDI transcriptions, so listen for habits rather than sound quality.
