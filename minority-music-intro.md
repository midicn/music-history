## 时间与地点

中国五十五个少数民族分布在高原、草原、绿洲、山地与海岛。音乐与语言、生计、信仰绑在一起，牧区有长调，稻作区有多声部合唱，绿洲有套曲，山地有铜鼓与芦笙。20 世纪以来，采录、院校与非遗制度改变了它的流传方式。

## 音乐特征

民歌分山歌、劳动歌、仪式歌与抒情歌；器乐有马头琴、冬不拉、葫芦丝、铜鼓；歌舞与说唱、戏曲常连在一起。侗族大歌、壮族三声部民歌用多声织体，蒙古族长调用诺古拉，维吾尔木卡姆是大型套曲。传承主要靠口传与仪式场合。

## 代表曲目

《辽阔的草原》《阿拉木汗》《侗族大歌》，以及藏族与蒙古族的《格萨尔》说唱，都是常被提到的样本。

## 听什么

先听语言的重音与声调怎样变成旋律，再听场合决定的形式。多声部听声部怎样进入，长调听长音怎样拐弯。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把少数民族音乐当成一种统一的风格。五十五个民族的语言与音乐差别很大；也不是都只用五声，更不是只为旅游表演而存在。

## Time and place

China's fifty-five minority peoples live across high plateaus, grasslands, oases, mountains and islands. Their music is bound to language, livelihood and belief: long song in pastoral areas, choral singing in rice-growing regions, suites in the oases, bronze drums and reed pipes in the mountains. Since the twentieth century, fieldwork, conservatories and heritage listing have changed how it is passed on.

## Musical features

Song repertory includes mountain songs, work songs, ritual songs and lyric songs; instruments include morin khuur, dombra, hulusi and bronze drums; dance, narrative singing and opera often run together. Dong grand song and Zhuang three-part singing use polyphonic textures, Mongolian long song uses nogula, and Uyghur muqam is a large suite. Transmission depends on oral teaching and ritual occasions.

## Repertoire

Liaokuo de Caoyuan, Alamuhan, the Dong grand song, and the Gesar epics of Tibetan and Mongolian singers are cited examples.

## What to listen for

Hear first how the stresses and tones of the language become melody, then how occasion shapes the form. In polyphony, listen for the order in which parts enter; in long song, for how a sustained note bends.

## Common misunderstandings

Minority music is often treated as a single style. The languages and musics of fifty-five peoples differ widely; not all use pentatonic scales, and none exists only for tourist shows.
