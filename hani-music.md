## 时间与地点

哈尼族聚居在云南红河、普洱、西双版纳与玉溪，元阳、绿春、红河、金平、江城是主要分布区。村寨沿哀牢山梯田而建，栽秧、薅草、收割按节令进行。哈尼语属汉藏语系藏缅语族，方言差别大，没有通行的传统文字。歌曲在梯田、村寨、婚礼与节庆中唱，多声部民歌在红河、元阳一带尤为集中。

## 发生了什么

哈尼族的口传音乐与稻作紧密相连。栽秧时节，人们在田里唱栽秧歌，一人起句，众人应和，句幅随劳动的快慢伸缩。哈尼哈巴是叙事歌，唱迁徙、创世与家族谱系，常在婚礼与节庆的夜里由长者领唱。多声部民歌在二十世纪后期被音乐研究者记录，领唱与持续低音叠合，形成四声部以上的织体。关于各地多声部的具体声部数与唱法，公开材料并不一致，这里只取较通行的描述。

## 音乐特征

旋律以五声为骨架，音域不宽，句尾常下滑。栽秧歌的节奏跟着脚步与手势，强拍落在落秧的动作上。多声部不是功能和声，而是各声部按自己的句法先后进入，低音声部常持续一个音，上方声部加花。巴乌用铜簧，音色柔，常吹情歌；口弦音量小，靠口腔选泛音；草杆与树叶是随手可得的乐器。

## 听什么

先听栽秧歌的起句与应和怎样错开，再听低声部持续时上方怎样加花。chinafolk-003944 是栽秧歌，能听到劳动歌的句读；chinafolk-001366 是云南的问答民歌，可听问句与答句的接法。essen-008777 是白族对唱，essen-000032 是弥渡山歌，只作滇西、滇中的声腔参照，不是哈尼族录音。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把哈尼族音乐等同于梯田上的几声吆喝，或以为多声部民歌遍布所有哈尼村寨。多声部集中在特定区域，栽秧歌、哈尼哈巴、情歌与儿歌各有场合。也不是所有哈尼歌都用巴乌伴奏，清唱与口弦同样常见。这些歌仍在劳动与仪式中唱，不是为录音棚准备的民俗展示。

## Time and place

The Hani live in Honghe, Pu'er, Xishuangbanna and Yuxi in Yunnan, mainly in Yuanyang, Luchun, Honghe, Jinping and Jiangcheng. Villages sit among the Ailao Mountain terraces, and transplanting, weeding and harvest follow the seasons. Hani belongs to the Tibeto-Burman branch of Sino-Tibetan, with strong dialect differences and no widely used traditional script. Songs are sung in terraces, villages, weddings and festivals; polyphonic songs are concentrated around Honghe and Jianshui.

## What happened

Hani oral music is tied closely to rice farming. At transplanting time people sing in the fields, one voice starting and others answering, with phrase lengths stretching or shortening to the work. Hani Haba is narrative song, telling migration, creation and family genealogies, usually led by elders at weddings and festival nights. Polyphonic songs were documented by researchers in the late twentieth century; a lead voice overlaps with a sustained low part to make textures of four or more parts. Published accounts differ on the exact number of parts and local practice, so this entry gives the common description.

## Musical features

Melody has a pentatonic frame, a modest range and falling phrase ends. Transplanting-song rhythm follows feet and hands, with the strong beat on the planting stroke. The polyphony is not functional harmony: parts enter by their own phrasing, a low voice often holds one note, and upper voices ornament. The bawu uses a metal reed and a soft tone, often for love songs; the mouth harp is quiet and selects overtones with the mouth; straw reeds and leaves are everyday instruments.

## What to listen for

Hear how the lead and answer offset each other, then how upper parts ornament over a held bass. Chinafolk-003944 is a transplanting song where the work-song phrasing is audible; chinafolk-001366 is a Yunnanese question-and-answer song, useful for hearing how an answer picks up a question. Essen-008777 is Bai antiphonal singing and essen-000032 is a Midu mountain song, regional references from western and central Yunnan, not Hani recordings.

## Common misunderstandings

Hani music is often reduced to a few shouts in the terraces, or polyphony is assumed to exist in every Hani village. Polyphony is localised, and transplanting songs, Hani Haba, love songs and children's songs have separate occasions. Nor is every Hani song accompanied by the bawu; unaccompanied singing and the mouth harp are equally common. These songs are still sung at work and in ritual, not staged folklore for a studio.
