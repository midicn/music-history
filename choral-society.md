::: zh
十九世纪的市民自己凑钱租谱、请乐队，把清唱剧从宫廷剧院搬进市政厅。

## 时间与地点

1791 年，柏林歌唱学院成立，由法施主持，成员多为市民与官员。1815 年，波士顿的亨德尔与海顿协会成立。1846 年，门德尔松《以利亚》在伯明翰音乐节首演，合唱队由外地协会的歌手组成。1859 年，伦敦举办亨德尔百年纪念，数千人同唱。

## 发生了什么

协会由商人、职员、教师与他们的家属组成，每周排练，交会费，租场地，自己选理事与司库。协会自建图书室，抄谱与租谱并行，曲目靠几代人的手抄本传下来。协会之间互相借谱，曲目在城际流动。演出时请职业乐队与独唱，合唱部分自己承担。出版商配合发行廉价的钢琴伴奏谱与单首合唱分谱，声部可以分开练，指挥用钢琴给音。曲目以清唱剧为主，因为有合唱、有戏剧性却不需布景，内容也体面。女声唱高声部，男声唱低声部，混声合唱的编制在协会里定型，合唱队也成了城市里少数男女共同参与的公共团体。

## 改变了什么

演出从宫廷与歌剧院转到市民结社，听众不是买票的旁观者，而是参与者。一个城市的中产通过唱而学会经典，亨德尔与门德尔松成了共同记忆。合唱能力成为体面的标志，后来又被用来标记地方与民族身份。协会要管账、租谱、订音乐厅，合唱指挥从伴奏者变成训练者；跨城合唱节还带动了铁路旅行与乐谱销售。

## 听这个转折

先听《弥赛亚》的合唱段，声部进入清楚，和声以块状进行，业余团练几周就能站住。再听《以利亚》的群众场面，合唱代表人群，力度与歌词都更戏剧化。最后听门德尔松的两首宗教合唱，短小、无伴奏，适合分开排练。转折前，这类音乐属于宫廷礼拜与剧院；转折后，它属于交了会费的市民。听的时候留意合唱的进入是否整齐，那是业余团体最在意的指标。

Nineteenth-century citizens pooled money for scores and hired orchestras, moving oratorio from court theatre into the city hall.

## Time and place

Berlin, 1791, where Fasch founded the Sing-Akademie, its members mostly burghers and officials. Boston, 1815, the Handel and Haydn Society. Birmingham, 1846, the premiere of Mendelssohn's Elijah with a chorus drawn from provincial societies. London, 1859, the Handel centenary, with thousands singing together.

## What happened

Societies were made of merchants, clerks, teachers and their families. They rehearsed weekly, paid dues, rented rooms and elected their own committee and treasurer. Each built a library, copying and renting scores, so the repertory survived through generations of manuscript. For performances they hired professional players and soloists while singing the chorus themselves. Publishers supplied cheap vocal scores and separate chorus parts so sections could rehearse alone, with the conductor giving notes from the piano. Oratorio dominated because it had choruses and drama without staging, and its subject matter was respectable. Women took the upper parts and men the lower, fixing the mixed-voice layout.

## What changed

Performance moved from court and opera house to civic association, and listeners became participants rather than ticket buyers. A town's middle class learned the canon by singing it, with Handel and Mendelssohn held in common. Choral ability became a mark of respectability and later of local and national identity. Societies kept accounts, rented scores and booked halls, the chorus master grew from accompanist into trainer, and festivals across cities boosted rail travel and score sales.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with a chorus from Messiah, its entries clear and its harmony mostly blocklike, music an amateur group can steady in a few weeks. Then the crowd scenes of Elijah, where the chorus stands for a people and both dynamics and words turn dramatic. Finally Mendelssohn's two sacred choruses, short and unaccompanied, easy to rehearse by section. Before the turn this music belonged to court chapel and theatre; afterwards it belonged to dues-paying citizens. Listen for whether the entries are tidy, the measure amateur groups care about most.
