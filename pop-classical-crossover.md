::: zh
古典与流行不是两座孤岛，它们一直在互相借乐器、节奏与观众。

## 问题

谁在借用谁？借来之后，是融合还是收编？当音乐厅采用流行语汇，它是在开放，还是在驯服对手？

## 历史脉络

十九世纪末拉格泰姆进入钢琴沙龙，德彪西与萨蒂听到爵士前夕的切分。1920年代格什温把爵士写进音乐厅，欣德米特写拉格泰姆，魏尔把歌舞厅歌曲变成剧场音乐。伯恩斯坦在《西区故事》里把拉丁节奏、爵士和声与歌剧结构并置。战后，摇滚、采样与电影配乐继续双向流动。

## 关键转折

转折在录音与市场。唱片让流行歌曲进入古典听众的客厅，也让古典片段成为流行采样；音乐厅因此失去垄断，却获得新观众。融合常带来创造力，也带来谁更正宗的争吵。

## 听什么

先听格什温前奏曲的蓝调音，再听欣德米特拉格泰姆的机械切分，最后听《Mack the Knife》的剧场语气，比较三种跨界。

Classical and popular music are not two islands. They have long borrowed instruments, rhythms and audiences.

## The question

Who borrows from whom? Is the result fusion or appropriation? When the concert hall adopts popular language, is it opening up or domesticating a rival?

## Historical arc

Ragtime entered piano salons at the end of the nineteenth century, and Debussy and Satie heard the syncopation that preceded jazz. In the 1920s Gershwin brought jazz into the concert hall, Hindemith wrote ragtime, and Weill turned cabaret song into theatre music. Bernstein set Latin rhythm, jazz harmony and opera side by side in West Side Story. After the war, rock, sampling and film scoring kept the traffic moving.

## The turning point

Recording and the market changed the terms. Records carried popular song into classical living rooms and made classical fragments available for sampling. The concert hall lost its monopoly and gained new listeners. Crossover brings invention, and arguments about authenticity.

## What to listen for

Hear the blue notes in Gershwin's preludes, the mechanical syncopation of Hindemith's ragtime, and the theatrical tone of Mack the Knife.
