## 问题

殖民不只划地图，也重新安排耳朵。军乐、赞美诗、学校唱歌课把帝国的节奏带进被占领的地方；被殖民者用这些乐器写出自己的歌。谁的声音被当成“地方色彩”，谁又有权替别人收藏音乐？

## 历史脉络

18到19世纪欧洲军乐队随军队和贸易站扩散，铜管与鼓在殖民地城市形成新的公共声音。传教士把赞美诗翻成当地语言，学校教欧洲记谱法。20世纪唱片与广播把殖民地音乐做成商品，也录下反殖民歌曲。

## 关键案例

《不列颠万岁》1740年代写于伦敦，后来成为英国海军的非官方颂歌；《英国掷弹兵进行曲》随军团行进，在印度、非洲与北美留下军乐传统；《Aloha Oe》由夏威夷女王利留卡拉尼在1878年前后写作，1893年王国被推翻后成为失去主权的记忆，后来被旅游业改写成明信片。三首歌展示宣告、管理与收编。

## 听什么

## Question

Colonization did not only draw maps; it rearranged ears. Military bands, hymns, and school singing lessons brought imperial rhythm into occupied places. At the same time, the colonized wrote their own songs with those instruments. Who gets called local color, and who may collect another people's music?

## Historical background

European military bands spread with armies and trading posts in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, and brass and drums formed a new public sound in colonial cities. Missionaries translated hymns into local languages, and schools taught European notation. In the twentieth century, records and radio turned colonial music into a commodity while also recording anti-colonial songs.

## Key cases

Rule, Britannia! was written in London in the 1740s and became an unofficial anthem of the British navy. The British Grenadiers marched with regiments and left a band tradition in India, Africa, and North America. Aloha Oe was written by Queen Liliuokalani around 1878; after the kingdom was overthrown in 1893, it became a memory of lost sovereignty, then was softened into a tourist postcard. The three show proclamation, administration, and appropriation.

## What to listen for

Hear whether brass is standing in for an administrative order, and how a local melody emerges from under the chords. Exotic orchestration often turns living people into scenery.
