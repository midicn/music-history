## 时间与地点

湖南民歌分布在湘中、湘西与湘南，汉族与土家、苗、侗等民族的歌调互相影响。湘西山高，山歌高亢；湘水船运发达，号子一领众和；城镇与年节则流行小调与花鼓调。

## 音乐特征

山歌常用高腔起句，音区高，句尾下滑，衬词多。船工号子节奏短促，领唱与众人接应紧密。花鼓调与采茶调接近小调，二拍子，句幅整齐。羽调式与徵调式常见，四度、五度跳进和方言声调结合。

## 代表曲目

《澧水船工号子》《冷水泡茶慢慢浓》《古丈山歌》《望城山歌》是常见样本，花鼓戏的采茶调也常被单独演唱。

## 听什么

听山歌第一句怎样拔高，再听句尾怎样落下。号子要听领唱与众人接应的空隙。小调则可数板，注意衬词并不只是填充，它常决定句子的长短。山歌的句尾常带一声长叹，像把话说完又留住。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把湖南民歌都当成高腔山歌。号子、小调与花鼓调各有场合。桑植一带的歌调兼有汉族与土家族成分，不能只按一个民族归类。

## Time and place

Hunan's folk songs are spread across the central lowlands, the mountains of the west and the south of the province. Han songs mix with the traditions of the Tujia, Miao and Dong peoples. High mountains produced ringing mountain songs, the busy Xiang River produced call-and-response work songs, and towns and festivals favoured short tunes and flower-drum melodies.

## Musical features

Mountain songs often open in a high register and fall away at the phrase end, with many vocables. Boatmen's songs are short-breathed, the lead answered tightly by the crew. Huagu and tea-picking tunes sit closer to xiaodiao, in duple time with neat phrase lengths. Yu and zhi modes prevail, and fourth or fifth leaps are shaped by local speech tones.

## Repertoire

The Lishui boatmen's songs, Cold Water Makes Tea Steep Slowly, the Guzhang mountain song and the Wangcheng mountain song are common examples. Tea-picking tunes from flower-drum opera are also sung on their own.

## What to listen for

Hear how the first line climbs, then how the ending falls. In work songs, listen for the gap between the lead and the answering crew. Town tunes can be counted; note that vocables are not mere filler but often fix the phrase length. A mountain song often ends on a long sigh, as if the sentence were finished and yet held.

## Common misunderstandings

A common mistake is to hear all Hunan song as high mountain calls. Work songs, town tunes and flower-drum melodies belong to different settings. Songs around Sangzhi combine Han and Tujia elements and cannot be assigned to a single people.
