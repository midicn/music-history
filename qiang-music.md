## 年代与地方

羌族聚居在四川阿坝州的茂县、汶川、理县、黑水与绵阳北川，岷江上游的高山村寨是核心区。羌语属汉藏语系藏缅语族羌语支，分南北两大方言，没有传统文字。瓦尔俄足在农历五月初五前后举行，是妇女的节庆；祭山、还愿与婚礼由释比主持。2008年汶川地震后，许多村寨重建，音乐传承也随之调整。

## 它是怎么来的

羌族音乐活在仪式与劳动里。释比在祭山、还愿、治病时唱经，戴猴头帽，击羊皮鼓，边走边唱；多声部民歌在薅草、背粪、婚礼与节庆中唱，领唱与低声部叠合。瓦尔俄足期间，妇女上山采花、唱仪式歌，男子负责伴奏与后勤。二十世纪后期，羌笛与多声部民歌被采录，2006年羌笛演奏及制作技艺列入国家级非遗。具体仪式歌的完整唱本多由释比口传，公开记录有限。

## 声音的骨架

羌笛是双管双簧的竖吹乐器，两支管同时发声，音域约一个八度，用鼓腮换气法可一口气吹很久。它的音色清脆带悲凉，常吹《折柳词》《思念曲》《莎郎曲》。多声部民歌以高腔为主，领唱先起，低声部在一个音上持续，上方声部加花，句尾常一起下滑。酒歌响亮，劳动歌节奏短促，萨朗是围圈歌舞，脚步与歌声同步。

## 听什么

先听羌笛两支管同时发声时的微微不准，那是双簧叠韵的效果。再听多声部里低声部怎样稳住，领唱怎样在句尾换气。essen-009699 是四川山歌，essen-000496 是康定情歌，只作川西声腔参照；chinafolk-008875 与羌笛曲牌《折柳词》同题，可作联想，但不是羌笛录音。

## 常被误会的地方

常见误解是把羌笛当成汉族的笛子。它是双管双簧、竖吹、音域只有一个八度，吹法与曲目都不同。也不是所有羌族歌都是多声部，山歌、酒歌、劳动歌各有唱法。羌族音乐不是古羌遗音的标本，它在重建后的村寨与节庆里继续被唱。

## When and where

The Qiang live in Mao County, Wenchuan, Lixian and Heishui in Aba Prefecture, and in Beichuan in Mianyang, Sichuan, with highland villages along the upper Min River as the core. Qiang belongs to the Qiangic branch of Sino-Tibetan and has two major dialect groups but no traditional script. Wal'ezu falls around the fifth day of the fifth lunar month and is a women's festival; mountain sacrifices, vow fulfilments and weddings are led by ritual specialists called shibi. After the 2008 Wenchuan earthquake many villages were rebuilt, and transmission adjusted with them.

## How it came about

Qiang music lives in ritual and work. Shibi chant at mountain sacrifices, vow rites and healing, wearing a monkey-head cap and beating a sheepskin drum while walking. Polyphonic songs are sung at weeding, carrying manure, weddings and festivals, with a lead voice over a low part. During Wal'ezu women gather flowers on the mountain and sing ritual songs while men handle accompaniment and logistics. From the late twentieth century the qiangdi and polyphonic songs were recorded, and qiangdi playing and making entered the national intangible heritage list in 2006. Full ritual texts are mostly oral among shibi, and published records are limited.

## The sound's frame

The qiangdi is a vertical double pipe with double reeds; both pipes sound together, the range is about one octave, and circular breathing sustains a phrase for a long time. Its bright, mournful tone carries tunes such as Zhe Liu Ci, Si Nian Qu and Sha Lang Qu. Polyphonic songs are mostly high-register: the lead starts, a low voice holds one note, upper voices ornament, and phrase ends often fall together. Drinking songs are loud, work songs are short and rhythmic, and Salang is circle dance with steps locked to the song.

## What to listen for

Hear the slight mistuning of the two pipes sounding together. Then listen for how the low part steadies the texture and where the lead takes breath at phrase ends. Essen-009699 is a Sichuan mountain song and essen-000496 is Kangding Love Song, regional references only; chinafolk-008875 shares a title with the qiangdi piece Zhe Liu Ci and is an association, not a qiangdi recording.

## What is often mistaken

The qiangdi is often mistaken for the Han dizi. It is a vertical double pipe with double reeds, one octave in range. Nor are all Qiang songs polyphonic; mountain songs, drinking songs and work songs differ. Qiang music is not a fossil of ancient Qiang culture; it is still sung in rebuilt villages and festivals.
