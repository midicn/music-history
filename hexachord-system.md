::: zh
六个音节没有改变任何一个音高，却让歌手在没有乐器的时候也能找到音。圭多把这套工具写进一本小册子，欧洲的唱名法从此有了一把共同的尺子。

## 时间与地点

十一世纪二十年代末，意大利。圭多先在蓬波萨修道院教唱，后到阿雷佐主持教堂学校，《Micrologus》约写于1025至1030年间。确切年份没有定论，学界通常只说十一世纪。

## 发生了什么

圭多从赞美诗《Ut queant laxis》每句的起音取字，得到 ut、re、mi、fa、sol、la 六个音节。六个音构成一个六声音阶，半音固定在 mi 与 fa 之间。体系有三个位置，分别落在 G、C、F 上，叫硬六声、自然六声与软六声。唱到框架边缘时，歌手要在六声之间换位，把同一个音改叫另一个名字。全部可用音从最低的 Γ 到高音 la，合称 gamut。唱名不指定绝对音高，只标出相对位置，所以同一段旋律能在不同高度用同一套音节唱出。这套办法一直用到十七世纪，才被七声音阶的唱名取代。

## 改变了什么

读谱不再必须依赖乐器。教堂学校可以用统一口令教唱，四线谱上的音高有了能念出来的名字。换位训练逼着歌手理解音程，而不只是背旋律。限制同样明显，六声框架里 fa 与 mi 之间的三全音必须避开，遇到 B 还要判断用硬六声还是软六声，这个判断后来长成 musica ficta。可动唱名的观念留了下来，十九世纪的可动 do 与柯达伊教学法都能追到这一步。

## 听这个转折

先听《Ut queant laxis》的管风琴曲，六句旋律的起音一级一级往上走，唱名就是从这条线上取下的。再听《Pange lingua》的赞美诗，旋律以级进为主，换位不多，是这套体系的舒适区。最后听希尔德加德的《O Virtus Sapientiae》，音域超过六度，歌手必须在六声之间来回换位，才能把高音与低音装进同一套唱名。三首都在体系之内，差别在于换位的次数。

::: en
Six syllables changed no pitch, yet they let a singer find notes without an instrument. Guido put the tool in a short treatise, and European solmization acquired a shared ruler.

## Time and place

Italy, late 1020s. Guido taught singing at the monastery of Pomposa and later directed the cathedral school at Arezzo. His Micrologus was written around 1025 to 1030; the exact year is disputed, so scholars usually say only the eleventh century.

## What happened

Guido took the opening note of each line of the hymn Ut queant laxis and drew six syllables from them, ut, re, mi, fa, sol, la. The six notes form a hexachord with the semitone fixed between mi and fa. The system sat in three positions, on G, C and F, called the hard, natural and soft hexachords. At the edges the singer had to mutate, renaming the same pitch in order to move into the next hexachord. The whole available range, from the low Gamma to the high la, was the gamut. Solmization named relative position rather than absolute pitch, so one melody could be sung on the same syllables at different heights. The system lasted into the seventeenth century, when seven-note solmization replaced it.

## What changed

Reading music no longer required an instrument. Cathedral schools could teach singing with a common vocabulary, and pitches on the four-line staff had names that could be spoken. Mutation forced singers to think in intervals rather than memorised tunes. The limits were real, since the tritone between fa and mi had to be avoided, and a B had to be assigned to the hard or the soft hexachord, a decision that grew into musica ficta. The idea of movable solmization survived, and both movable do and the Kodaly method trace back to this step.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with the organ verset on Ut queant laxis, where each phrase starts one step higher, the line from which the syllables were taken. Then the hymn Pange lingua, which moves mostly by step and needs little mutation, the comfortable range of the system. Finally Hildegard's O Virtus Sapientiae, whose range exceeds a sixth, so a singer must mutate back and forth to bring high and low into one set of syllables. All three sit inside the system; what differs is how often the singer changes hexachord.
