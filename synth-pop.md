::: zh
合成器流行让合成器包办低音与和声，鼓机定拍。

## 起源与年代
1970 年代末到 1980 年代初，英德乐队用降价后的复音合成器与鼓机做流行歌。Human League、Depeche Mode、Eurythmics 与 Pet Shop Boys 把它送进排行榜。

## 听辨特征
先听低音，常由合成器序列音型担任，颗粒清楚、重复不变。鼓机给出稳定的四拍或半速拍，军鼓是电子音色。和声多用三和弦与挂留，铺底长音让空间变冷。人声平直、少颤音，副歌靠叠录加厚。

## 代表人物
Human League 用极简合成器与男女对唱，Depeche Mode 把工业质感带进流行，Eurythmics 把灵魂唱腔放进合成器框架，Pet Shop Boys 用叙事与反讽写舞曲。

## 谱系
上承电子摇滚、迪斯科、Kraftwerk 与后朋克场景，下接电子舞曲、合成器浪潮与 2000 年代合成器流行复兴；它把录音室音色变成歌曲的主角。

## 常见误解
合成器流行不等于没有人味，人声与旋律仍是核心。也不等于固定音色。把它当 1980 年代怀旧，会漏掉它对当代流行制作的持续影响。

::: en
Synth-pop lets synthesizers carry bass and harmony while a drum machine keeps time and the voice stays cool.

## Origins and era
In the late 1970s and early 1980s, British and German groups used cheaper polyphonic synthesizers and drum machines to make pop songs. The Human League, Depeche Mode, Eurythmics and Pet Shop Boys took the sound to the charts, and MTV spread image and synthesizer together.

## What you hear
Start with the bass, often a synthesizer sequence with a clear grain that repeats unchanged. A drum machine sets a steady four-to-the-floor or half-time pulse, its snare electronic. Harmony stays close to triads and suspensions, while sustained pads cool the space. Voices are flat and light on vibrato, thickened by overdubs in the chorus. Verse and chorus remain, but the break is left for colour changes.

## Key figures
The Human League paired minimal synths with male-female duets, Depeche Mode brought industrial texture to pop, Eurythmics placed soul singing inside synth frames, and Pet Shop Boys wrote dance songs with narrative and irony. Kraftwerk supplied the earlier template.

## Lineage
Electronic rock, disco and the post-punk independent scene feed in; electronic dance music, synthwave and the 2000s synth-pop revival come out. Studio timbre became the lead instrument.

## Common misunderstandings
Synth-pop is not inhuman, since voice and melody remain central. Nor is it one fixed tone, running from cold to warm. Treating it as 1980s nostalgia misses its hold on current pop production.
