::: zh
十一世纪的修士在抄写圣咏时，没有精确音高记号。圭多·达雷佐把线、间与六声音节拼成一套教学工具，后世又把它们画到一只左手上。

## 时间与地点

约1025至1030年，意大利阿雷佐。圭多在蓬波萨修道院写下《微逻辑》，随后到阿雷佐主教座堂教唱诗班。

## 发生了什么

他把圣咏《Ut queant laxis》每句的开头音取作ut、re、mi、fa、sol、la，让歌手用一组固定音节记住半音与全音的位置。抄本用红线标F、黄线标C，线间距离表示音高，四线谱由此成为读谱工具。圭多手把二十个音级分配到左手关节，歌手指着手就能找到六声音阶的起点。它未必出自圭多，却把他的教学法变成可携带的记忆术。

## 改变了什么

音高不再只靠口传和记忆，而是可以写下来、寄出去、照着唱。唱诗班能在短时间内学会新圣咏，作曲家也能把多个声部对齐。线谱后来加到五条，纽姆符变成方形音符，但线与间表示音高的原则一直用到今天。记谱从辅助记忆变成音乐的存在方式。

## 听这个转折

先听希尔德加德的单声圣咏，旋律在一个八度内起伏，像一条没有伴奏的线。再听佩罗坦的四声部奥尔加农，长音托住上方花唱，几个声部必须靠谱面才能协调。库中版本是合成音色，听音高关系即可。

::: en
An eleventh-century monk copying chant had no precise way to write pitch. Guido of Arezzo turned lines, spaces and a set of syllables into a teaching tool; later diagrams placed the whole system on the left hand.

## Time and place

Around 1025–1030 in Arezzo, Italy. Guido wrote the Micrologus at Pomposa and then taught singers at the cathedral of Arezzo.

## What happened

He took the opening syllables of the hymn Ut queant laxis for ut, re, mi, fa, sol, la, giving singers fixed syllables for semitone and whole-tone steps. Red lines marked F and yellow lines marked C, and the distance between lines showed pitch. The four-line staff became a reading tool. The Guidonian hand mapped twenty pitches onto the joints of the left hand, so a singer could find a hexachord's starting point by pointing. The diagram may not be Guido's own, but it made his method portable.

## What changed

Pitch could be written down, sent away and sung from the page instead of being held in memory. Choirs learned new chant quickly, and composers could coordinate several voices. The staff later gained a fifth line and neumes became square notes, but the principle that lines and spaces carry pitch is still with us. Notation stopped being a memory aid and became the way music exists.

## Listen for the turn

Begin with Hildegard's monophonic chant, a single line moving within an octave. Then hear Perotin's four-voice organum, where sustained notes support florid upper voices and the parts need a written page to coordinate. The versions are synthesized; listen to pitch relationships.
