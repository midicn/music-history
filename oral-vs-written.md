## 问题

口传音乐活在记忆、身体与社群中，允许装饰与即兴，也依赖师承。书写能固定旋律骨架，却常把装饰、音色与速度留在纸外。记谱因此是一次选择。

## 历史脉络

格里高利圣咏最早靠口传，后来才写进带纽姆符的唱经本。书写固定了旋律骨架，装饰与地方变体仍留在歌者口中。十九世纪，采集者带着笔记本和蜡筒下乡，把口传旋律变成谱面与录音。巴托克与格兰杰把农民曲调记谱改写。二十世纪，爵士靠唱片与口头传授并行，爱尔兰传统音乐靠 ABC 谱与录音继续流动。

## 关键转折

第一次转折是采集，把口传音乐写成固定版本，变体被压成标准曲。第二次转折是录音，蜡筒与磁带保存了音色、装饰与方言，记谱漏掉的细节第一次可被听见。第三次转折是回流，录音与网络让口传传统重获权威，学习者可以跟着师承录音学。

## 听什么

先听爱尔兰《Cooley's》里同一段旋律的重复与装饰，注意重复并不相同；再听巴托克《罗马尼亚民间舞曲》，农民旋律被写进拍号与和声；最后听《Amazing Grace》的多种版本，同一首歌在不同场合变形。库中多为合成音色，注意骨架与装饰的差别。

## The question

Oral music lives in memory, the body and a community. It allows ornament, dialect and improvisation, and depends on apprenticeship. Writing can fix a melodic frame, yet often leaves ornament, timbre and tempo outside the page. Every transcription is a choice.

## Historical arc

Gregorian chant was first carried by oral teaching and later written into chant books with neumes. Writing fixed the melodic frame while ornament stayed in singers' mouths. In the nineteenth century, collectors took notebooks and wax cylinders into the countryside, turning oral melody into scores and recordings. Bartók and Grainger saved peasant tunes and recast them as concert works. In the twentieth century, jazz ran on records and oral teaching at once, while Irish traditional music kept moving through ABC notation and recordings online.

## The turning point

The first turn was collecting. Notators turned oral music into fixed versions, pressing variants into standard tunes. The second turn was recording. Cylinders and tape preserved timbre, ornament and dialect, making audible what notation left out. The third turn was return flow. Recordings and networks gave oral traditions new authority, and learners could study directly with a lineage of recordings.

## What to listen for

Begin with the repeats and ornaments of the Irish reel Cooley's, and notice that no repetition is the same. Then hear Bartók's Romanian Folk Dances, where peasant melody is fitted to metre and harmony. Finally hear versions of Amazing Grace, the same song taking different shapes in different settings. The library versions are synthesised, so follow the frame and its ornaments.
