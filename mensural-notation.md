::: zh
十四世纪的作曲家开始用音符的形状记录时值，而不只是音高。长短、比例与分组被写进谱面，音乐第一次能像建筑一样被精确设计。

## 时间与地点

十三世纪末至十四世纪，巴黎与意大利。菲利普·德·维特里的《新艺术》约1320年成书，马肖在兰斯写下《圣母弥撒》，约1360至1365年。

## 发生了什么

有量记谱用长、短、半短、最小音符等形状表示相对时值，再用拍号与色音符规定三拍与二拍的分组。三拍长期被视为完美，二拍在新艺术中获得合法地位。音符可以连写、切分、倒影，节奏成为独立的结构层。马肖把常规弥撒的五个段落写成一套连贯的复调，各声部的比例被谱面锁定。

## 改变了什么

音乐从记下音高变成记下时间。作曲家能写作大型套曲，歌手能在异地还原同样的节奏，抄本成为可传播的作品。今天的全音符、二分音符、小节线与拍号，都能追溯到这套体系。记谱从此不只是保存声音，也塑造了声音可以怎样被构思。

## 听这个转折

马肖的《圣母弥撒》听四个声部如何在同一时间网格里错开；佛罗伦萨的《荣耀经》听三拍与二拍的交替；再听马肖的尚松，世俗曲同样靠音符形状固定节奏。库中版本是合成音色，注意各声部的长短比例。

::: en
Fourteenth-century composers began to write duration with the shapes of notes, not only pitch. Length, proportion and grouping entered the page, and music could be designed as exactly as a building.

## Time and place

The late thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, in Paris and Italy. Philippe de Vitry's Ars Nova treatise dates from around 1320; Machaut wrote the Messe de Nostre Dame at Reims around 1360–1365.

## What happened

Mensural notation used longa, brevis, semibrevis and minima to show relative duration, while mensuration signs and coloured notes fixed the grouping of threes and twos. Triple time had long been called perfect; duple time became legitimate in the Ars Nova. Notes could be ligated, syncopated and inverted, and rhythm became an independent layer. Machaut set the five movements of the ordinary Mass as one continuous polyphonic structure, with the proportions of the voices locked on the page.

## What changed

Music moved from recording pitch to recording time. Composers could build large cycles, singers could reproduce the same rhythm far away, and a manuscript became a work that could travel. The whole note, half note, barline and time signature all descend from this system. Notation no longer merely preserved sound; it shaped what sound could be conceived to be.

## Listen for the turn

In Machaut's Mass, hear how four voices offset one another inside a single grid. In the Florentine Gloria, hear triple and duple groupings alternate. Then hear Machaut's chanson, where note shapes fix rhythm in a secular song as well. The library versions are synthesized; follow the proportions between the voices.
