::: zh
音乐不只是表演，它还要有人教、有人学、有人考。教育制度决定了哪些技术被保留，哪些被遗忘。

## 问题

谁有资格学音乐，教什么，怎么教，谁来发证书？答案随教会、宫廷、市场与国家的力量移动。教育既保存传统，也筛选人。

## 历史脉络

中世纪教堂学校教唱圣咏，圭多手是记忆工具。文艺复兴出现唱诗班学校，训练歌声与礼仪。十八世纪宫廷乐师仍靠师徒制，音乐是手艺。十八世纪末巴黎音乐学院建立，十九世纪莱比锡音乐学院把训练标准化，练习曲、视唱与考试曲目成为通行货币。二十世纪学校音乐课普及，达尔克罗兹、柯达伊、奥尔夫把身体、民歌与游戏带进课堂。录音、视频与网络又让自学重新兴起。

## 关键转折

最大转折是从师徒到机构。机构把音乐拆成可测的技能，也让出身不再是唯一门槛；但考试曲目会固化品味，教材市场会把一种技术写成唯一正路。

::: en
Music must be taught, learned and examined. Institutions decide which skills survive.

## The question

Who may study music, what is taught, and who grants the certificate? The answers move with church, court, market and state. Education preserves tradition and selects people.

## Historical arc

Medieval choir schools taught chant with the Guidonian hand. Renaissance choir schools trained voices and conduct. In the eighteenth century court musicians still learned by apprenticeship. The Paris Conservatoire was founded at the end of the century, and Leipzig standardised training in the nineteenth. Etudes, sight-singing and examination pieces became common currency. In the twentieth century Dalcroze, Kodály and Orff brought movement, folk song and play into schools. Recording and the internet have now revived self-teaching.

## The turning point

The shift was from apprenticeship to institution. Institutions broke music into measurable skills and made birth less decisive. Yet examinations freeze taste, and textbooks can present one technique as the only road.

## What to listen for

Hear a Czerny study's repetition, then how a Well-Tempered Clavier prelude hides technique. Mikrokosmos is a ladder; Anna Magdalena's notebook shows the home lesson.
