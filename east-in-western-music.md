::: zh
十八世纪欧洲宫廷穿土耳其服装，十九世纪作曲家开始听东方的音阶。

## 问题

东方在西方音乐里是真实的声音，还是被制造的形象？当作曲家借用五声、钟声与鼓点，他是在倾听，还是在布景？

## 历史脉络

莫扎特写土耳其进行曲，用的是欧洲想象的军乐。十九世纪殖民博览会与铁路把爪哇甘美兰、日本乐器带到巴黎。1889年世博会后，德彪西从甘美兰的分层音响里找到新和声；拉威尔、普契尼、里姆斯基-科萨科夫各取所需。普契尼《图兰朵》借中国旋律，却仍讲欧洲的东方故事。二十世纪，录音与移民让交流更双向。

## 关键转折

转折从装饰到结构。早期东方只是色彩与标题，后来音阶、音色与时间感进入作曲法本身；但东方仍常由西方定义，真正平等的对话要等到演奏者与作曲家跨文化流动。

## 听什么

先听《塔》里五声音阶的钟声，再听《天方夜谭》的独奏小提琴，最后听《图兰朵》的合唱，比较想象与借用。

Eighteenth-century courts dressed as Turks. In the nineteenth century composers began to listen to Eastern scales.

## The question

Was the East in Western music a real sound or a manufactured image? When a composer borrows pentatonic melody, bells and drums, is he listening or building scenery?

## Historical arc

Mozart's Turkish march used a European idea of military music. Colonial exhibitions brought Javanese gamelan and Japanese instruments to Paris. After the 1889 exhibition, Debussy found new harmony in the gamelan's layers; Ravel, Puccini and Rimsky-Korsakov each took what they needed. Puccini's Turandot borrowed Chinese melody but still told a European story of the East. Recording and migration later made exchange more two-way.

## The turning point

The shift was from decoration to structure. Early orientalism was colour and title; later, scale, timbre and time entered compositional method. Yet the East was still often defined by the West.

## What to listen for

Hear the pentatonic bells in Pagodes, the solo violin of Scheherazade, and the chorus of Turandot.
