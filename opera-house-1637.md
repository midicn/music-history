::: zh
1637年，威尼斯圣卡西亚诺剧院开门卖票。歌剧不再是宫廷婚礼的装饰，而成为城市里可以反复观看、必须回本的生意。

## 年代与城市

1637年，威尼斯。圣卡西亚诺剧院上演曼内利的《安德罗梅达》，观众买票入场。此前歌剧多在宫廷内部演出，观众是受邀的贵族。

## 当时发生了什么

威尼斯贵族家族把剧院当作投资，按包厢和座位售票。演出集中在狂欢节季，制作人请作曲家、歌手和布景师，用新作品吸引观众。歌剧要迎合公众口味，咏叹调变得更重要，歌手成为卖点。蒙特威尔第晚年的《尤利西斯的归来》和《波佩阿的加冕》就是为这种市场写的。

## 后来变了什么

歌剧从一次性庆典变成常设行业。剧院、经纪人、票房、明星歌手与季节性演出构成一套商业系统，这套系统随后传到那不勒斯、维也纳、伦敦和巴黎。作曲家必须考虑观众，也要考虑成本；歌剧的保留曲目观念开始形成。公共歌剧也把音乐消费从宫廷扩展到城市公众。

## 听这一刻

先听《波佩阿的加冕》的终曲《Pur ti miro》，听二重唱如何抓住观众；再听同一部歌剧的宣叙调，听它如何在有限预算里推进剧情；最后听《阿里安娜的哀歌》，听宫廷歌剧留下的独唱遗产。

::: en
In 1637 the Teatro San Cassiano in Venice opened its doors and sold tickets. Opera was no longer a decoration for a court wedding but a city business that had to fill the house and recover its costs.

## Year and city

Venice, 1637. The Teatro San Cassiano staged Manelli's Andromeda before a paying audience. Before this, opera was mostly a court entertainment for invited nobles.

## What happened then

Venetian noble families treated theatres as investments and sold boxes and seats. Productions clustered in the carnival season, and impresarios hired composers, singers and scene painters to attract a public. Opera had to please paying listeners, so arias grew more important and singers became the draw. Monteverdi's late Il ritorno d'Ulisse and L'incoronazione di Poppea were written for this market.

## What changed after

Opera turned from a one-off celebration into a permanent industry. Theatres, agents, box office, star singers and seasonal production formed a commercial system that later travelled to Naples, Vienna, London and Paris. Composers had to think about audience and cost, and the idea of an opera repertory began to form. Public opera also widened music consumption from the court to the urban public.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with the final duet Pur ti miro from L'incoronazione di Poppea and hear how it holds an audience; then hear recitative from the same opera and how it moves the plot on a limited budget; finally the Lamento d'Arianna, the solo heritage that court opera left behind.
