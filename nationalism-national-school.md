::: zh
十九世纪，音乐被要求证明一个民族存在。

## 问题

民间音调那么多，为什么某些被选为民族之声？谁有权代表民族？民族乐派既反抗帝国，也可能压平地方差异。

## 历史脉络

赫尔德收集民歌，把语言与歌谣当作民族灵魂。1848年革命前后，作曲家把舞曲、史诗与农民旋律写进音乐厅。肖邦用玛祖卡与波罗乃兹把流亡变成波兰的声音；格林卡与强力集团寻找俄罗斯旋律；德沃夏克、格里格、西贝柳斯各自把地方音调抬进管弦乐。巴托克与柯达伊则用录音机回到村庄。

## 关键转折

转折是从引用到建构。民族乐派不只发现民歌，还发明了适合舞台的民族风格；这帮助新兴国家获得文化席位，也把复杂的地方传统压成一个可识别的标签。

## 听什么

先听肖邦波罗乃兹的舞步与叹息，再听格里格挪威进行曲的节奏，最后听德沃夏克斯拉夫舞曲的配器光泽，比较民间素材如何被音乐厅放大。

::: en
In the nineteenth century, music was asked to prove that a nation existed.

## The question

Folk tunes were plentiful. Why were some chosen as the national voice, and who could represent the nation? National schools resisted empires, yet could flatten local differences.

## Historical arc

Herder collected folk songs and treated ballad and language as the soul of a people. Around 1848, composers brought dances, epics and peasant melodies into the concert hall. Chopin turned mazurka and polonaise into the sound of exiled Poland. Glinka and the Mighty Handful searched for Russian melody, while Dvořák, Grieg and Sibelius lifted local accents into orchestral music. Bartók and Kodály took a phonograph back to the villages.

## The turning point

The shift was from quotation to construction. National schools did not simply discover folk song; they invented a stage-ready national style. This won new states a cultural seat, but compressed local traditions into a label.

## What to listen for

Hear the steps and sighs of Chopin's polonaise, the rhythm of Grieg's Norwegian March, and the orchestral sheen of Dvořák's Slavonic Dances.
