## 问题

画面给出时间与地点，音乐给出意义。两个人对视，是和解还是决裂，往往由一段不说话的旋律决定。

## 历史脉络

一八九五年电影放映时，钢琴师或留声机在旁伴奏。一九〇八年圣-桑为《吉斯公爵被刺》写下原创配乐，那是已知最早的作曲家署名配乐之一。一九一〇年代影院靠曲目单与钢琴师即兴；一九二七年有声片出现，音乐被录进胶片。一九三〇年代好莱坞设音乐部门，斯坦纳为《金刚》写配乐，科恩戈尔德把晚期浪漫派语汇带进片厂。战后爵士与电子进入配乐，一九七七年《星球大战》让交响乐回到中心，一九八〇年代合成器流行，一九九〇年代后大型音响与预告片音乐成型。

## 关键转折

转折是音乐从伴奏变成叙事者。瓦格纳式的主导动机被用来标记人物，同步点让动作与音符咬合；到预告片时代，音乐又变成可识别的商标。

## 听什么

先听《骷髅之舞》，那是圣-桑为默片写的语汇；再听普罗科菲耶夫的《基日中尉》组曲，电影配乐被整理成音乐会作品；然后听《女武神》的进行，看它怎样在电影里变成直升机的声音；最后听《查拉图斯特拉如是说》的开头，几个音就够做开场。

## The question

The image gives time and place; music gives meaning. Whether a look between two people means reconciliation or rupture is often decided by a melody that says nothing.

## Historical arc

When films were shown in 1895, a pianist or a phonograph played alongside. In 1908 Saint-Saëns wrote an original score for The Assassination of the Duke of Guise, among the earliest film music credited to a known composer. In the 1910s cinemas relied on cue sheets and improvising pianists; sound arrived in 1927 and music was recorded onto film. Hollywood set up studio music departments in the 1930s, Steiner scored King Kong, and Korngold brought late Romantic orchestral writing into the studio. Jazz and electronics entered after the war, Star Wars put the symphony back at the centre in 1977, synthesizers spread in the 1980s, and the Zimmer-style sound and trailer music took shape in the 1990s.

## The turning point

Music shifted from accompaniment to narrator. Wagnerian leitmotif labelled characters, sync points locked notes to action, and in the trailer era music became a recognisable trademark.

## What to listen for

Hear Danse macabre, the idiom Saint-Saëns brought to the silent screen. Then Prokofiev's Lieutenant Kijé suite, film music remade for the concert hall. The Ride of the Walküre becomes helicopter sound in a film, and the opening of Zarathustra needs only a few notes to start a story.
