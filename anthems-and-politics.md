::: zh
国歌不是自然长成的，它是被选出来、被规定、被演唱的国家声音。

## 问题

一首歌怎样获得唯一性？谁决定歌词、速度与场合？国歌把个人情感收编为集体仪式，也留下争吵。

## 历史脉络

《天佑国王》的曲调在18世纪英国公共生活中传播，后来被其他国家借用。《马赛曲》创作于1792年，1795年被定为法国国歌，第三共和国于1879年重新确认其国歌地位。《星条旗》的歌词写于1814年，美国国会到1931年才正式采用它。创作、流行和法律确认可能相隔很久，不能把其中一个年份当作同一事件。

## 关键转折

转折在于演唱被制度化。学校、军队、广播与体育场把一首歌变成条件反射；版本之争、歌词删改与站立礼仪，让国歌始终是政治谈判的现场。

## 听什么

先听《马赛曲》开头的军号式跳进，再听《天佑国王》的平稳颂歌气质，最后听《国际歌》的合唱推进，比较三种集体语气。

::: en
An anthem is not a natural growth. It is chosen, regulated and sung for a state.

## The question

How does one song become the only song? Who decides the words, tempo and occasion? An anthem turns private feeling into ritual, and leaves quarrels behind.

## Historical arc

The tune of God Save the King circulated in eighteenth-century Britain and was borrowed by other states. La Marseillaise was written in 1792, declared the French national anthem in 1795, and reaffirmed by the Third Republic in 1879. The words of The Star-Spangled Banner date from 1814, but Congress formally adopted it in 1931. Composition, popular use and official adoption can occur decades apart.

## The turning point

The turn came when singing was institutionalised. Schools, armies, radio and stadiums turned a song into a reflex. Disputes over versions, edited verses and rules of standing keep the anthem political.

## What to listen for

Hear the bugle-like leaps of La Marseillaise, the hymn-like poise of God Save the King, and the choral drive of The Internationale.
