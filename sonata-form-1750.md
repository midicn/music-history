::: zh
呈示部摆出两个世界，发展部把它们拆开，再现部让第二个世界回到主调。奏鸣曲式让器乐音乐不靠文字，也能讲一场冲突与和解。

## 年代与城市

十八世纪中叶至十九世纪初，维也纳。海顿、莫扎特与贝多芬把二部性的段落结构推向呈示、发展、再现的戏剧模型；理论家后来把它写成规则。

## 当时发生了什么

奏鸣曲式以调性对立为核心。呈示部把主调与属调或关系调并置，发展部拆解动机、游历远调，再现部把第二主题拉回主调。它不是固定模板，而是一套处理对比与回归的程序。海顿建立段落感，莫扎特让主题在对话中变形，贝多芬扩大发展部与尾声。

## 后来变了什么

器乐音乐可以不靠文字、布景或人声制造冲突与解决。交响曲、四重奏、协奏曲和独奏奏鸣曲共享这套语法，它成为十九世纪音乐教育的核心，也带来忠实于形式与突破形式的长期争论。

::: en
The exposition sets up two worlds, the development pulls them apart, and the recapitulation brings the second world home to the tonic. Sonata form lets instrumental music stage conflict and reconciliation without words.

## Year and city

Vienna, from the middle of the eighteenth century to the early nineteenth. Haydn, Mozart and Beethoven turned a two-part sectional pattern into the dramatic model of exposition, development and recapitulation; theorists later wrote it down as a rule.

## What happened then

Sonata form turns on tonal opposition. The exposition sets the tonic against the dominant or a related key, the development breaks motives apart and travels through remote keys, and the recapitulation brings the second subject back to the tonic. It is not a fixed template but a procedure for handling contrast and return. Haydn built the sense of sections, Mozart made themes change inside a conversation, and Beethoven enlarged the development and the coda.

## What changed after

Instrumental music could make conflict and resolution without text, scenery or voices. Symphony, quartet, concerto and solo sonata share the grammar; it became the core of nineteenth-century musical education and produced a long argument between fidelity to form and the need to break it.

## Listen for this moment

Begin with the first movement of Haydn's Hob. XVI:52 and the tonal contrast between theme and second subject; then Mozart's K.157, where four instruments take the exposition apart; finally Beethoven's Waldstein, where the development travels far and the recapitulation returns.
