::: zh
民谣摇滚把民歌的叙事与和声装上电吉他、贝斯与鼓组，1965 年在洛杉矶与纽约同时出现。

## 从哪儿来
1965 年 1 月，Byrds 在洛杉矶录下“Mr. Tambourine Man”，四月发行，十二弦吉他的叮响与人声和声从此成为模板。同年 7 月，迪伦在新港民谣节带乐队上台，此前他已录下“Like a Rolling Stone”。洛杉矶的民谣俱乐部与英国入侵的节拍提供了土壤，1967 年英国的 Fairport Convention 又把传统民歌电气化。

## 耳朵先抓到什么
速度多为中速，100 到 130 BPM。鼓组是标准摇滚二四拍，贝斯跟着根音走，十二弦电吉他弹出密集的叮响，人声用二到三部和声，铃鼓加重反拍。旋律多取自民歌，常用分节歌，一段旋律反复配多段歌词，叙事推进靠词不靠和声变化。迪伦式的咬字把重音压在句尾，句子常比小节长。贝斯在根音与五度之间跳进，鼓手在副歌才加镲，主歌常只留铃鼓与吉他。编曲把民歌的句子拉长，让电声的余音填满句尾。和声多在句末进入，前几句留给主唱独自叙述。

## 绕不开的人
Byrds 的 McGuinn 用十二弦吉他定义音色，迪伦把歌词密度与电声编制带进来。Fairport Convention 把英国传统歌谣与摇滚节奏结合，Mamas and the Papas 把和声推向主流。Judy Collins 与 Tom Rush 一类的歌手则把民歌素材带进城市听众的耳朵。

## 它影响了谁
上承美国与英国民歌、伍迪·格思里一脉的叙事歌曲，以及英国入侵的节拍。下接乡村摇滚、创作歌手与 1990 年代的另类民谣。它也把“作者歌手”这一身份固定下来，让歌词第一次在摇滚里获得与音色同等的分量。它的听众后来分成两路，一路走向创作歌手，一路走向乡村摇滚与大型场馆。

## 常被说错的地方
常见说法是迪伦一人发明民谣摇滚，其实 Byrds 的单曲先发行，且编曲由乐队完成。另一个误解是新港事件是一场彻底决裂，迪伦此前已有电声尝试。把民谣摇滚等同于抗议歌曲，也会漏掉它大量爱情、风景与个人叙事的作品。

::: en
Folk rock put folk narrative and harmony onto electric guitar, bass and drums, appearing in Los Angeles and New York in 1965.

## Where it came from
In January 1965 the Byrds recorded Mr. Tambourine Man in Los Angeles and released it in April; the twelve-string chime and vocal harmony became the template. That July, Dylan brought a band onstage at the Newport Folk Festival, having already recorded Like a Rolling Stone. Folk clubs in Los Angeles and the beat of the British Invasion supplied the ground, and in 1967 Britain's Fairport Convention electrified traditional song.

## What the ear catches
Tempos are usually medium, between 100 and 130 BPM. The kit plays a standard rock two and four, bass follows the roots, a twelve-string electric guitar produces a dense chime, voices stack in two or three parts, and tambourine weights the backbeat. Melodies often come from folk song and use strophic form, one tune repeated under many verses, so the story moves through words rather than harmonic change. Dylan's delivery pushes accents to the end of lines that often run past the bar. Bass leaps from root to fifth, the drummer saves cymbals for the chorus, and verses often keep only tambourine and guitar. The arrangement stretches the folk phrase so electric resonance fills the end of each line. Harmony usually enters at the end of a verse, leaving the first lines to one voice.

## Names you cannot avoid
McGuinn of the Byrds defined the twelve-string tone, and Dylan brought lyrical density and an electric band. Fairport Convention joined British traditional song to rock rhythm, while the Mamas and the Papas took the harmony side toward mainstream pop. Singers such as Judy Collins and Tom Rush carried folk material to city audiences.

## What it influenced
American and British folk song and the narrative tradition around Woody Guthrie feed in, along with the British Invasion beat. Country rock, the singer-songwriter era and 1990s alternative folk come out. It also fixed the identity of the author-singer, giving lyrics a weight in rock equal to tone. Its audience later split, one path toward the singer-songwriter, another toward country rock and large halls.

## What is often misread
The common claim is that Dylan invented folk rock alone, though the Byrds' single came out first and the band made the arrangement. Another is that Newport was a clean break, when Dylan had already worked with electric sound. Equating folk rock with protest song also misses its many songs about love, landscape and private life.
