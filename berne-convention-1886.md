## 时间与地点

1886 年 9 月，瑞士伯尔尼。十个国家签署《保护文学和艺术作品伯尔尼公约》，音乐进入国际版权保护。

## 发生了什么

公约为外国作者提供国民待遇，签约国给外国作者与本国作者同样保护。作品完成即自动受保护，不必登记。音乐作品被明确列入保护对象，包括乐谱、歌词与公开表演。公约设定最低保护期，1908 年柏林修订改为作者终生加五十年。此后每二十年修订一次。

## 改变了什么

乐谱的跨境盗印受到约束，出版社可在多国同步发行同一版本，作曲家的收入扩展到海外。歌剧与轻歌剧的跨国巡演需要授权，版权代理与集体管理组织因此壮大。美国长期不加入，直到 1989 年才实施公约，其间靠双边协议与国内法处理外国作品。流媒体时代的分版税，仍以国民待遇为底。

## 听这个转折

《蓝色多瑙河》是 1867 年的圆舞曲，出版后在欧洲与美洲迅速流通；《茶花女》的选段在各国剧院上演，乐谱与改编本同时发行；奥芬巴赫《地狱中的奥菲欧》在巴黎与维也纳轮演。三首都是公约生效前后流通最广的曲目，版权保护决定了谁能印、谁能演。

## Time and place

Berne, September 1886. Ten states signed the Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works, bringing music into international copyright.

## What happened

The convention gave foreign authors national treatment, so a signatory protected their works as its own. Protection came automatically, without registration. Musical works were named explicitly, covering scores, lyrics and public performance. The convention set a minimum term, revised in Berlin in 1908 to life plus fifty years.

## What changed

Piracy across borders was constrained, publishers could issue one edition in several countries, and a composer's income reached beyond the home market. Touring opera needed authorisation, and agents and collecting societies grew around that trade. The United States stayed outside until 1989, handling foreign works through bilateral deals and domestic law. Royalty splits in the streaming era still rest on national treatment.

## Listen to the turn

The Blue Danube, a waltz from 1867, circulated through Europe and the Americas soon after publication. Selections from La traviata played in many countries while scores and arrangements appeared at once, and Offenbach's Orpheus in the Underworld travelled between Paris and Vienna. Copyright decided who could print and perform them.
