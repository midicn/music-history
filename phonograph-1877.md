::: zh
1877年，爱迪生让一台机器说出玛丽有只小羊羔。这项发明的重要之处是能录下声音，并由机器重放。此前斯科特的声波描记器已经能记录声波痕迹，但当时不能直接播放。

## 事情发生在哪一年

1877年，美国新泽西州门洛帕克。爱迪生的留声机用锡箔圆筒记录振动；1887年柏林纳的唱盘机改用圆盘，1890年代唱片工业成形。

## 当时的经过

留声机把声波变成针尖的振动，再刻进锡箔或蜡筒。最初它被当作口述办公工具，音乐只是副产品。圆盘唱片可以复制、压印、装进纸套，价格降下来后，客厅、酒吧和游乐场都开始放音乐。苏萨的铜管进行曲、约普林的拉格泰姆、柏林的热门歌曲成为第一批大规模销售的声音商品。

## 影响与后果

听音乐不再需要乐手在场。录音制造了表演的复制品，也制造了明星、唱片公司、版税与排行榜。歌手开始为录音调整音量与音色，作品长度受唱片容量限制，流行曲的写法随之改变。录音还保存了演奏传统，让后来的研究者能听到十九世纪末的嗓音与乐队。

## 听一段转折

库中没有当年的唱片，只有这些曲目的 MIDI还原。先听《星条旗永不落》，再听《枫叶拉格》，最后听《亚历山大的拉格泰姆乐队》。这些作品帮助辨认当时流行音乐的风格；MIDI不能再现历史录音的频宽、噪声、演奏与录制条件。比较这些条件，需要查听博物馆或档案馆保存的实际录音。

::: en
In 1877 Edison made a machine speak Mary had a little lamb. Its crucial feature was the ability both to record sound and to play it back mechanically. Scott’s earlier phonautograph had already traced sound waves, but could not directly replay them at the time.

## The year it happened

Menlo Park, New Jersey, 1877. Edison's phonograph recorded vibrations on a tin-foil cylinder; Berliner's gramophone moved to discs in 1887, and the record industry took shape in the 1890s.

## What took place

The phonograph turned sound waves into the movement of a needle and cut them into foil or wax. It was first promoted as an office dictation machine, with music as a by-product. Discs could be copied, pressed and slipped into paper sleeves; once prices fell, parlours, bars and fairgrounds began to play music. Sousa's brass marches, Joplin's ragtime and Berlin's popular songs became the first mass-market sound goods.

## Effects and aftermath

Listening no longer required a musician in the room. Recording made a copy of a performance and also made stars, record companies, royalties and charts. Singers adjusted volume and tone for the machine, song length was limited by disc capacity, and popular songwriting changed with it. Recording also preserved performing traditions, so later researchers could hear the voices and bands of the late nineteenth century.

## Listen for the turn

The library has no historical discs, only MIDI realisations of these pieces. Begin with The Stars and Stripes Forever, then Maple Leaf Rag, then Alexander's Ragtime Band. These pieces help identify musical styles associated with early commercial recording. MIDI cannot reproduce the bandwidth, noise, performances or recording conditions of historical discs and cylinders. Those comparisons require actual archival recordings.
