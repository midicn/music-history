## 时间，与当时的地点

1955 年，加州雷德伍德城。Ampex 为录音机加装 Sel-Sync 同步放音头，同一台机器可以在回放已录声轨的同时录新声轨。

## 当时的场面

此前的叠录要把第一遍录满整盘带，再用两台机器对接复制，每次复制都损失音质。Sel-Sync 让录音头兼作放音头，新旧声轨保持同步。1957 年 Ampex 为莱斯·保罗造出八轨机，他把吉他、贝斯、鼓与人声分层录进同一盘带。1960 年代，四轨与八轨机进入英美录音室，编曲被拆成可叠加的层。

## 之后不一样了

录音从记录一场演奏变成建造一件作品。鼓可以单独录，人声可以叠成合唱，同一乐手可以包办整个编制。制作人、录音师与编曲者的权力上升，录音室成为乐器。流行音乐的编配不再受现场演出限制，叠录、补录与立体声混音成为标准工艺。古典录音也采用多轨，剪辑点从整段缩到小节。

## When, and where

Redwood City, California, 1955. Ampex added the Sel-Sync playback head, so one machine could play existing tracks while recording a new one.

## The scene at the time

Earlier overdubbing filled a whole reel and copied it through a second machine, losing quality each pass. Sel-Sync let the record head serve as a playback head and kept old and new tracks in step. In 1957 Ampex built an eight-track machine for Les Paul, who layered guitar, bass, drums and voices onto one reel. In the 1960s four- and eight-track machines entered major studios.

## What was different after

Recording shifted from capturing a performance to building a work. Drums could be recorded alone and voices stacked. Producers, engineers and arrangers gained power, and the studio became an instrument. Arrangements escaped the limits of live performance, while overdubbing and stereo mixing became standard craft.
