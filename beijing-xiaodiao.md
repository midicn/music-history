## 时间与地点

北京小调流传于内外城胡同、天桥、庙会与茶馆，唱的是北京官话，儿化音重，句尾常带卷舌。明清两代北京是都城，南方的时调、小曲随商路与官宦进来，与本地秧歌、儿歌、叫卖调混在一起。城里人听小调多在年节、庙会与堂会，郊区则与秧歌、夯歌相连。同一首曲调，城里唱得规整，城外唱得粗一些。

## 发生了什么

明清小曲在北京落脚后，一部分继续作为民歌传唱，一部分被说唱吸收。八角鼓票房的牌子曲用【剪靛花】【叠断桥】一类曲牌，与小调同源；单弦从这些曲牌里取材料，京韵大鼓则另走板腔一路。20世纪唱片与广播普及，小调从胡同进入电台，唱的人少了，听的人多了。1949年以后，采录者把胡同里还能唱的老人录下来，北京小调才有了可查的音响。

## 音乐特征

北京小调多为四句一段，二拍子，句幅方正，叙事性强。旋律以五声为骨，徵调式与宫调式常见，级进为主，句尾收得利落。垛句是特色，字多腔少，一连串短字挤在一拍里，像说话加快。儿化音改变韵脚，也改变句子的长短。衬词不多，但位置固定，常落在句尾的收腔上。

## 听什么

先听《孟姜女寻夫》，这是孟姜女调的长篇叙事，注意四句一段怎样把故事往前推。再听《叫五更》，一更一段，段落之间的换景就是时间的推移。接着听《十二月花名》，数板式的整齐最明显。最后听《绣荷包》，儿化音与句尾收腔都在细节里。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把北京小调当成京剧。京剧是板腔体戏曲，小调是民歌，两者在唱法、结构与场合上都不同。也有人以为北京小调都是胡同里的土产，其实不少曲调来自江南时调，只是在北京换了口音与词。小调还不等于儿歌，儿歌只是其中一类。

## Time and place

Beijing short songs belong to the lanes of the inner and outer city, to Tianqiao, temple fairs and teahouses. They are sung in Beijing Mandarin, heavy with erhua, the retroflex ending, and with phrases that curl at the close. As the imperial capital in the Ming and Qing, Beijing drew southern shidiao and short tunes along trade and official routes, and these mixed with local yangge, children's songs and street cries. Townspeople heard them at festivals, fairs and private gatherings; in the countryside they joined yangge and pounding songs. The same tune is neater inside the walls and rougher outside.

## What happened

Once southern tunes settled in Beijing, some continued as folk songs while others were absorbed into narrative singing. The banner-drum clubs used qupai such as Jian Dian Hua and Die Duan Qiao, which share roots with the short songs; single-string narrative drew on this material, while drum ballad took the banqiang road. With records and radio in the twentieth century, the songs moved from lanes to broadcasts: fewer sang them, more heard them. After 1949 collectors recorded the older singers still alive in the lanes, giving the repertory a documented sound.

## Musical features

Most items are four-phrase stanzas in duple time, square in shape and strongly narrative. Melody rests on a pentatonic frame, often zhi or gong mode, moving by step with clean endings. Patter phrases are characteristic: many syllables, little tune, a run of short words packed into one beat, like speech accelerating. Erhua changes the rhyme and the length of the line. Vocables are few but fixed in place, usually at the final cadence.

## What to listen for

Begin with Meng Jiangnu Seeking Her Husband, a long narrative on the Meng Jiangnu tune, and follow how each four-phrase stanza pushes the story forward. Then take Calling the Five Watches, one stanza per watch, where the change of scene marks the passage of time. The Twelve Months of Flowers shows the even tread of enumeration. Finally the embroidered-purse song keeps erhua and the closing curl in its details.

## Common misunderstandings

A common mistake is to confuse Beijing short songs with Peking opera. The opera is a banqiang drama; these are folk songs, different in delivery, structure and setting. Nor are they all home-grown in the lanes: many tunes came from Jiangnan shidiao and merely changed accent and words in Beijing. They are not simply children's songs either; that is only one category within the repertory.
