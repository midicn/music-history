创意曲是巴赫为键盘学生写的短小对位曲，一条动机走完全篇，长度通常只有一页。

## 起源与年代
1720 年代，巴赫在克滕为长子威廉·弗里德曼写键盘小曲，原题写作前奏与幻想曲，后来改称创意曲与辛弗尼亚。十五首二部与十五首三部分别按调性排列，从 C 大调走到 b 小调。此前意大利的里切尔卡与德国的幻想曲已有类似写法，巴赫把篇幅压到一页，把写法固定下来。此后创意曲成为对位教学的入门体裁，几乎每个学键盘的人都要经过它。

## 听辨特征
单乐章，一到两分钟，一条短动机贯穿全曲。二部创意曲只有两条线，主题在第一声部出现后立刻由第二声部在属调上模仿，中段用模进与转调展开，结尾回到主调。三声部创意曲多一条中声部，织体密度上升，和声更满，主题进入时常有休止留出空隙。听辨时先记住开头三四个音，再数它在整曲中出现几次，注意每次进入的调性与声部。巴赫在二部创意曲里通常让主题先完整出现一次，再由另一声部在属调上答句，中间的模进把调性带到近关系调，最后回主调收束，这套路线每一首都能复述出来。三声部版本还会把主题倒影或增值，让听者在熟悉的轮廓里听出变化。

## 代表人物
巴赫是这一体裁的中心，他的二部与三声部创意曲规定了长度与写法。二十世纪的教材仍以这三十首作为对位入门，作曲家偶尔用创意曲作标题向它致敬。

## 谱系
上承里切尔卡与幻想曲的模仿写法，下接赋格、三声部创意曲与二十世纪的键盘练习曲。

## 常见误解
创意曲不是自由即兴，每首都有严格的模仿与调性计划。它也不只是儿童练习，三声部创意曲的声部处理已经是赋格的预备。

::: en
An invention is a short contrapuntal piece Bach wrote for keyboard students, one motif carrying the whole thing, usually a single page long.

## Origins and era
In the 1720s, at Köthen, Bach wrote keyboard pieces for his eldest son Wilhelm Friedemann, first titled praeambula and fantasias and later renamed inventions and sinfonias. Fifteen two-part and fifteen three-part pieces are ordered by key, running from C major to B minor. Italian ricercars and German fantasias had used similar writing before, but Bach cut the length to a page and fixed the method. Ever since, the invention has been the entry point to contrapuntal teaching, and almost every keyboard player passes through it.

## What you hear
One movement of a minute or two, with a short motif threading the whole piece. A two-part invention has only two lines, the subject stated in the first voice then imitated at once by the second in the dominant, the middle developed by sequence and modulation, the close returning to the tonic. A three-part invention adds a middle voice, the texture thickens, the harmony fills out, and the subject often enters after a rest that opens a gap. Memorise the first three or four notes, count how often they return, and note the key and voice of each entry. In the two-part pieces the subject usually appears once in full, the other voice answers in the dominant, sequences carry the key to a neighbour, and the close returns to the tonic, a route that can be retold for every one of them. The three-part versions also invert or augment the subject, so a familiar outline keeps changing shape.

## Key figures
Bach is the centre of the genre, since his two- and three-part inventions set its length and method. Twentieth-century teaching still uses these thirty pieces as an introduction to counterpoint, and composers occasionally borrow the title as homage.

## Lineage
It grows from the imitative writing of the ricercar and fantasia and leads to the fugue, the three-part invention and twentieth-century keyboard studies.

## Common misunderstandings
An invention is not free improvisation, because every piece has a strict plan of imitation and key. Nor is it only child's practice, since the voice-leading of the three-part inventions already prepares for the fugue.
