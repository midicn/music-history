## 问题

音乐家靠什么生活？在古典时期之前，答案通常是**宫廷或教会**：乐师是仆役，作曲是职务的一部分。到了 19 世纪，答案变成**市场**：出版、音乐会、教学与版税。**赞助制度的转变，改变了音乐本身。**

## 历史脉络

- **中世纪—文艺复兴**：教堂与宫廷是主要雇主；音乐家的地位依附于职位。
- 巴洛克：宫廷乐队与教堂乐监仍是核心；巴赫、亨德尔都在这个体系里工作。
- 古典时期：海顿在埃斯特哈齐家族任职近三十年；莫扎特离开宫廷，尝试靠出版与演出生活；贝多芬则靠贵族赞助、出版与音乐会，成为独立艺术家的原型。
- 19 世纪：公共音乐会、音乐出版与音乐学院一起成长；作曲家可以靠作品与名声生活，但也更依赖市场口味。
- 20 世纪：唱片、广播、电影与版权收入加入；赞助以基金会、大学与政府项目的形式回来。

## 关键转折

从「职务」到「作品」：当音乐家不再依附某个宫廷，作品就必须自己站住——出版、演出与评论成为新的裁判。这既带来了自由，也带来了不稳定性。

## 听什么

同一体裁在不同制度下写法不同：宫廷舞曲要短、要能跳；音乐会作品可以长、可以复杂；出版用的钢琴曲要考虑家庭演奏者的技术。听音乐时，也听它背后的生活。

## The question

How did musicians live? Before the Classical era the answer was usually **court or church**: musicians were servants and composing was part of the job. By the nineteenth century the answer became **the market**: publishing, concerts, teaching and royalties. **The shift in patronage changed the music itself.**

## The outline

- **Medieval–Renaissance**: church and court were the main employers; a musician's status depended on a post.
- Baroque: court ensembles and church posts remained central; Bach and Handel both worked inside this system.
- Classical: Haydn served the Esterházy family for nearly three decades; Mozart left court service and tried publishing and performance; Beethoven lived from aristocratic support, publishing and concerts, becoming a prototype of the independent artist.
- Nineteenth century: public concerts, music publishing and conservatories grew together; composers could live by works and reputation, but depended more on public taste.
- Twentieth century: recording, radio, film and copyright added income; patronage returned through foundations, universities and state programmes.

## The turning point

From "post" to "work": once musicians were no longer attached to a court, the work had to stand on its own — publication, performance and criticism became the new judges. This brought freedom and instability at once.

## What to hear

The same genre is written differently under different systems: court dances must be short and danceable; concert works can be long and complex; published piano music must suit the technique of domestic players. When you hear the music, hear the life behind it.
