## 时间与地点

侗族大歌流传于贵州黎平、从江、榕江，广西三江与湖南通道的侗族村寨。歌队在鼓楼与歌堂演唱，节庆、迎客、祭萨与农闲时都唱。2009年列入联合国教科文组织人类非遗名录。

## 音乐特征

大歌是多人合唱，分高声部与低声部。高声部由一两位歌头领唱，音区高，旋律加花；低声部众人用持续长音托住，形成多声。没有指挥，也没有乐器伴奏，起音、换气与收束靠歌头示意。歌词用侗语，押韵讲究，声音歌模仿蝉鸣、流水与鸟叫。除大歌外还有小歌、耶歌与叙事歌。

## 代表曲目

《蝉歌》与礼俗歌、叙事歌常被提起，各地歌队的曲目并不相同。

## 听什么

先听低声部的长音，它像一条不断的水。再听高声部怎样在它上面进出，两个声部并不齐唱，而是错开。听声音歌里模仿蝉鸣的段落，注意歌头与众人之间的呼应。

## 常见误解

常见误解是把大歌理解成“大声唱”，或以为它是单声部民歌。大歌是侗语“嘎老”的译名，重在多声与歌队。它也不只唱情歌，礼俗、叙事与自然题材都入歌。

## Time and place

Dong grand song belongs to villages in Liping, Congjiang and Rongjiang in Guizhou, Sanjiang in Guangxi and Tongdao in Hunan. Choirs sing in the drum tower and the song hall at festivals, on greeting guests, at rituals for the ancestor goddess and in the slack season. UNESCO listed it in 2009.

## Musical features

A grand song is choral, divided into a high and a low part. One or two leads take the high line, high in range and freely ornamented, while the rest hold long notes below to make the polyphony. There is no conductor or instrument; the lead signals entries, breaths and closes. Words are in Dong with strict rhyme, and sound songs imitate cicadas, streams and birds. Smaller songs, ye dances and narrative songs exist alongside.

## Repertoire

Cicada Song and the various ceremonial and narrative songs are most often named, and each village choir keeps its own list.

## What to listen for

Begin with the long note in the lower part, like water that never stops. Then hear the upper line enter and leave above it; the parts do not sing in unison but overlap. In the sound songs, listen to the exchange between lead and chorus as the cicada is imitated.

## Common misunderstandings

Grand song is often read as simply loud singing, or taken for monophonic folk song. The name translates the Dong term ga lao and points to polyphony and the choir. Nor is it only love song; ritual, narrative and nature all enter the repertoire.
