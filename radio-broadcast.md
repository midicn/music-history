## 时间与地点

1920 年 11 月，匹兹堡 KDKA 电台播出选举结果，通常被看作商业广播的开端。1922 年英国广播公司成立，1926 年 NBC、1927 年 CBS 开播，收音机进入客厅。

## 发生了什么

广播把音乐变成按时刻表播出的公共流。电台需要填满时段，舞曲乐队、歌手、交响乐团和教堂合唱都被请进演播室；麦克风让轻声唱法成为可能，三分钟的歌曲正好填满一个节目段。1920 年代后期，美国形成全国广播网，赞助商买下节目，爵士与舞曲从城市传进乡村。托斯卡尼尼与 NBC 交响乐团的广播音乐会也吸引了数百万听众。

## 改变了什么

听音乐从买票进场变成打开开关，家庭成为新的听众席。电台把地方风格变成全国口味，歌手与乐队的名声不再靠巡演，而靠每晚的固定节目。广播也让版权与演出权变成产业问题，电台、唱片公司与出版商为播放许可反复谈判。指挥家则成为媒体明星。

## 听这个转折

图里纳的《Radio Madrid》把电台写进标题，把城市广播声当作音乐素材。格什温《The Man I Love》是 1924 年的流行歌曲，靠电台与舞厅同时传播，成为最早一批全国标准曲。《Swanee》是 1919 年的热门歌曲，电台把这类旋律送进千家万户。

## Time and place

In November 1920, KDKA in Pittsburgh broadcast election returns, usually taken as the start of commercial radio. Britain's BBC was founded in 1922, NBC followed in 1926, CBS in 1927, and the receiver moved into the living room.

## What happened

Radio turned music into a public stream on a timetable. Stations needed to fill hours, so dance bands, singers, orchestras and choirs were brought into studios; the microphone made quiet singing possible, and the three-minute song fitted a slot. In the late 1920s American networks went national, sponsors bought programmes, and jazz and dance music travelled from cities to farms. Toscanini's NBC Symphony broadcasts reached millions.

## What changed

Listening moved from buying a ticket to turning a switch, and the home became a new auditorium. Radio turned local styles into national taste, and a singer's fame depended on a nightly programme rather than touring. Broadcast made copyright and performance rights an industry issue, as stations, labels and publishers bargained over licences. Conductors became media stars.

## Listen to the turn

Turina's Radio Madrid puts the medium in its title, treating the city's broadcast sound as material. Gershwin's The Man I Love, a 1924 song, spread through radio and dance halls, becoming an early national standard. Swanee, his 1919 hit, shows the melody radio carried into thousands of homes.
