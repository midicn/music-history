## 核心问题

游戏音乐要为玩家的动作服务，又要在循环中不被厌烦。它如何在有限芯片、无限重复和互动选择之间，写出可听的秩序？

## 历史的走向

1970年代街机用单声道蜂鸣音标记碰撞与得分；1980年代家用机以三到四个声道写旋律，短循环必须十秒内抓住人。1990年代CD让游戏配乐变成管弦乐，动态分层让音乐随战斗变化。2000年代网络游戏用无缝循环覆盖长时间在线，2010年代独立游戏把芯片音色变成怀旧风格。

## 几个例子

《科罗布奇基》原是俄罗斯民歌，被《俄罗斯方块》改编成无穷循环的主题；8位元“Boss Battle 2”用快速琶音与噪声音色制造紧迫；《Theme Song 8 bit》以方波旋律示范家用机音源的窄音域写法。三例分别对应民歌改编、战斗与芯片音色。

## 听这几处

听《科罗布奇基》的旋律是否在循环末尾回到开头；听Boss战的低音是否不断重复；听8位旋律的跳进是否受音域限制。

## The core question

Game music serves the player's actions and must survive endless repetition. How does it build an audible order between limited chips, infinite loops and interactive choice?

## The direction of history

Arcade games of the 1970s used single-channel beeps to mark collisions and scores. Home consoles of the 1980s wrote melody in three or four channels, and a short loop had to catch the ear within ten seconds. CDs made orchestral scores possible in the 1990s, and dynamic layers let music change with battle. Online games of the 2000s used seamless loops across long sessions, and indie games of the 2010s turned chip timbres into a nostalgic style.

## A few examples

Korobeiniki began as a Russian folk song and became an endless theme in Tetris. The eight-bit Boss Battle 2 uses fast arpeggios and noise timbres for urgency. Theme Song 8 bit demonstrates the narrow range of home-console square-wave writing. Folk adaptation, battle and chip timbre, one case each.

## Listen to these

Hear whether Korobeiniki returns to its opening at the end of the loop. Hear whether the bass of the boss battle keeps repeating. Hear whether the leaps of the eight-bit melody are limited by its range.
