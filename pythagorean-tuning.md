## 问题

八度与五度若保持纯的比例，音阶里就有一个音无法安放，三度、六度也会偏离听觉习惯。键盘不能随时微调，调律必须决定把哪些音程调纯，把误差藏到哪里。

## 历史脉络

毕达哥拉斯学派用弦长比例定义八度、五度与四度。中世纪理论家借波爱修斯传下这套比例，用五度相生推出音阶。中世纪管风琴与复调偏爱纯五度，三度却不够纯，纯律与中音律由此出现。文艺复兴键盘需要自由转调，中音律牺牲远关系调。十八世纪，十二平均律均分八度，巴赫用二十四调展示良律。十九世纪末的音分与二十世纪的微分音，让调律重新成为选择。

## 关键转折

关键转折是狼五度。五度相生十二次，最后一个五度比纯五度窄一个毕达哥拉斯音差。中音律把它藏到少用的调上，平均律则把误差均摊到每个半音。平均律不是更纯，而是处处可用，让转调自由，却让三度偏离纯律。

## 听什么

先听圣咏，旋律停在调式音阶上；再听文艺复兴的复调，三度格外纯，那是纯律或中音律的痕迹；最后听巴赫《平均律》前奏曲，所有调在同一架键盘上成立。库中为合成音色，注意三度的甜与糙。

## The question

If octaves and fifths stay pure, one note has nowhere to sit, and thirds and sixths drift from what the ear expects. Once a keyboard is fixed, tuning must decide which intervals to keep pure and where to hide error.

## Historical arc

In the sixth century BCE, the Pythagoreans defined the octave, fifth and fourth by string length. Medieval theorists wrote these ratios into Boethius and derived scales by cycling fifths. Medieval organs loved pure fifths, but thirds were impure, so just intonation and meantone appeared. Renaissance keyboards needed free modulation; meantone sweetened the third but lost remote keys. In the eighteenth century, twelve equal semitones divided the octave, and Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier displayed a workable temperament across all twenty-four keys. In the late nineteenth century, cents turned deviations into numbers, and twentieth-century composers returned to just intonation and microtones.

## The turning point

The problem was the wolf fifth. Cycle twelve fifths and the last one is narrower than pure by a Pythagorean comma. Meantone hid the wolf in rarely used keys; equal temperament spread the error across every semitone. Equal temperament is not purer, only usable everywhere. It frees modulation while leaving every third slightly away from pure.

## What to listen for

Begin with a chant whose melody rests on modal steps. Then hear Renaissance polyphony, where thirds sound unusually pure, a trace of just intonation or meantone. Finally hear a prelude from Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier, where every key works on one keyboard. The library versions are synth sounds, so notice the sweetness or roughness of thirds.
