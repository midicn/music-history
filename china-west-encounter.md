## 问题

一八四〇年以后，教堂、军乐队、乐谱与学校一起进来，中国人要用别人的语法写自己的声音。难处不在学不会，而在学会之后，什么该留、什么该改。

## 历史脉络

明末利玛窦进献键盘乐器，宫廷第一次听见西洋调律。清末通商口岸出现教堂唱诗与海关乐队，一八七九年上海公共乐队成立，一九二二年由帕奇扩为管弦乐团。一九〇四年起，沈心工、曾志忞编学堂乐歌，用日本与欧美曲调填词，简谱随教科书铺开；李叔同的《送别》借的是一首美国歌。一九二七年国立音乐院在上海创办，作曲成为可教的职业。刘天华改二胡、办国乐改进社，黄自写《长恨歌》，马思聪写《思乡曲》，冼星海在延安写《黄河大合唱》。

## 关键转折

转折是从借曲填词走到用交响、合唱与艺术歌曲写中国题材。学院给了技术，战争给了听众，两股力量把中西之争变成路线之争。

## 听什么

先听《送别》所借的原曲，注意它的四句结构与和声；再听《茉莉花》，那是被西方作曲家取用的中国旋律；最后听《图兰朵》，听普契尼怎样把这首民歌放进异国故事。

## The question

After 1840, churches, brass bands, printed scores and schools arrived together. Chinese musicians had to write their own sounds with someone else's grammar. The difficulty was deciding what to keep and what to change.

## Historical arc

Matteo Ricci brought a keyboard instrument to the Ming court, where the ear met Western tuning. In the treaty ports, hymn singing and customs bands took root; the Shanghai Public Band, founded in 1879, became an orchestra under Mario Paci in 1922. From 1904 Shen Xingong and Zeng Zhimin fitted Chinese words to Japanese and Western tunes, and numbered notation spread with the textbooks. Li Shutong's Farewell borrowed an American song. The National Conservatory opened in Shanghai in 1927, making composition a teachable profession. Liu Tianhua rebuilt the erhu repertoire, Huang Zi wrote the cantata The Song of Everlasting Sorrow, Ma Sicong composed Nostalgia, and Xian Xinghai wrote the Yellow River Cantata in Yan'an.

## The turning point

The turn was from borrowed tunes with new words to symphonies, cantatas and art songs on Chinese subjects. The conservatory supplied technique, the war supplied an audience, and the argument over East and West became an argument over direction.

## What to listen for

Hear first the American song behind Farewell, its four phrases and its harmony. Then Molihua, the folk tune Western composers took up. Finally Turandot, where Puccini sets that tune inside an invented China.
